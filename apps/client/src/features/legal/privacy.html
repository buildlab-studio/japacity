Who we are

Japacity is a game run by {{operator.name}}, {{operator.place}} ("we"). We are the data controller: we decide what the game keeps about you and why. Write to {{operator.email}} about anything on this page.

The law this notice follows

This notice follows the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. We keep information about you to run the game you chose to play, to keep players safe, and to see how the game is used, and for nothing else.

In short

We keep what the game needs to run, to keep your life in it, and to keep players safe. We do not ask for your real name, a photo, a phone number or a password. We do not sell your information.

What we keep, why, and for how long

  • Your account: your username, so other players know you; that you passed the age screen, and when, never your date of birth; and the country your connection came from when the account was made, which counts play by country and picks the adverts shown there. It never changes how you can meet other players: that is the same wherever you are. Once you keep your life with Google or an email address, also the sign-in you chose: your email address, or your Google account's id and email, and a passkey's public key if you add one. With each sign-in session, while it lasts: your internet address and the kind of device and browser. Kept while your account exists.
  • Your game: your character, every move you make and saved copies of your life, so the life is kept and no move can be faked. Kept while your account exists. A guest life, one never kept with Google or an email address, that has not been played for 90 days may be removed.
  • Other players: invites you send or accept, your character's links with other players' characters, and which of the game's phrases you sent, to whom and when. Nobody can type to you, so no typed message is kept. Kept while both accounts exist.
  • A light on the dedication page: if you light one, the name and the years you type for it, and when you lit it. The light shows to everyone at once; the name and years you typed are kept with your account and shown to other players only once we have looked them over. Kept until you put the light out, light another in its place, we refuse its name, or your account is deleted.
  • Blocks, reports and decisions: blocks and reports you make, reports about you, and what we decided about them, in a log that is never changed. Kept for 3 years, to keep players safe and to answer appeals. What a report points to is held until the report is decided, for at most 90 days.
  • Usage counts: totals such as how many turns were played on a day in a country. No account is stored with them, so they name no one, and they are kept only as long as the game needs them. You can turn off the counts your device sends in the settings, with "Send usage counts".
  • Feedback you send from the menu: the text, the screen you sent it from, the game's version, your graphics setting, the day and the country. It is not linked to your account, and is kept for 180 days.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, the address your browser gives us for sending them, kept until your browser stops accepting them or your account is deleted.
  • On your device: a cookie that keeps you signed in, a cookie that remembers a refused age screen for 30 days, your settings, and the cards you made.

Your internet address is also used for a short time to limit how often requests can be made, to stop abuse.

Backups of the database are kept for up to 7 days; a deleted account is not brought back by a restore.

Who else handles it

  • Cloudflare hosts the game and its server, and its Turnstile check makes sure a new player is not a bot.
  • Neon hosts the game's database.
  • Google handles your sign-in if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • If you turn notifications on, the push service your browser uses carries them to you.

They handle your information only to run the game for us, and their servers may be outside Nigeria. We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Adults only

The game is for adults. If you are under 18, please do not play. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can:

  • ask for a copy of the information we keep about you;
  • ask us to correct it;
  • ask us to delete it, or delete your account yourself (below);
  • complain: to us first, and to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

For any of these, write to {{operator.email}}. We reply within 14 days.

Deleting your account

In the settings, choose "Delete my account". Your account stops at once and can no longer sign in, and your character leaves every place and every link with another player. What your account holds is then removed within 30 days. What stays:

  • totals that name no one;
  • the published ticket lists of past draws;
  • reports, what was decided about them, and anything an open report holds until it is decided;
  • a scrambled form of the account's id, so a second request can be answered, and of a banned account's sign-in, so it cannot return.

Changes

This notice can change. When it changes in a way that matters, we say so in the game.
