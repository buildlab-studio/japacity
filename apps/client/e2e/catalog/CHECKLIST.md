# What to look for in the screen catalog

One fault a line. Look at every picture against every line, the first three first.

1. A speech bubble's tail is joined to its own box, with no gap, no seam and no tail hanging off a corner, and it points at the one speaking.
2. No line of words over a face: bubbles, labels, chips and markers stand clear of every person's head.
3. People at one scale: the character, the family and the crowd stand at a grown person's height; nobody grown is drawn at a child's size, and nobody floats or sinks.
4. Text cut: a word clipped at a box's edge, a line running out of its box or off the screen, an ellipsis where the whole line should fit.
5. A chip, label, bubble or toast over a button, over another chip or over the band.
6. A dash in player text: an em dash or an en dash, or a hyphen standing in for one.
7. A bare key, a raw id, a kobo figure, "undefined", "NaN", "null" or a placeholder in braces on screen.
8. Player words that say slots, turn, banked, pages, days left, a day count or a percentage.
9. A button under the thumb buttons, the band, the phone's home bar or the screen's edge, cut off or hard to reach.
10. A sheet or card taller than the screen with no way to scroll to its button.
11. More than three callouts at once (the nudge chip, a word bubble, the marker, an open fan), or two on one thing.
12. Anything standing over a sheet, the phone or the map that belongs under it (a bubble or a label drawn above a dimmed screen).
13. The night paint: words the colour of their ground (navy on navy, white on white), or a surface left in the day's paint at night.
14. Money in the wrong currency for where the life is, a figure without its thousands marks, or a sum running out of its pill.
15. Small or pale words on a busy scene or a picture with nothing behind them.
16. Two pieces of text on top of each other, or an icon over a word.
17. Out of line: a row or column off its neighbours, uneven gaps in a list, one tile a different size from its siblings.
18. A picture missing: a blank box, a broken image, a grey square where a sticker belongs, a scene that did not draw.
19. A card's close button missing or off its card; a card hanging off the screen's edge.
20. A screen with nothing on it and no line saying why.
21. The same words twice on one screen, or two lines saying the same thing.
22. The character out of view, inside a wall or furniture, or standing where a person cannot stand.
23. A thing's label away from its thing, or a mark over the band or the thumb buttons.
24. A name or place cut short in the band.
25. Icons or stickers of different sizes in one row.
