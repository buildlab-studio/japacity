Privacy notice

Last changed 8 October 2026.

Who we are

Japacity is a game made and run by Al-Ameen Ogundiran, Lekki, Lagos, Nigeria ("we"). We decide how the information below is used. Write to hello@japacity.com about anything on this page.

In short

We keep what the game needs to run, to keep your life in it, and to keep players safe. We do not ask for your real name, a photo, a phone number or a password. We do not sell your information.

What we keep, and why

  • Your game: your character, every move you make and saved copies of your life, so it is kept and so we can check no move was faked.
  • Your username, so other players know you.
  • That you passed the age screen, and when. We do not keep your date of birth.
  • Your country: the country your connection came from when your account was made. It decides how you can meet other players.
  • Your sign-in, once you secure your account: your email address if you use a code by email; your Google account's id and email, and the sign-in details Google sends us, if you use Google; a passkey's public key if you add one. Before you secure it, your life is reached only through the browser you play in.
  • Your sign-in sessions: with each one, your internet address and the kind of device and browser. Your internet address is also used for a short time to limit how often requests can be made, to stop abuse.
  • Other players: invites you send or accept, your character's links with other players' characters, and which of the game's phrases you sent, to whom and when. Nobody can type to you, so no typed message is kept.
  • Blocks and reports you make, reports about you, and what was decided, to keep players safe. Phrases a report points to are held until it is decided.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, the address your browser gives us for sending them.
  • Feedback you send from the menu: the text, the screen you sent it from, the game's version, your graphics setting, the day and the country. It is not linked to your account.
  • Usage counts: totals such as how many turns were played on a day in a country. No account is stored with them. You can turn off the counts your device sends in the settings, with "Send usage counts".
  • On your device: a cookie that keeps you signed in, a cookie that remembers a refused age screen, your settings, and the cards you made.

We keep each of these only as long as the game needs it.

Who else handles it

The companies that host the game and its database, send sign-in emails and check that a visitor is not a bot handle information for us, only to run the game. If you sign in with Google, Google handles that sign-in.

Adults only

The game is for adults. If you are under 18, please do not play. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by writing to hello@japacity.com. Requests are answered.

Deleting your account

In the settings, choose "Delete my account". Your account stops at once and can no longer sign in, and your character leaves every place and every link with another player. What your account holds is then removed. What stays:

  • totals that name no one;
  • the published ticket lists of past draws;
  • copies of moves kept to check the game was played fairly;
  • reports, what was decided about them, and anything an open report holds until it is decided;
  • a scrambled form of the account's id, so a second request can be answered, and of a banned account's sign-in, so it cannot return.

Security

To report a security problem, write to security@japacity.com.

Complaints

This notice is written under the law of Nigeria. Tell us first, at hello@japacity.com. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. If you live in the United Kingdom or the European Union, you can complain to the data protection regulator where you live.

Changes

This notice can change. When it changes in a way that matters, we say so in the game.
