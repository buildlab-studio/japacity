# Rehearsal: the second stageshow cut

The three scenes of `StageshowBPortrait`, as the stageshow skill's verify.md asks a scene to be handed over: the gold it started from, what it holds, what was filmed, what the film said and what was not checked.

## Gold

- `phone-touch`: a phone app told as one continuous object, the touch cursor, a few beats to a payoff that holds. Kept: the kit's `Phone` drawn large (452 wide in the 540 by 960 frame, doubled to 1080 by 1920), the touch cursor that shows only where it taps, taps that open or close a surface in place. Changed: the app is the game on the water at Makoko, and each scene plays once into the next.
- `payout-flow` for the month's results: the savings bar fills on its own clock beside its figure rolling with `Count`, and the result holds.
- The road's move down the Japa app is the kit's `Scroll`, driven by `a.scroll`, the finger resting on the app while it moves.

## Beats

- `b-racks` (4.8 s): the act's card is up over Makoko from the first frame; Start work is pressed at 0.6 s; the sheet sinks, the scrim lifts, and the act's toast ("Look for a job", "Answer at the month's end") slides in under the band with the brief's chip under it.
- `b-payday` (6.6 s): the month's results lead with "Salary, the fish racks" and its figure, then what was kept; the money held for the move fills its bar; Back to Makoko is pressed and the sheet sinks; the brief's chip and Lanre's toast slide in and the month's dot turns green.
- `b-road` (6.4 s): the brief's chip is tapped and the phone rises on Japa at the brief it carries, the way out heard at a send-off first and ticked; the finger moves the app down to the ways out, four in the game's order, and they hold.

## Captured

- Widths: each scene at its shown width (1080) and at 390.
- Look: the day paint only.
- Driven: the composition steps each scene's virtual clock to the frame; the film's runs use the browser's own clocks.

## Gate

The last line of `film.mjs --gate` for each scene and width is in the report that hands this over.
