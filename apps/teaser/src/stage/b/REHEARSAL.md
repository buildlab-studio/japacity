# Rehearsal: the second stageshow cut

The three scenes of `StageshowBPortrait`, as the stageshow skill's verify.md asks a scene to be handed over: the gold it started from, what it holds, what was filmed, what the film said and what was not checked.

## Gold

- `phone-touch`: a phone app told as one continuous object, the touch cursor, a few beats to a payoff that holds. Kept: the kit's `Phone` drawn large (452 wide in the 540 by 960 frame, doubled to 1080 by 1920), the touch cursor that shows only where it taps, taps that open or close a surface in place. Changed: the app is the game on the water at Makoko, and each scene plays once into the next.
- `payout-flow` for the month's results: the savings bar fills on its own clock beside its figure rolling with `Count`, and the result holds.
- The road's move down the Japa app is the kit's `Scroll`, driven by `a.scroll`, the finger resting on the app while it moves.

## Beats

- `b-racks` (3.8 s): the act's card is up over Makoko from the first frame; Start work is pressed at 0.6 s; the sheet sinks, the scrim lifts, and the act's toast ("Look for a job", "Answer at the month's end") slides in under the band with the brief's chip under it.
- `b-payday` (6.3 s): the month's results rise over the water and lead with "Salary, the fish racks" and its figure, then what was kept; the money held for the move fills its bar once the sheet has landed; Back to Makoko is pressed and the sheet sinks; the brief's chip and Lanre's toast slide in and the month's dot turns green.
- `b-road` (6.4 s): the brief's chip is tapped and the phone rises on Japa at the brief it carries, the way out heard at a send-off first and ticked; the finger moves the app down to the ways out, four in the game's order, and they hold.

## Captured

- Look: the day paint only.
- Driven: the composition steps each scene's virtual clock to the frame; the film's runs use the browser's own clocks.

## Review

A fresh-eye review of the filmed and rendered frames, and what each finding became:

- The month's figures rolled under the fade from the racks, before the sheet had landed. The sheet rises over the water as the scene opens, and the money held for the move fills only once it has landed.
- Holds ran long: the racks' toast stood over three seconds. The racks hold about 2.8 s after the toast, the month's toast about 2 s; the brief in Japa is read for about 2.4 s and the ways out hold about 2.2 s.
- The world is a still: it is the game's own recording of Makoko by morning, as the first cut's family house is; a moving world is past what a still from the recording gives.
- The band under the status bar is the bridge over the lagoon, the top of the game's own view of Makoko, kept.
- The phone rises from the brief's chip, which the finger taps, as the game opens the Japa app from it.
- The second row of ways out runs off the handset's foot before the app moves down to it, as a list does; it lands whole.
- The brief's chip and the name pill keep the game's own padding, its round mark on the left.
- The Map and Phone thumbs and the band's sky draw the game's own pictures, as the game draws them.
- The scenes cut with the cut's fades, as the first cut's do.

## Not checked

- Reduced motion beyond the poster states each stage sets.
- Hover and keyboard: the scenes are filmed, not used.
