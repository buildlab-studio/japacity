# Home: where the character lives in Lagos

> Where the character lives at home: the family house, a room of your own, and a place on the lagoon the player can live in, cheaper than the family house, with the risk that the settlement is cleared. Nothing here moves money between lives (person:money-counted-never-moved).

## Why

The point of the game is saving to leave. A cheaper room makes the saving faster and the risk real: the settlement is cleared some months, and then the character needs somewhere to go. Eviction is part of life there and it is part of the game; bad news is told straight, never as a joke line (text.md).

## Rules

**home:home-slice-fields** The `home` slice holds where the character lives at home (and the `things` slice beside it holds what the life owns, home:things-a-life-owns): `place`, one of the four home places (`"place.family-house" | "place.makoko" | "place.compound-room" | "place.hostel-room"`), null after an eviction and while a guest; `since` (the month the character came to live there); `guest: { host: "friend" | "someone" | null; by: string | null; until: number } | null` (the kind of host, who it is, and the month the stay ends); `moved`, the month of the last move (home:move-home-once-a-month); `evicted`, the month of the last eviction (home:makoko-eviction-draw); and `hosting: { pair; until } | null`, a real player's character staying with this one (home:hosting-costs-the-host). A new character lives at the family house. Abroad the slice is kept as it was and is not read (the room abroad is abroad-money.md).

**home:move-home-once-a-month** The command `home/move` (`to`) is legal at home only, once a month, and never to the place the character lives in. Moving to Makoko needs `tuning.home.makokoUpkeep` in naira held (by its size, decision 0014, obligation:charges-paid-whole-or-missed): the first month's rent, charged on the move; from then the monthly charge is `obligation.upkeep-makoko` at that amount in place of `obligation.upkeep`, which closes. Moving back to the family house reopens `obligation.upkeep` at the scenario's charge (as place:family-takes-you-back does on a return) and costs `tuning.home.returnStanding` standing: the family talks. A guest (home:taken-in-after-eviction) moves the same way. Moving to the hostel room (decision 0018) (`place.hostel-room`) needs a month's rent, `tuning.home.hostelRentKobo` (₦15,000, a working figure, about half the compound room's year told by the month), held and takes it on the move; `obligation.upkeep-hostel` (type `rent`, monthly, grace 2) then charges the month to come at each month's end in place of `obligation.upkeep`, which closes; there is no year's lump, which is why the hostel is the cheap way out. Its default sends the character home as home:rent-default-returns-to-family. From the hostel the family house is as from the room.

**home:makoko-eviction-draw** Eviction. Each month lived in Makoko, in the `clocks` phase, a draw at `tuning.home.evictionBp` (luck:no-kicking-while-down halves it while the character is down). On it the settlement is cleared: `home.place` becomes `null` with `guest: null`, `obligation.upkeep-makoko` closes with nothing owed, the character loses the naira cash held up to `tuning.home.evictionLossNaira` (what went with the room), and the event `home/evicted` carries a line told straight. The month's results lead with it, News carries a story of it, and standing does not fall: it was not the character's doing. The same month `offer.taken-in` opens (home:taken-in-after-eviction), and no other `bad` offer opens that month.

**home:taken-in-after-eviction** Taken in. `offer.taken-in` has the answers `friend` (a real player this character is joined to whose facts show a home in Lagos, person:others-known-by-facts; shut with its reason when there is none), `someone` (a computer person of the story with a home: the first `person` entry of role `friend` or `sibling` whose `when` holds) and `family` (back to the family house at its full charge, with home:move-home-once-a-month's standing cost). `friend` and `someone` make the character a guest for `tuning.home.guestMonths` months with no upkeep charged; when `until` comes, the guest must choose again: `offer.where-next` opens with `makoko` (home:move-home-once-a-month's move, if the rent is held) and `family`. The offer left unanswered at the month's end takes `family`. Being taken in is a kindness recorded as a moment with that person (person:time-together-is-an-act where the person is a pair).

**home:hosting-costs-the-host** Hosting costs the host. While a real player's character is a guest, the host's own upkeep charge rises by `tuning.home.hostBp` for those months, told as "{guest} stays with you: upkeep up {amount}". The host's state learns of the guest only by an authority command (`home/host`, person:lives-meet-by-authority-command) and no money moves between the lives (person:money-counted-never-moved). A computer host is not simulated and costs nothing.

**home:home-is-the-home-point** The home is the home point (world:opens-where-last-stopped): the game opens where the character lives. `place.makoko` is a place of the city (city.md, district Makoko on the lagoon beside the bridge) whose room is the character's home while they live there; it stages the fish trade's ladder (`activity.job-fish-racks`, the kiln's `activity.buy-kiln` and the fish trade's `activity.start-fish-trade`, activity:capital-opens-own-work) and its side job, the canoe deliveries (`activity.canoe-deliveries`, for a life living there), and its things carry the same acts as the parlour's for the home's own entries. obligation:cooking-and-fetching-water's relief applies to whichever upkeep is open. The month lives in the phone's Calendar app (world:top-band-is-a-pill); the bed's Sleep, the papers box, cooking and the water drum stand at home.

**home:view-carries-home** The view carries `home: { place, since, guest, rent: { currency, amount } | null, evictionBp, moved, moves, roomRent }` (`moves` as home:moves-carry-cost-and-reason gives them, `roomRent` as home:compound-room-is-a-home) and the phone's home card shows where the character lives, the monthly charge, the choice to move (shut with its reason when the rent is short or the move was made this month), and while a guest, who took them in and for how long. Words come from content; the card says the risk once, plainly: "Some months the settlement is cleared."

**home:moves-carry-cost-and-reason** The home view carries the card's moves. At home, each home but the one lived in is a move with `monthly`, what living there charges a month; `now`, what the move takes at once (Makoko's first month's rent, nothing for the family house); and `shut`, why `home/move` would refuse it now: `moved` (a move was made this month) or `short` (the first month's rent is not held), null when it would be taken. The command and the view read one refusal. Abroad there are no moves. A charge a month, in `monthly` and in `rent` (home:view-carries-home), is the obligation kind's scheduled charge before any relief: a naira upkeep rises with `var.inflation` (director:december-and-inflation), so the card and the bill under it show the same figure. The phone's card reads the view and keeps no copy of the rule. The home says what it is: the home card in Bills opens with the place's blurb (`place.<id>.blurb`, "One room in a compound of ten..."), and the first entry into a home in a life shows that blurb once as a small card the player dismisses, kept beside the save as a place's first names are (world:things-are-tappable-models), never again. The hostel is a move with `monthly` and `now` both the month's rent, shut `short` while it is not held; the home card shows it, and the map shows the hostel only as home, while lived in.

**home:starting-charges-from-one-function** One function gives the starting life's charges at home: a fixed life's starting obligations, or for a life from sixteen, which starts owing nothing, what its home card's family-house answer opens (prologue:closing-opens-home-charges, prologue:makoko-start-never-a-trap). The family house reopens its upkeep from it on a move back (home:move-home-once-a-month), on a return from abroad or from the road (place:family-takes-you-back, road:exit-is-assisted-return), and the family's support abroad opens from it at the remit share (place:departure-moves-abroad, abroad-money:arrival-opens-upkeep-and-room): for a life from sixteen, `obligation.home-support` at `tuning.prologue.homeSupport` (₦40,000 a month before the share, between the fixed lives' ₦25,000 and ₦60,000), since the home card's effects open at home where no support is sent. So a life from sixteen that comes home pays the family house's upkeep the card names from the next month, as any life does. The home card names no support, so a life from sixteen sends none abroad.

**home:compound-room-is-a-home** `place.compound-room` is a home place: one rented room in a shared compound in Ojuelegba (city.md has its row as neutral scenery under city:sponsor-shows-name-only). The room is the character's home while they live there, seen down its length as world:room-seen-down-its-length, with the bed, the cooking corner, the water drum at the compound's tap, the papers box and the bag by the door carrying the home's acts (home:home-is-the-home-point). Rent is paid the Lagos way: a year upfront, `tuning.home.compound.rentYear` (₦350,000, so a life from sixteen with ₦500,000 saved can take the room at the age it starts at, prologue:front-door-three-screens; decision 0018), plus the agent's and the agreement's fees at `tuning.home.compound.feesBp` (2,000: a tenth each, the Lagos cap), taken at once as `money/take` with `pocket: "any"` under the reason `rent`. `obligation.rent-compound` (type `rent`, `everyMonths` 12, `prepaid`, obligation:prepaid-entries-skip-first-charge) then charges the year's rent again twelve months on; `obligation.upkeep-compound` (type `upkeep`, `tuning.home.compound.upkeep`, ₦50,000 a month: food, light, the waste and the compound's small charges on your own; no water share, since the drum is filled free from the yard's tap or well and the room has no water bill, obligation:cooking-and-fetching-water) replaces `obligation.upkeep`, which closes. The move in play goes through `home/move` as Makoko's does (home:move-home-once-a-month): at home, once a month, the rent and fees held, else shut `short`; the home card shows it with its monthly cost, what it takes now and why it is shut (home:moves-carry-cost-and-reason). Moving back to the family house is as from Makoko: the upkeep reopens, `tuning.home.returnStanding` is paid, the rent obligation closes and the year paid is gone. On a flight the room is let go: place:departure-moves-abroad closes `rent` with `upkeep` and `support`; the return goes to the family house (place:family-takes-you-back). The home view carries the room's rent while it is open, `roomRent: { year, fees, dueIn }` (the year's figure, the fees paid on it at the move, and the months until the next year is taken, 0 at this month's end), null where none is open; the home card says "Rent ₦350,000 a year, next in N months" with the living costs on their own line; Coming up carries the rent from two months ahead; the Month sheet (the Calendar app, home:home-is-the-home-point) says in one line when the money held is short of a rent due this month; the move back says the year paid is gone ("The year's rent stays with the landlord") and that the family house's charge is living costs, food and light included, not rent. The room is one wide house (decision 0018): your room of about 5 by 5 metres with a bathroom, a kitchenette and the compound yard with your spot by the gate, about 9 by 8 metres in all, seen whole as world:room-seen-down-its-length; its things stand within 4.2 metres of each other, and the figure reads above 5% of the screen's height whole and above 7% eased in. Meals there are your own, `activity.cook-own` and `activity.cook-own-pot` (home:living-by-hand).

**home:rent-default-returns-to-family** A defaulted `rent` sends the character back to the family house in the `clocks` phase, as an eviction from Makoko does (home:makoko-eviction-draw): the upkeep reopens, the standing cost is paid, a line with attention says it. The room's rent defaults on the third miss in a row (grace 2, as a room abroad), and its default closes it (the landlord takes the room back), so a room lived in with no rent open is the room lost; the default's own line says the landlord wants the room back, since a Lagos landlord keeps no credit file. `obligation.upkeep-hostel` is a rent too; its default (grace 2) closes it and sends the character home the same way.

**home:living-by-hand** Living by hand (decision 0013). At home the living is lived, not charged: meals are bought and cooked as acts that cost at once (`reason: "food"`), water is fetched, fares are paid at the stop, data from the phone. `tuning.home.livingByHandBp` (10,000: the whole of it) is the share of a naira `upkeep` that `livingCharge` leaves out as the month turns, so `obligation.upkeep`, `obligation.upkeep-compound` and `obligation.upkeep-makoko` charge nothing at home while it stands at the whole (Makoko's room keeps its first month paid on the move, and the child of the water keeps equal or better odds); the scenarios keep their monthly figures as the measure of a month's living, and the room's acts are priced so a month of eating and moving costs about that. Upkeep abroad is in the country's money and is untouched. The relief of obligation:cooking-and-fetching-water applies to whatever is charged. Nobody pays to cook in a house with family (decision 0018): at the family house `activity.cook-at-home` is free, Food from the pot, Mama's pot being the house's, and `activity.cook-jollof` is A big pot for the house, ₦7,500 under the reason `foodstuff` (food bought for everyone, with Mama's thanks over her head). In a home of your own in Lagos (the compound room, the hostel and Makoko's room) the meal is `activity.cook-own` (₦1,500) or `activity.cook-own-pot` (₦7,500) under `food`, so cooking is never free away from the family. The room abroad cooks in the country's money: `activity.cook-own-uk` in the UK (streets:london-life-by-hand), `activity.cook-own-canada` (C$8), `activity.cook-own-uae` (AED 8) and `activity.cook-own-italy` (€4) (streets:calgary-life-by-hand, streets:dubai-life-by-hand and streets:castel-volturno-by-hand); `activity.cook-own` is `where: "home"`, so no naira is charged abroad. Each corner's acts are named by id in its place file, never by a cost-by-place rule in the sim. The family's monthly figure is told as your share for the house, Papa paying the rest, on the home card, in the move lines and in the bill notice. Anyone visiting the family house may eat from the pot free; it is a repeat act there, true to life.

**home:things-a-life-owns** What a life owns (`../design/08-the-lived-month.md` section 2, decisions 0018 and 0019): the `things` slice (`owned`: each thing once, with the month it came and what was paid) and the content kind `thing` (`packages/content/src/thing/`): each thing's `price`, `resaleBp`, `running` cost a month, the `homes` it may stand in (the family house holds the small things in your corner and the car in the carport; a home of your own holds all it lists; Makoko's deck the fan, the radio, the router and the phone), `needs` (the decoder needs the TV), `group` (the light and the cool air do not add up: only the dearest owned counts), `bought` lifts given once, `monthly` lifts at the close, `acts` (what a thing adds to an act, its half-days and its cost share, applied by `withThings` in `actNow`, `resolveSlot` and `activityOptions`; the view shows a changed time as `halfDays`), `eases` (the phone halves the data plan) and `drive` (the car). `thing/buy` at a home in Lagos, the home allowed, the needs owned and the price held, takes the price under the reason `goods`, delivers at once and applies the `bought` lifts; `thing/sell` pays the resale share into the bank under `sale`. In the `due` phase each thing that works takes its running cost under `running` and then gives its monthly lift; short of money it stands idle that month (`things/stood`) and gives nothing. A thing owned in a home that cannot hold it is kept but `stored` and does nothing. The view carries `things` (`owned` with `stored` and `resale`, `market` with each thing's price, resale, running cost and why it is `shut`: owned, home, needs, short or away, `drive`, `running`). Every figure is content's; the prices are working figures from 2025 and 2026 Lagos listings. The Market app (world:phone-rises-over-scene) sells only at home; elsewhere in Lagos it says the rider brings it to your gate, and abroad that the market is a Lagos thing.
