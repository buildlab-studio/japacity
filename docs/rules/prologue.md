# The prologue: 16 to 22

> Fees and allowances are the real ones at the dates given; the draws and gains are working values. Sources at the end.
> Kind: `prologue`. One per character, finished before month 1. Background: `../design/01-concept.md` section 5, `../research/01-japa-journey.md` section 7.
> The fixed lives in `scenario.md` and `route-gates.md` skip the prologue and serve as test fixtures.

Background is drawn and then played: eleven steps from secondary school to where the life sleeps. The front door picks a person, a life and a home, and the life's preset answers every step in one go (prologue:archetype-presets-answers). The result is the starting life.

## Rules

**prologue:prologue-life-starts-empty** A new character from `scenario.prologue` starts with every quality at its minimum except age (192: sixteen years), no money, no job, no documents, no obligations, and the prologue open at step 0. While the prologue is open, `turn/end` and every other command except `prologue/choose` and `turns/grant` are rejected with `prologue-open`.

**prologue:family-draw-on-first-answer** Step 0 is the draw, made on stream `prologue` when the first answer is given (the opening card's one answer, `begin`), not when the prologue opens: `familyMeans` between 10 and 80, `town` (`city` 55%, `town` 45%), `siblings` between 1 and 2 (a house of 5 at most with the parents and the character), `parentsEducated` (chance by family means: 2000 plus 60 per point). The draw sets `quality.family-means`, `quality.network` (city 15, town 5, plus 10 if parents educated) and `quality.standing` 50, and is shown to the player from the card after the opening card. Every life is from a hometown, a Nigerian city or town, named on the page and the family card and in the lines from home. Lagos is the city the game is played in; most lives come to it from elsewhere. The hometown is read from the family drawn, never drawn on its own, so no stream moves and a sibling holding the inviter's draw (person:real-sibling-shares-family-draw) is from the same place: a family drawn in the city is from one of `tuning.prologue.hometowns.city` (25 state capitals and trading cities), one drawn in a town from one of `hometowns.town` (16 smaller towns, none a state capital), picked by its means times 7, its siblings times 13 and 5 for schooled parents, in whole numbers, as the remainder over the list's length; both lists reach the north and the south about evenly, and no hometown is more than a fifth above its fair share over every family the draw can make. A life whose home answer says where it was born is from there: Makoko's is Lagos (`hometowns.byHome`, prologue:makoko-start-never-a-trap). A fixed life names its own (`hometown` on its scenario: the graduate Enugu, the banker Abeokuta, the developer Kaduna, the nurse Owerri, the teacher Uyo). The view carries it as `hometown` (`hometown.<slug>`, its words `hometown.<slug>.name` in content), null until the family is drawn and kept after the prologue closes; a line names it with `{hometown}`, which the screen fills from the view, so one line serves every hometown and no line shows its brace (with no life on screen, "your hometown"). FROM means what "where are you from" means in Nigeria, the family's place of origin, not where the life grew up. The life starts in the family's Lagos house in Surulere, where Mama and Papa live and the player grew up, and the hometown holds the wider family and the village: the uncles, the family meeting, the levies, the burial, the roof of the family's house there, and the sibling who stayed. So the lines that name it are the roof of the house there and the family's contribution to the burial (told by Papa), the harvest (an uncle, from there), the sibling who stayed ("Your sibling in Enugu"), the opening card ("Your family is from Enugu"), and the card's title; Mama's own asks, the parents on the People screen and the call home stay at home.

**prologue:steps-offer-legal-answers** Each later step offers two to four answers. `prologue/choose` must name the current step and one of its answers whose `requires` hold, else `not-legal`. Answers apply effects (qualities, money as `money/add` with reason `gift` for family money and `gig` for earned cash) and may set `flags`. Some answers end in a draw on stream `prologue` whose result is shown as the next card and decides the next step.

**prologue:age-advances-with-steps** Age advances with the steps: each step adds its `months` to `quality.age-months`. With no setback the degree path ends at 277 months (23), the apprenticeship at 283 (23) and the shop counter at 259 (21); a retake (its year and the exam again) or a strike puts the end later. The age picked is the age the life starts at, to the year (decision 0023): each path's first card after school is its age card, whose answers set that age in years, 18 to 29, never below the year its path ends in with no setback (its longest way, every draw without a setback). A strike or a retake that would leave no year of work before that age does not fire: the draw is not made, the exam passes, the strike does not come. After the send-off the years from the path's end to the age picked are worked, never a card: `quality.age-months` becomes the picked years times twelve plus the months into the year the path reached, and each year worked saves ₦120,000 (`earned`, ₦10,000 a month worked, the older start's saving counted once and never twice), the second year bringing the passport held. A retake or a strike costs years of work and their saving, never the age. The age cards take no months; the tile's preset sends the year its path reaches unless the player stepped it older on the door (prologue:front-door-builds-a-person).

**prologue:headlines-and-friend-route** Two headlines pass during the prologue, drawn from the era pool for the years before the game's start, as flavour only: they change no variable. A route a friend took is heard of (`route/hear` with `via: "friend"`) at step 10, by the player's choice of whom to listen to.

**prologue:closing-opens-home-charges** When the last step is answered the prologue closes: the home the last step chose opens its charges (the family house's `obligation.upkeep` at its amount; the room's `obligation.rent-compound` and `obligation.upkeep-compound` with the year and fees taken, home:compound-room-is-a-home; Makoko's `obligation.upkeep-makoko`, prologue:makoko-start-never-a-trap), the turn bank is at `tuning.turn.start`, and the state is a legal starting life in the year its age card picked: scenario:state-built-from-scenario, scenario:new-state-starts-clean and scenario:first-turn-always-endable hold for it, the age it prints in whole years is the age picked (decision 0023), and a test proves both for 200 random prologues.

**prologue:fixed-life-skips-prologue** A fixed life (`scenario.graduate` and the other ready lives) has the prologue closed from the start and no steps recorded.

**prologue:view-shows-step-card** The view shows the current step's card: its text key, its answers with `allowed`, the draw just made in words, and the life so far (qualities and money as they stand). It never shows a draw before it is made.

**prologue:front-door-builds-a-person** The front door builds a person, never picks a named one. The player picks a look from the characters already shipped (whole looks, no sliders; a look never carries a stat), a first name typed by the player or taken from the pool, with a reroll beside the box and three of the pool's names under it while it is empty (a typed name passes the username's checks and can be reported and reset like one), and one archetype. The box is the character's name, never the player's: titled "Character name", "Name" in it while empty, and one line under the title, "What people in your story call you. Type one or take one."; the account sheet's row beside the username's says "Character name" too (account:username-and-unconfirmed-email). Wherever the door opens for a signed-in account (the sign-in path, account:sign-in-without-secret; a later life on the same account, account:start-a-new-life; an account with no character signed in on another device), the username was chosen before the door, so a new run's name starts from it: the run of letters it starts with, a capital first and the rest as typed, or in lower case where those letters are all capitals (`olamide_88` and `OLAMIDE` are Olamide), where that passes the name's checks and is not shorter than the shortest name; otherwise the box starts empty with the three names under it and the reroll, never a name the pool picked. On Play's path, with no account yet, the username is asked on the door's last page, after the name, so the name starts from the pool. A name the player typed, took or rolled is never replaced, and a run kept on the device keeps its name. The name the player gives is the player's own on every screen: no computer person in the life carries it (person:one-mixed-name-pool). The fixed lives are the ready lives, each named by its kind and never its person, behind Settings (prologue:front-door-three-screens), and test fixtures. The figure is a sticker of the shipped look, shot by the shooter as a face, a whole figure with a hand on the hip and a cheer (assets:contributor-renders-fixed-views). Every answer is printed onto a travel data page with the look as the photo once "Begin" has started the life (prologue:front-door-three-screens, prologue:printed-page-hands-over), stamping nothing. The age is set in steps from the tile's own year up to three years older, within 18 to 29 and never past 29 (decision 0023: the age picked is the age the life starts at), never typed: older starts with a little more saved and more papers in order, younger with more years before young-favouring routes close, and the page shows what a step does. The real date of birth at the door is the terms' age check only, separate from the character and kept to the minimum. The person built is kept on the device beside the save (`japacity.person`); a kept look that is not among the shipped looks is dropped alone, the name, the archetype, the colours and the photo standing, and every screen shows the look it shows with no person kept.

**prologue:archetype-presets-answers** An archetype is a preset of the prologue's answers, held by the client, never by the simulation: for each step it names the answer to send, and it presets every step: the exam's way (study), the strike (wait), the send-off (the tile's crowd, as the table names it) and where to sleep (the home chosen on the door, prologue:front-door-three-screens: the family house, or Makoko, the child of the water's own) included. Offline the client sends `prologue/choose` for each step in turn, at once, with the preset's answer while it is `allowed`; where a draw has shut it, the open answer that suits the archetype goes instead. Online, Begin sends the preset as one request and the server plays every step in one transaction (server:server-plays-prologue-preset), the answer at each step from content's `presetAnswer`, the same function the client plays offline; the page fills from the reply's trail, its chips coming in one after another over about two seconds so the years still read as years. No card of "The years go by" shows for a new life; the draws still run, and the page says how the years went (prologue:printed-page-hands-over). A card shows, as prologue:view-shows-step-card has it, only for a life with no archetype: a fixed scenario a test starts, or the setting below. Every archetype's tile names the routes it opens in words a first-time player reads at once (the road is "the road through the desert"), with the year it hands over from (its path's end with no setback; older is the player's step), and every archetype opens at least one route of its own effort (the UK master's, the skilled or the talent route, UK care, Canada's points, the Gulf contract, the road). The full eleven-card prologue is behind the device setting `japacity.prologue.full` (`on` shows it), off by default. The page's line of who the person is says the archetype in a few words. Words on a tile: its tag, the name, one line of what it was, one line of what it opens.

**prologue:tile-path-open-from-start** A tile's path is open from the first card, since the family's draw alone would shut "Learn a trade" three times in five. The front door starts the life on a seed whose draw admits the tile's first preset answer, trying seeds in turn (up to eight) where the authority lets a life be started over (local); where it does not (online, the server makes one fresh seed for each life and retries none, even when a life is ended and started over, account:start-a-new-life), the tile's words say the years may go another way, and the handover reads the life as lived (`sixteenPath`), never the tile.

**prologue:makoko-start-never-a-trap** One starting archetype begins in Makoko: its life starts in Makoko's room (home.md) with the family house still there to go back to, its cards answered as a child of the water would. A Makoko start is never a trap: its first months' work is at hand, its saving is faster than a start at the family house, its tile names a way out it reaches as surely as the trader reaches the Gulf, and an eviction leaves it a place to go the same month; its odds of leaving are no worse than any other start, so it reads as a hard start with momentum, never a punishment. Its page's chips of the years before are hers, school by canoe and the fish racks in place of a shop counter, and its draw is told in Makoko's words (the begin card's own lines), never as a town.

**prologue:front-door-three-screens** The front door is three screens with everything already chosen, so Next, Next, Begin starts a life. Every screen stands on `DoorFrame`: the flat sky in both paints, round Back and Shuffle, the Skip pill, one heading and one line, the three dots over the yellow action, the door's 600 px column on a laptop, the one the start and age sheets use; `data-gate` carries each screen's mark (Where you live carries the stage id `page`). The door's head row (Back, the lockup, Shuffle, Skip) stands on the sky's own ground above the body, so nothing scrolled shows through it; while the body has more below its fold the foot over the dots says so with a soft edge, gone once the end shows. Skip on the first two screens goes to Where you live; Shuffle picks a look, a name and a tile at random. A name that fails its checks sends the run back to the first screen, saying why.

*You*: the lockup above the title row, light by day and dark at night (the other two screens carry none); the photo card with the whole figure, the sun or the moon, the name on a sticker; the name box with the dice: a first name filled from the pool for the look's sex (or from the username, prologue:front-door-builds-a-person), the dice for another, and typing keeps it (a new look then keeps the typed name); the look strip as round face stickers of every look, no body row; Make it yours as a disclosure (prologue:player-colours-on-looks). Make it yours, opened, scrolls into the door's body as far as needed to show it whole once its rows stand, and each colour picked brings the next part to choose whole into view the same way; smooth, or at once under reduced motion. Online the door's first screen is the start screen (account:start-screen-over-live-city), whose sheet carries the lockup at its head; the account screens carry it on their band.

*Your life so far*: the lives as big sticker cards swiped sideways in a scroll-snap deck, the first chosen, each with its thing on its tint, the player's face, the tag chip, the name, the life in a few words from content `prologue.archetype.<id>.life`, what it was and a savings chip; the picked life's routes as picture chips; pager dots; the age stepper card under them, preset to the tile's own and stepped as prologue:front-door-builds-a-person says.

*Where you live*: a strip with the face, the name, the path and the age; the home picked as the front postcard with the J postage stamp carrying the money to start, the others fanned behind; a row of small home tiles to switch, the pick ringed, a home short of money shut with a lock and short or needed; the picked home's line and costs as chips; the self-contain as the locked goal row; Begin the one action; online the guest's username fields above Begin. The homes come in the order your own room (the compound room), the hostel room and the family house, offered to every tile, and Makoko for the child of the water alone (its default). Each home has its icon, its name and place ("The family house · Surulere", "A room on the water · Makoko"), a line of what it is under its name ("Cheap, by the month, the corridor never sleeps"), and a cost line. Each home's picture is the room as the game draws it in `high`, with shadows and antialiasing, at three picture pixels for each CSS pixel of the largest box it fills (the postcard in front), so it is sharp on any phone whatever mode the city runs in. The money to start is what the life holds at Begin: the tile's `savings` figure (`prologue.archetype.<id>.savings` in content, whole naira, kept within a quarter of the median of its lives' naira at the close by a test) and the age step's saving, less what moving in takes (the room's year and fees): the figure the band shows when the world opens, with "about" before it where the money is drawn, not set (a trade's settlement). The month's living is named apart on the cost line ("Then ₦50,000 a month"). The room's line says what moving in takes with the agent's and the agreement's fees (`tuning.home.compound.rentYear` and `feesBp`, home:compound-room-is-a-home), the figure Bills asks for the move, then the year's rent after it: "₦420,000 to move in, then ₦350,000 a year · living ₦50,000 a month". The hostel's reads "Then ₦15,000 a month · no year's rent". The family house's tells the month's measure as your share, bought as you go, since nothing is charged there (home:living-by-hand). Where the move-in and a month of living cannot be paid, the shortfall shows over "short" (for the room, the figure still needed over "needed"), the home shut with a lock. The room opens only when what is left after the rent and fees still covers a month of living in it (`tuning.home.compound.upkeep`). The self-contain is always shown, locked, as the goal, with its figure (`tuning.home.selfContainRentYear`, ₦700,000, plus the same fees) and no action; for a tile whose savings at the age picked already cover it, the row says "reached" beside the lock and stays, never changing or leaving. **prologue:printed-page-hands-over** The page is the handover.
