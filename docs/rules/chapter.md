# Chapters

> The prologue is a set piece (`prologue.md`), not a chapter.
> Kind: `chapter`. One current at a time. Background: `../design/01-concept.md` section 10.

A chapter is a short checklist that teaches one part of the game and ends with a full bank of turns.

## Rules

**chapter:first-chapter-starts-current** A new character's current chapter is the first by order, and none is done.

**chapter:met-items-close-the-chapter** In the `chapter` phase, if every item of the current chapter is met, it is done, the next by order becomes current, and the turn bank is refilled. At most one chapter closes in a turn. A chapter whose entry names a `closesOn` item closes when that one item is met whatever the others say: chapter 3's is `granted`, since a grant is decision day whether or not the held funds lasted. The chapter phase runs on the turn the character departs as well, so chapter 4 can close and the send-off card be made (chapter:send-off-card-on-close). A grant finishes the chapters before and at decision day: once a route with a country is `granted` or `departed` (every route but the road: a partner's and a dependant's count), the current chapter is finished whenever its base comes at or before decision day by order, whatever its items say. The skilled worker's route has no variant of its own, and the base's items name the master's. The one exception to one close a turn is the flight: on a turn such a route is `departed`, the chapters close in order up to and including the send-off in that same turn, each its own close with its own refill, so the send-off card shows from the flight (chapter:send-off-card-on-close) even when the life flew from an earlier chapter. A close the grant made, not the items, rests on that grant, and a grant withdrawn before the flight reopens it (chapter:done-chapter-stays-done).

**chapter:done-chapter-stays-done** A done chapter stays done, whatever happens to its items afterwards, with one exception: a chapter closed on a grant that is withdrawn before the flight is reopened. A close is on the grant when the grant clause of chapter:met-items-close-the-chapter or the flight's chain closed it and its items did not: the `chapter/closed` event then carries `onGrant`, the route granted or flown, and the slice keeps `byGrant` (`{ chapter, route }`, absent while none) for those closes. When `route/withdraw` (person:joint-collapses-with-bond) takes a dependant grant back to `known` while the character is at home and no route is granted or departed, the chapter phase that turn emits `chapter/reopened`: the chapters closed on the grant leave done, the first of them by order is current again as the variant standing in for its base under the lean as it now is (chapter:chapters-follow-lean-route), `byGrant` is cleared, and no chapter closes that turn. No refill is taken back. The report's line: "The visa is gone. Back to {chapter}." A flight makes the closes final: once the route is `departed`, the chapter phase clears `byGrant` (`chapter/sealed`) and nothing reopens.

**chapter:no-chapter-after-last** When the last chapter is done there is no current chapter.

**chapter:items-are-live-requirements** Items are requirements, checked against the state as it is. The view shows each item of the current chapter as met or not.

## Entries

| Order | Id | Title | Items |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `chapter.find-your-feet` | Find your feet | `job`: have a job. `pay`: one month in any job. `heard`: have heard of a route of your own effort (the UK master's, care, talent or skilled, Canada's points, the Gulf contract: any of them). `known`: have looked into it |
| 2 | `chapter.build-the-file` | Build the file | `passport`: passport held. `transcript`: transcript held. `english`: English result at band 6.0 or better. `offer`: an offer from a school |
| 3 | `chapter.decision-day` | Decision day | `deposit`: deposit paid. `held`: funds covered for a month. `tb`: TB certificate held. `applied`: application sent. `granted`: the visa is granted |
| 4 | `chapter.send-off` | The send-off | `departed`: you have flown |
| 5 | `chapter.landing` | Landing | `room`: a roof (an open `rent` obligation, or `status.gulf-worker` held, since the employer houses the Gulf worker). `job`: a job abroad held one month. `status`: the first rule of your status read (the `status/is` requirement holds). `request`: the first request from home answered, read as ₦150,000 or more in naira, bank and cash together (a family request comes only then, "not when broke" in `offer.md`, and is answered or lapses within a month). `file`: a first mark on the credit file (credit 3 or more) |
| 6 | `chapter.secure` | Secure | Any one of: `settled`: a settled or citizen status held; `home`: a return `with-papers` or `by-choice` recorded; `second`: a second route granted after a return. The chapter's items are an `any` requirement, so one path closes it |

The orders in the data run 1, 2, 7, 12, 13, 14 for these six, with the variants of chapter:chapters-follow-lean-route at 3 to 6 (the file for care, talent, points and the Gulf) and 8 to 11 (their decision days); the engine reads the base's place in the journey, and one order belongs to one chapter.

A refusal leaves chapter 3 open. Its items come undone and can be met again: regrouping is part of the chapter.

**chapter:send-off-card-on-close** Closing `chapter.send-off` produces the send-off card: the view's `card` carries the route, the japacity figure, the months played, the standing, and the three lines the player chose most (the activities with the most months in `activity.played`, activity:months-played-counted, counted up to the month the chapter closes). The interface renders it as an image sized for WhatsApp. The card is data in the view; it is never stored in the state. It shows from the flight until the first month abroad ends, so its standing and its lines are the flight month's. A chapter that closes in the same report as the send-off (the flight's turn, chapter:met-items-close-the-chapter) has no chapter card of its own; the send-off card is that report's. The card is not the screen the flight shows (that is the send-off scene, chapter:send-off-scene-in-beats): it is offered as the keepsake of share:send-off-card-kept.

**chapter:long-game-after-last-chapter** Chapters 5 and 6 refill the bank as every chapter does. After `chapter.secure` there is no current chapter and the long game (`goal.md`) is what the view shows in its place.

**chapter:family-marks-the-grant** The month the story's visa is granted, home marks it: `offer.family-send-off` opens once at the family house (`when`: the story's route is `granted`; `maxTimes` 1; `chanceBp` 10000 while the condition holds, never by chance; `expiresAfter` 2), staged on the house so the nudge's asks and the house's own act both reach it. Its choices: `party` (a send-off at the house, `tuning.chapter.sendOffNaira` from the naira by transfer, the bank first; wellbeing +6, standing +4, network +3; a results line that names who came), `dinner` (a quiet last dinner: free; wellbeing +3, standing +2), `skip` (no time: standing -2). Lapsed, it is `dinner`. Nothing else in the simulation changes; the plane still needs the held funds returned (route:deposit-secures-school).

**chapter:chapters-follow-lean-route** The chapters follow the way out the life leans to. A chapter entry may carry `route` and `variantOf`: it is that base chapter's variant for one route of the player's own effort. The lean route is the first own-effort route the life has heard of or looked into (`route.uk-care`, `route.uk-talent`, `route.canada-points`, `route.gulf-contract`, else `route.uk-masters`), read again each time a chapter closes; when the base chapter's turn comes by order, the variant for the lean route stands in for it when one exists, else the base. The player may choose the way the chapters follow: `chapter/lean` (`route`, a way of the player's own effort heard of, with a file of its own, while at home and no visa granted) sets `chapter.chosen`, read before the time order; the Japa app names the plan ("Your plan: care work") and another way's page says what switching would do to japacity with "Keep mine" and "Make this the plan". The view carries `plan: { route, ways }`. The variants of `chapter.build-the-file` and `chapter.decision-day`:
- care: `tenure` (24 months as a nurse, `activity/tenure` on `activity.job-nurse`), `verification` (`document.nmcn-verification` held), `sponsor` (a sponsor recorded, the gate step), `applied`; decision day: `tb`, `police`, `applied`, `granted`.
- talent: `portfolio` (`quality.portfolio` at the endorsement's bar), `endorsement` (the gate step), `applied`; decision day: `tb`, `applied`, `granted`.
- points: `eca` (`document.eca` held), `english` (the band at 60), `pool` (the gate step), `invited`; decision day: `police`, `applied`, `granted`.
- gulf: `passport`, `medical`, `agent`, `contract` (the gate steps; the passport first, since only the road leaves without papers); decision day: `police`, `applied`, `granted`.
Each item's words say what it takes in a person's words ("Twenty-four months on the ward, then the council's verification"); the nudge's ways for them are in the city's table. The first chapter and the send-off are the same for every life.
The grant decides the plan. Once any route with a country is `granted` or `departed` (the one flown, else the one granted, in content's order; a partner's and a dependant's count as the send-off does, chapter:met-items-close-the-chapter), the chapters follow it, before `chapter.chosen` and before the time order; the lean command already refuses past that point. The current chapter is judged, and the next chosen, by the variant standing in for its base under that route (the base when the route has none), not by the variant that happened to be current, and its close records that variant. The view's items (chapter:items-are-live-requirements) read the same variant, so the Japa app shows the right checklist. The view's `plan.route` names that route too, and `plan.ways` carries it even when it has no file of its own, so the Japa app's "Your plan" and the Papers app's counts name the route actually granted.

**chapter:view-says-closes-and-next** The chapter view carries `closes`, true when the current chapter would close at this month's end as things stand (every item met, or the one item its `closesOn` names, chapter:met-items-close-the-chapter), and `next`, the chapter that follows it by order with the lean as it stands now (chapter:no-chapter-after-last, chapter:chapters-follow-lean-route), null after the last. Japa shows the chapter ahead from these and keeps no copy of the order or the lean.

**chapter:first-chapter-mission-card** The first chapter opens as a mission card handed by its person, once per life, in their voice, with the three things as sticker tiles, the reward as picture chips, the next brief faint and Let's go (design page 09 section 4). A mark kept on the device beside the save and the person is written as it shows, so a reload never shows it again, and a new life shows it again. The card waits for the place's first frame and stands on the drawn place, dimmed as behind the chapter's close, never on a blank world. The wait is marked in the document (`[data-brief-pending]`), so the keep-this-life ask (account:guest-secures-the-life) and the browser suite's helpers wait with it, and nothing is tapped under a card still coming. A later chapter, in a life that has not played a month, opens with the same mission card: its opening line and its brief in the giver's words, with Let's go. A chapter that closes while the world is open comes back as the close card with every tick, the stamp, confetti and the next briefs as picture cards. The brief's words are content's, keyed by chapter (world:nudge-says-one-thing), and the Japa app keeps the log of the briefs carried and done (world:phone-holds-what-phones-hold).

**chapter:send-off-scene-in-beats** The send-off is a scene: six beats, seen once per life, from the airport's gate to the first ride abroad, each beat one screen with one yellow button and nothing else to decide.

1. Departures: the airport place dressed as the hall, the counters, the board, two people ahead in the queue, the family behind the rope. Mama says the first of the airport's own overheard lines (place.md, `place.airport-lagos.overheard.*`) as the hall opens. The button "Step forward" is tapped three times, each tap moving the queue one place and bringing one line in a bubble over the speaker's head: the cousin's (line 2), Papa's (line 4) and the character's own (line 5). Line 3 is nobody's and is not said. After the third tap the counter comes by itself. The scene adds one line to the airport's, Papa's at the gate, `place.airport-lagos.overheard.6` with its `.by` (city:place-words-are-text-keys).
2. The counter: the passport and the boarding stub (the stub of share:card-drawn-on-device, landscape) slide across as stickers, the flight chip shows what was paid (the flight's figure of route:departure-pays-flight, or "Paid by the employer"), and the counter asks "Window or aisle?" as two chips that change nothing but the seat words. The stub carries no seat letter: the choice is kept in words, "By the window" or "On the aisle" (share:send-off-card-kept), and the stub sticker is the share stub's own words (The send-off, Lagos to the city, the first name, the seat words), never a boarding pass, a gate or a seat code (share:card-never-looks-official). The button is "Take the stub".
3. The gate: the character at the gate's door with the plane in the window, the family waving behind (the cheer pose turned to a wave), Papa's line. The button "Board the plane" is the one act that sends `route/depart` (chapter:send-off-card-on-close and route:departure-pays-flight hold: the flight is paid here and the chapter closes here), so beats 1 and 2 change nothing in the life, and leaving the scene before this beat leaves the life at the airport. A small "Not yet" button, or Escape, leaves beats 1 to 3 with nothing sent, and the act opens the scene again from its start.
4. The plane: a cut of about three seconds (world:plane-cut-at-send-off), the night map, the plane lifting off Lagos along a dashed line toward the route's city, the city's name arriving on a sticker; a still with the name under reduced motion.
5. Out into the place: the landing as the route's first neighbourhood (the country's hub place in content, `place.peckham` for the UK, `place.calgary-north-east` for Canada, `place.deira` for the UAE, `place.castel-volturno` for Italy), dusk, the weather chip of that place, the bag on the ground, someone at the stop saying the place's first overheard line (Peckham's "You just land? Welcome. It gets colder." is in content), and the country's staple pulling in. The button is the staple's first ride in its words alone, with no fare and no figure typed: London the red double-decker with the hub's name on its blind, "Get on the bus"; Calgary snow, breath clouds, a pickup and the long flat road, the chip at minus twenty, "Get in the truck"; Dubai the heat shimmer and the game's cream taxi, "Get in the taxi"; Castel Volturno the bus along the Domitiana, "Get on the bus". The person at the stop is each city's own look from the published cast and of the sex the stop's label names (an aunty in London, a neighbour in Calgary, a man at the corner in Dubai, a brother at Castel Volturno; `STOP_PEOPLE` in `sendoff/plan.ts`), never one person for all and never the character's own look. They stand at the character's own scale, never smaller, and the stop's line hangs from a fixed foot above the character's head, so a longer line grows upward and never covers the face.
6. The first ride: inside the ride with the place coming at the window (London the top deck's front window and Rye Lane, the others their own road). The ride's inside is the staple's own: the top deck with its pole and seats for London, the pickup's cab with its wheel and bench for Calgary, the taxi's back seat behind the front seats and the driver for Dubai, the Domitiana bus with its rail, handles and pole for Castel Volturno. The character holds the bag: "So this is it." The family's first messages ping in a stack, each whole, beside the character and clear of her: the first Mama's, the sender the game has (`chats.from.mum`: "You have landed? Have you eaten? Is it cold? Call me."), the second the cousin's size 44 line, the third Terna's. The friend's ping ends on the staple she rides ("Even the bus stop." on a bus, "Even the taxi.", "Even the truck."), while the phone's Chats keeps content's one line. The chip "Send-off card saved" with a tick (share:send-off-card-kept) stands in the scene's toast lane under the band, and the Phone's badge counts the pings. The button "Ring the bell" ends the scene, and the life stands in the room abroad as after any flight. The landing's moment card (moment:outcomes-that-are-moments) waits while the scene shows and follows Ring the bell as the landing's stamp with its count of months; the scene does not replace it.

Beats 4 to 6 run on the client over the view after the reply; the simulation knows nothing of them, and a life reopened after beat 3 opens in the room abroad with no scene. Every beat's motion is transform and opacity under half a second (world:motions-under-half-second), still under reduced motion; the plane's lift of about three seconds (world:plane-cut-at-send-off) is the one motion longer. The scene is never counted beyond the events the flight already sends (analytics:client-sends-interface-counts has no key for it). The scene's own band shows the character's first name, the age, the beat's place and the money, naira before the plane and the country's money after; its place line is never cut, the age kept whole and the place wrapping to a second line. The scene opens from the gate's act and from the packed bag at home alike, loads in its own chunk (engine:first-download-has-budget) and is handed the game and the person by the city, so it pulls nothing into the first visit.

The scene's people stand at one scale and clear of its buttons. The yellow button, Not yet and the thumbs stand in a lane at the foot (`LANE_TOP`) that no person of the hall, the counter or the gate reaches into. In the hall the character stands in the queue as its last, the two ahead and she at one size, stepping up a place a tap to the counter; the clerks behind the three counters are smaller with distance and the family behind the rope nearest. At the counter the officer behind the desk is smaller than the character and his head no higher than hers, the Window and Aisle chips above his head. At the gate the family waving stand above the lane, and Papa's bubble hangs above their raised hands, its tail at him, clear of his head. Every bubble's tail sits in its border with no gap.
