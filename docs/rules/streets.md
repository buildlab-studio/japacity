# The streets' life

> Rules for the hidden places' life: Computer Village, Third Mainland Bridge, the National Stadium, and the streets abroad. Rests on `world.md` (things and acts), city:places-stage-their-acts (staging), luck:luck-never-reads-money (chance never reads money), health:low-wellbeing-warns-first (the run-down months), `quality.md`, `activity.md`, `offer.md`. The numbers are working values.
> Kinds: `activity` and `offer` entries, one tuning block of its own, `tuning.street`, and `sex` on the scenario. No kind of its own.

Each hidden place has something real to do. Every act here is a thing in the place (world:things-are-tappable-models), happens through the kinds the game already has, and is told straight: never graphic, never a joke.

## Rules: Computer Village

**streets:buy-phone-at-computer-village** A phone can be bought at Computer Village: `activity.buy-phone`, slot `none` (a purchase done now, activity:now-acts-and-month-acts), cost `tuning.street.phonePrice` from the naira, by its size (decision 0014: cash first at or under `tuning.wallet.cashUpTo`, the bank first above it), staged at `place.computer-village`. It lifts `quality.wellbeing` by `tuning.street.phoneMood` and `quality.network` by `tuning.street.phoneNetwork`. It can be bought again; each is a new phone.

**streets:phone-sales-job** Selling phones is a job: `activity.job-phone-sales`, a main-slot job hunted and held like any other (`activity.md`), pay `tuning.street.salesPay` a month, hire chance `tuning.street.salesHireBp`, needing `quality.education-level` 2 or more, staged at `place.computer-village`. Each month held raises `quality.tech` by 1 and lowers `quality.wellbeing` by 1.

**streets:apprentice-learns-repair** The trade can be learned as an apprentice: `activity.apprentice-repair`, a free slot, no pay, staged at `place.computer-village`. Each month raises `quality.tech` by `tuning.street.apprenticeTech` and lowers `quality.wellbeing` by 1. It is allowed while `quality.tech` is under `tuning.street.apprenticeUntil`.

## Rules: Third Mainland Bridge

**streets:sleeping-under-the-bridge** Under the bridge is where the character sleeps when they have nowhere else: `activity.sleep-rough`, a free slot, staged at `place.third-mainland-bridge`, allowed only while naira cash and bank together are under `tuning.place.brokeLine`. It costs nothing and gives the rest an empty slot gives (`tuning.quality.wellbeingRest`). It has a price, each drawn on the `offer` stream as a bad offer of the `life` group that opens only in a month the character slept rough (`when` reads the month's played activities and never money, luck:luck-never-reads-money): `offer.bridge-theft` at `tuning.street.theftBp`, which takes `tuning.street.theftNaira` from cash (nothing when cash is short, wallet:no-pocket-below-zero); `offer.bridge-mosquitoes` at `tuning.street.mosquitoBp`, which adds 1 to `quality.run-down-months` (the warning path of health:low-wellbeing-warns-first); `offer.bridge-night` at `tuning.street.harassBp`, which lowers `quality.wellbeing` by `tuning.street.harassMood`, told as harassment when the character is a woman and as being challenged to a fight when a man (`sex` on the scenario, streets:sex-optional-with-neutral-path), and as trouble in the night when the life does not say. Each has one choice, to carry on. The lines are plain.

## Rules: abroad

**streets:street-shopping-abroad** Shopping on the street abroad: `activity.street-shopping-uk` and `activity.street-shopping-uae`, each a purchase that answers at once, as often as the money allows and taking no time (activity:now-acts-and-month-acts, activity:now-acts-spend-half-days), costing `tuning.street.shoppingGbp` pence or `tuning.street.shoppingAed` fils from that currency's cash under the money reason `enjoyment`, lifting `quality.wellbeing` by `tuning.street.shoppingMood`. The UK's stalls stand on Rye Lane (`place.rye-lane` stages `activity.street-shopping-uk`), and nothing of it fills the belly. The UAE's souq stands at Naif (`place.naif` stages `activity.street-shopping-uae`, streets:dubai-life-by-hand). Italy's evening at the Nigerian shop is streets:castel-volturno-by-hand's `activity.street-shopping-italy` on the Domitiana.

**streets:street-finds-abroad** Finding something interesting: `offer.street-find-uk` and `offer.street-find-uae`, offers of the `life` group opening at `tuning.street.findBp` a month while the character is in that country, two choices: look, lifting `quality.wellbeing` by `tuning.street.findMood`, and walk on, which changes nothing; each with a line about what was found, drawn from the entry's lines.

**streets:peckham-talks-like-lagos** People talk to you in Peckham as they do in Lagos: the place's overheard lines in content, in Nigerian voices (world:street-talk-budget), at least six.

## Rules: the stadium

**streets:stadium-morning-run** The stadium is for a morning run: `activity.morning-run`, a free slot, no cost, staged at `place.national-stadium`, lifting `quality.wellbeing` by `tuning.street.runMood`. Nothing else is staged there; it is a landmark.

## Rules: the character

**streets:sex-optional-with-neutral-path** A scenario may say the character's `sex` (`f` or `m`); the prologue's life does not, and a rule that reads it then takes the neutral path. Nothing else reads it.

**streets:parcels-shipped-home** Things shipped home. On a birthday month at home, while a relative abroad is in the story (a sibling whose facts put them abroad at any stage of the bond but ended, or the aunty once a route is known), the offer kind opens `offer.parcel-shipped` by its own rule, never by a roll, and hands over who sent it (`from`) and which one thing the parcel holds, drawn on the stream `offer` from a phone (what `activity.buy-phone` gives, streets:buy-phone-at-computer-village), a laptop (wellbeing `tuning.parcel.laptopMood`), clothes (`quality.interview-prep` `tuning.parcel.clothesPrep`) or foodstuff (the next month's living costs relieved as cooking does, obligation:cooking-and-fetching-water). It waits at the cargo office on Bode Thomas Street (`place.bode-thomas`, the booth with its boards, the bags and the clerk) until it is collected there for the clerk's handling charge, `tuning.parcel.handling` in naira, cash first (decision 0014); only the thing it holds can be taken. The sheet is the waybill, a paper slip (world:one-action-one-sheet) with the sender's note in their own words and one button, Collect it. Its Not now lowers the asks sheet it stands in and the parcel waits on; a life short of the charge is never held on the slip. While the naira held pays no fare and the cargo office is a ride away, the nudge does not point at the parcel (world:nudge-says-one-thing). From anywhere else in the city the nudge names it; a flight closes it unanswered (offer:lapsed-offer-removed). Nothing here moves money between lives (person:money-counted-never-moved).

**streets:lagos-street-spending** Enjoyment and spending on the street (decision 0012: living in Lagos is the loop, japa the goal that makes each choice cost). The streets carry priced now-acts (activity:now-acts-and-month-acts, activity:acts-may-repeat-in-month) that answer at once, paid from the naira as they are done under the money reason `enjoyment`, each spending its time (turn:month-lived-in-half-days) and moving the body and the self a little (quality:body-belly-and-energy); content holds them in `activity.enjoy.data.ts`. At `place.ojuelegba`: `activity.buy-suya` (a wrap of suya, ₦2,500, a morning, repeats: belly +20, wellbeing +5), `activity.mallam-tea` (tea with the mallam, ₦500, a morning, repeats: wellbeing +4, energy +5, standing +1), `activity.pure-water` (cold pure water, ₦100, no time, a purchase, `purchase: true` on the entry so it runs as often as money allows: energy +4, wellbeing +2). At `place.bode-thomas`: `activity.barber` (a fresh low cut, ₦3,000, a morning, once a month: wellbeing +5, standing +1, interview-prep +2), `activity.viewing-centre` (the match, ₦1,000, a day, repeats: wellbeing +8, energy -3, network +1), `activity.beer-joint` (a cold one at the joint, ₦2,500, a morning, repeats: wellbeing +6, belly +5, energy -4), `activity.night-out` (a night out, ₦20,000, a day, repeats: wellbeing +12, energy -20, network +2). At `place.tejuosho-market`: `activity.buy-clothes` (new clothes, ₦15,000, a day, once a month: standing +2, wellbeing +4). The simulation knows no hour: which of these the street offers at which part of the world's day is the interface's (world:things-are-tappable-models: the viewing centre from the evening, the joint and the night out in the evening and at night), as the sky is.
