# Activities and jobs

> Pay and prices are set against the real figures in Sources and in `../research/09-uk-masters-numbers.md` (Pay in Lagos), each row with its real figure or "working value"; the English test's fee is the real one (`document.md`).
> Kind: `activity`. Background: `../design/01-concept.md` section 9; the ladders, the pay review and learning: `../design/13-the-economy.md`, decisions 0031 and 0032.

A month has one main activity, the job, and two or three free slots. An activity is an entry with requirements, a cost and a list of effects.

## Rules

**activity:one-job-paid-monthly** A character holds at most one job. In the `main` phase of each month a job is held, its pay is credited to the bank with the reason `salary`, the time in that job goes up by one month, and its effects apply.

**activity:illegal-slot-rejects-turn** `turn/end` is rejected with `not-legal` if any slot names an activity that is not a free-slot activity, whose requirements do not hold, or whose param is not one it may take now.

**activity:slots-resolve-in-order** Slots resolve in order. When a slot resolves, its requirements are checked again and its cost must be payable. If either fails, the activity fails: nothing is paid, no effect applies, and the report says why.

**activity:costs-paid-bank-or-cash** Each cost is paid under its own ledger reason. A cost in naira is paid by transfer, the bank first and then cash; a street price (the kiosk, the bet slip, the phone stall) follows its size, cash first at or under `tuning.wallet.cashUpTo` and the bank first above it (decisions 0012 and 0014). A cost in a foreign currency follows wallet:foreign-fee-payment-order.

**activity:job-hunt-lands-offer** One month of job hunting lands the named job with a chance of its base chance plus 20 basis points for each point of network, never more than 9500. On success a job offer opens that names the job. The job's requirements must hold to hunt for it, and a job already held cannot be hunted. A job at the hire cap reads "As good as certain" on the board and sorts with the likely ones (the racks, activity:fish-racks-at-makoko); every other job reads its own chance word on the board and on the hunt's card, and nothing on either says a hunt is sure. The draw alone lands a job: a hunt that misses says the named job came to nothing (`activity/hunted` with `hired` false), and the plan's other acts never touch it; a starting life's first miss opens a lead (activity:missed-hunt-opens-a-lead). The board opens on the life's own trade: the first job in its order that asks for something beyond schooling (a skill such as code or care, a degree's grade, a paper of its own, or months in a job before it), else its first row; with a job held, the first of these that pays more than it; in Makoko the racks stay the pick while they lead (activity:fish-racks-at-makoko). The order of the rows does not change.

**activity:new-job-replaces-held** Starting a job replaces the job held, at once. `activity/quit` leaves the character with no job; with no job to leave it is rejected with `not-legal`. Time served in a job is kept when it is left.

**activity:missed-hunt-opens-a-lead** A miss moves the story. In a life's first three months (the month being played, 1 to 3), the first hunt at home that misses while the life holds no job and has no job offer open opens a lead, once a life: after the line that says the named job came to nothing, a person in the life gives a lead (Lanre at the hub, who hands the first briefs), `offer.job-lead`, for the lowest-paid job the life may hunt in that slot: in the main slot the lowest-paid whose pay covers the naira the life owes a month, else the lowest-paid of them; in the side slot the lowest-paid (each valued in naira at the official rate, then by id). When that job is the one just missed, the lead is `offer.job-word` for it instead: the person put in a word for that same job. Either is an offer like the job offer: `accept` starts the job, `decline` lets it go, and it lapses after two months into nothing. Its line, `line.activity.hunt-lead` or `line.activity.hunt-word`, has tone `good`. The activity slice keeps the mark `lead` once a lead is given, and no second lead opens in that life, whether its hunts were now-acts or a month's slots. A hunt that lands, a hunt in the fourth month or later, a second miss, a hunt with a job held and a hunt with a job offer open open none. The lead's words name the person and the place, never a real business, and never say the job is sure; `offer.job-lead`'s words say in a short clause what the job costs the trade just hunted (the nurse: it pays, but it will not count for the wards), on its sheet above its answers and in the month's line.

**activity:requirements-judged-at-hunt** Requirements decide who can get a job. They are judged when the hunt lands, not again when the job offer is accepted. A job already held is kept even if they stop holding.

**activity:side-gig-pays-a-draw** A side gig pays a draw between its lowest and highest figure, plus its per-point figure for each point of tech, landing where its size says (wallet:money-lands-as-in-lagos: a sum over `tuning.wallet.cashUpTo` is a transfer to the bank). Reason `gig`.

**activity:last-month-plan-kept** The free slots played last month are stored. The client does not ask for the same again, since the plan carries on by itself (world:month-acts-carry-on).

**activity:side-job-holds-a-slot** A side job (`slot: "side"`) holds one free slot for as long as it is kept: it is started like a job (hunted, then offered) and ends with `activity/quit` with `side: true`. While held, it locks one slot (turn:slots-can-be-locked with the reason `side-job`), pays each `main` phase (naira under `gig` to the pocket its size takes, wallet:money-lands-as-in-lagos: cash at or under `tuning.wallet.cashUpTo`, the bank above it; a foreign currency to that currency's bank pocket under `salary`), costs its monthly wellbeing, and halves every skill gain as quality:high-skills-gain-slower does at the soft cap. One side job at a time. The view's `job` carries it as `side` (null for none).

**activity:months-played-counted** The slice counts the months each activity was played over the whole life, main or free, in `played`: one for each month it was in the slots at `turn/end`, and one for each month a now-act was done (activity:now-acts-and-month-acts). The send-off card's three lines come from it (chapter:send-off-card-on-close). The view does not show it.

The view lists each free-slot activity that applies where the character is as an `ActivityOption`: `allowed`; `missing`, the requirements not met now (with the wait for a school's answer when no school can be picked), so the screen can say why not; `params`, the params that may be picked now, null when it takes none; `cost`, the prices for the first param; and `affordable`, whether those prices could be paid now.

**activity:one-chance-bus-robbery** One chance: in each `main` phase at home with a side job held, there is `tuning.ladder.oneChanceBp` on stream `street` that the bus home is a robbery, unless that month `activity.safe-ride` was done (a now-act under activity:now-acts-and-month-acts, counted among the month's now-acts) or a slot held `activity.sleep-at-office`. The loss is every naira in the cash pocket (reason `mishap`) and a phone, ₦60,000 from the bank if the bank holds that much and nothing otherwise (`mishap`), and wellbeing -12, with a line that asks for attention. Beyond the phone, money in the bank is never touched.

**activity:waiting-months-build-profile** The waiting months build the profile toward a route. Each act below is a free-slot activity (or a side job) whose card says what it counts toward, and each moves a figure a route's gate reads:
- `activity.open-source` (the developer, needs tech 40): portfolio +1 every second month played, network +2; counts toward the UK talent route.
- `activity.tech-posts` (the phone, slot none, once a month, needs tech 30): network +1, standing +1; counts toward talent (the endorsement reads the network as the "notable" line).
- `activity.conference-talk` (needs portfolio 2 and network 40, once a life): portfolio +1, standing +5, network +5; a set piece of one question.
- `activity.be-noticed` (any job held 6 months or more): on a draw of 3000 the job's pay rises by `tuning.activity.raiseBp` (1000), once a job, on top of the pay as the yearly review left it (activity:naira-pay-reviewed-yearly); standing +2. The raise shows in the Jobs app on the held job's row ("Raised last month"), through `PlayerView.job.raise` (decision 0002).
- `activity.nurse-certification` (the nurse, care 50, needs the licence; ₦120,000; a wait of 2 months): care +10, `document.nurse-certificate` (for good); counts toward UK care and the Gulf's nursing offers; sat as a short quiz set piece of three questions from the care page's facts, a pass at two.
- `activity.masters-at-home` (a bachelor's, ₦1,800,000 over 12 months as an obligation, 12 months of one slot): education-level 4; counts toward Canada's points (the education row). The course ends with the flight: a course in progress when the character leaves records `activity/course-ended`, its fees close and nothing more is charged; the months played are kept, so a returnee who takes it again goes on from that count.
Every card names its route in words as a chip beside its figures, a plane and the route ("The talent route"), named "Counts toward: the talent route" for a screen reader; the card's line never says it as a clause. The talk's one question marks how it went, as the exam's do (`exam`: pass 1, nothing more given): running the code live or telling the bug's story scores 1, reading the slides 0; the results tell it in one line, "The talk went well: three people asked for your handle." or "The talk fell flat.", with what it counted for, "It counts toward the endorsement's letters.", and "The talk is over." is not told beside it. What the talk gives is the act's own. The care checklist counts the certificate (route-gates:care-route-needs-verification), so the certification card's chip, "Health care in the UK", is true, and the waiting months on the ward name the certification while she can pay it (world:nudge-says-one-thing). The certification is one sitting. It leaves the month's plan the month it runs, pass or fail (world:month-acts-carry-on), so a failed exam is never charged again by itself; "You can sit it again" means a new tap on its card.

**activity:side-jobs-pay-by-tier** Side jobs pay by level, so the waiting months are the saving months: each side job entry carries `tiers`, a list of `{ needs, pay }`, where `needs` is a list of requirements (a quality's figure, months across a trade's jobs); the pay a month is the highest tier whose `needs` all hold, the entry's own `pay` when none. The Jobs app and the board say the tier's pay and what lifts it ("₦80,000 a month; ₦120,000 once your English is strong and you have taught two years").

**activity:now-acts-and-month-acts** An activity entry says when it answers: `when: "now"` or `when: "month"`. A now-act is anything quick, a purchase or a conversation: the kiosk and the bet slip (the slip in hand now, the draw or the match later), asking around (a line from someone there now, the route heard now, the pitch opened now), the official page and the adviser's visit, the meetup, seeing or calling someone, the family meeting, buying a phone and the shopping abroad, the cash gig, posting from the phone, the safe ride, every paper's request (made now, the wait starting now) and every application (sent now, the answer with the month), and the job hunt (applied now, the employer answering with the month). The command `activity/now` (`activity`, `param`) runs a now-act at once for a character where it is staged: the same requirements, cost, effects and lines as a slot resolving (activity:slots-resolve-in-order, activity:costs-paid-bank-or-cash), applied in the month it is sent and answered in the place as it starts, by a toast or, where the answer carries what the player must see to choose, its sheet (world:things-are-tappable-models); its line is kept among the month's in News, and the month's results tell only what it came to (world:month-end-results-sheet); it fills no slot. A now-act that is not a purchase runs once a month unless it repeats (activity:acts-may-repeat-in-month); a purchase as often as money allows. A month-act is anything that fills time: every job and side job, the apprenticeship, study, the course, the portfolio, open source, the talk's preparation, the master's at home, the certification's study, interview practice, being noticed, the morning run, time with family, sleeping at the office or rough, and rest; it stays a slot pick, shows as "This month: ...", and lands in the month's results. In the recorded lives a now-act stands as its command at the tick it was planned. A purchase repeats while money allows, within its own ceiling where its page sets one (the kiosk's monthly tickets, luck:kiosk-sells-lotto-tickets). Cooking and fetching water, and filling the compound's drum, are now-acts that repeat (activity:acts-may-repeat-in-month, obligation:cooking-and-fetching-water). Rest is a month-act in the simulation as the empty slot's meaning, and is never planned (turn:free-slots-per-month).

**activity:trader-opens-own-shop** The trader's own shop. A life with a trade's settlement in hand (`activity.open-shop`: education-level 2 or less, at least ₦1,000,000 held, no shop running) opens its shop at its own stall at Tejuosho market, a free-slot act whose effect starts the main job `activity.run-shop` ("Your shop"): takings of ₦180,000 a month to the bank, growing by `tuning.activity.raiseBp` a year by the months the shop has stood, wellbeing down 3 and standing up 1 a month. No hunt lists it. Opening it meets the first chapter's `job` item; the first takings come the month after. The trader's first chapter speaks in her words (the settlement, the shop) and her way to the job step is the stall. Opening the shop costs nothing; the settlement is not moved on the ledger.

**activity:fish-racks-at-makoko** The fish racks at Makoko (the child of the water, prologue:makoko-start-never-a-trap). `activity.job-fish-racks` is a main job staged at `place.makoko` beside the racks: education-level 0, about ₦70,000 a month, a high hire chance, no hunt needed while the character lives in Makoko (the racks are where they grew up; the board lists it for anyone who asks around at Makoko). It is work at hand and the first rung of the fish trade: the way up is the kiln and then the fish trade, each opened with its capital on the racks' savings (activity:capital-opens-own-work), or the market stall reached on Makoko's savings (activity:trader-opens-own-shop); its side job is the canoe deliveries. The racks end like any job on the flight (activity:side-job-holds-a-slot). While the character lives in Makoko the board lists the racks first, as the nudge names them; elsewhere they take their place by chance and pay.

**activity:now-acts-spend-half-days** The time a now-act spends (decision 0011; turn:month-lived-in-half-days). An activity entry may carry `halfDays`, the month's free time the act spends: 1 a morning (cooking, a call, the kiosk's word, a look into a route), 2 a day (fetching water, the meetup, a paper's request, a hunt), 4 a weekend (the passport office, a visa application, a cash gig), 10 a week. The table is `packages/content/src/activity/activity.days.data.ts`, applied beside `when`. A now-act without a figure spends a morning; a purchase spends none, so the kiosk and the shops never take time; a month-act spends none here, since it fills the month as a commitment. `activity/now` applies the effect `turn/spend-days` after the act's prices are paid, so an act that cannot be paid spends nothing. The place says the time in words beside the act ("A morning", "A weekend"), never a number of days.

**activity:acts-may-repeat-in-month** Acts that repeat (decision 0012). Money and effects move when the act is done, never when the month turns. An activity entry may carry `repeat: true`: a now-act that may run again in the same month, as often as the body allows (activity:spent-body-refuses-acts), each time paid and answered at once, with its effects applied and its `played` count raised at once (`activity/now-played` with `counted`); the month's count of played acts leaves it out, and the month's results do not list it ("Done this month" is for what answered with the month). The repeat acts: the meals (`activity.cook-at-home`, `activity.cook-jollof`), the water (`activity.fetch-water`, `activity.fill-drum`), a nap, a bucket bath, the TV, gist with Mama (`activity.family-time`), Mama's shop (`activity.help-shop`), and the study that answers as it is done: `activity.study-english`, `activity.learn-tech`, `activity.build-portfolio`, each a day or a weekend with a small gain now, three of them a month giving what the month's slot gave; `activity.learn-tech` stops at three a month (activity:study-days-capped). A repeat act may still stand as a commitment in a slot (a recorded life planned it, or a player keeps it up): `turn/end` accepts it and the slots phase runs it `tuning.turn.repeatPerSlot` times (3), each paid, stopping when one can no longer be paid. The acts that are a once-a-month thing (asking around, a paper's request, a hunt) run as activity:now-acts-and-month-acts has them.

**activity:spent-body-refuses-acts** The spent body (decision 0011). What bounds a day of acts is the body, not a hidden budget: once `quality.energy` is under `tuning.turn.body.spentBelow` (10), a now-act that takes any time (`halfDays` above 0, a purchase excepted) is refused by `activity/now` and the view marks it `spent`, so the place says "You are spent. Sleep." where its button was; an act that takes no time (gist, looking, buying) stays open. Sleep (the month's turn) rests the energy back (quality:body-belly-and-energy) and the acts open again. The month's free time (turn:month-lived-in-half-days) is still spent and recorded, and the sky reads it, but it shuts nothing. A spent act's fan button is shut and carries the words; a tap on it sends nothing.

## Rules: ladders, the pay review and learning

**activity:each-trade-climbs-its-ladder** Every trade at home climbs its own ladder (decision 0031): two or three rungs and one side job of its own (Jobs: the ladders, Side jobs). A rung is a job like any other, hunted and held (activity:job-hunt-lands-offer, activity:requirements-judged-at-hunt), or own work opened with its capital (activity:capital-opens-own-work); each rung above the first asks months across the trade's jobs (`activity/tenure` over the trade's rungs, so the months carry from rung to rung) and one quality or paper of the trade. The Jobs app shows the next rung of the trade the held job is in, what it asks and what is still missing, and the passbook's ladder road reads the same rung (wallet:roads-to-the-target). A job whose months count on two ladders, as the bank's rungs count among the accounting jobs, climbs its own trade's: the next rung is the better-paid job that counts the held job's months over the fewest jobs paid no more than the one held, then the lowest paid, so the bank officer's next rung is the senior bank officer. A better-paid rung of the other trade that counts those months among more jobs, the chartered accountant for the bank officer, is the road across: Jobs shows it under the next rung with its pay and that the months held count toward it, Papers lists the papers it asks beside the next rung's (document:trade-papers), and it is never the next rung. Each trade's own surplus reaches a way out that fits it, and the targets for how soon are `../design/13-the-economy.md` section 2, held by scenario:economy-meets-its-targets.

**activity:capital-opens-own-work** Some rungs are opened, not hunted: own work started with its capital. A now-act staged where the work is takes the capital at once from the naira by its size (decision 0014) under the reason `goods`, all or nothing, and starts the job with `activity/start-job`, as the main job or the side job its entry names; with the capital short it is shut with its reason. `activity.buy-kiln` (at `place.makoko`; 12 months on the racks; ₦100,000 for the kiln and the first stock) starts `activity.job-fish-smoker`. `activity.start-fish-trade` (at `place.makoko`; 30 months across the racks and the kiln; ₦300,000 of capital, which a savings circle's pot, luck:savings-circle-holds-pot, and the kiln's takings reach) starts `activity.job-fish-trader`. `activity.open-pos-stand` (at `place.ojuelegba`; a bank job held and no side job; ₦300,000 for the machine and the float) starts the side job `activity.pos-stand`, which a cousin runs in the cousin's name, since banks bar their staff from outside business. Own work is not hunted and leaves the board; its takings grow by `tuning.activity.raiseBp` a year by the months it has stood, as the shop's do (activity:trader-opens-own-shop), and the capital is not given back when it ends. The trader's shop is the one own work that takes nothing, since the settlement is its stock.

**activity:naira-pay-reviewed-yearly** Naira pay is reviewed once a year (decision 0031). At each 12 months held in a naira main job (its own months, so a new rung starts its year again: activity:new-job-replaces-held), in the `main` phase before that month's pay, the pay is reviewed by the entry's `employer`. The job slice keeps the job's pay as held (`pay`, the entry's figure at the start) and `reviewRate`, the world's official dollar rate (kobo for one dollar, `rate.md`) on the day the job started and at each review. `formal`: the pay rises by `tuning.activity.reviewFormalBp` (10%) plus `reviewShareBp` (half) of the official dollar's rise beyond 10% since `reviewRate`, at most `reviewCapBp` (30%) in all; the rise is the rate now less `reviewRate`, times 10,000, over `reviewRate`, rounded down and never below 0. `informal`: the pay rises by `reviewInformalBp` (5%). `own` work is not reviewed and grows by its own rule (activity:capital-opens-own-work, activity:trader-opens-own-shop). The new pay is rounded down to whole naira and `reviewRate` becomes the day's rate. Dollar pay (the remote jobs, night support), every side job and every job abroad are not reviewed: the naira dollar pay buys moves with the world. `activity.be-noticed`'s raise stays once a job, on the reviewed pay (activity:waiting-months-build-profile). The event `activity/reviewed` carries the old and the new pay; the month's results tell it in one line ("Your pay was reviewed: ₦121,000 a month from now."), and the Jobs app shows the held job's next review in months. Real: Zenith Bank raised all staff 20% to 30% from Jan 2025; four banks' wage bill rose 27.49% in 2025 with headcount up 12.75%, about 13% a head; MTN Nigeria's staff bill rose 23.8%; federal civil servants got 25% to 35% (2024); headline inflation was 15.15% in Dec 2025 and 14.63% in Sep 2026; no survey of private-sector raises for 2024 to 2026 was found (Sources). The 10% sits under 2025's 13% a head and under inflation, so a calm year adds a little real pay; the 30% cap, the top of the real 2024 to 2025 rises, leaves naira pay falling against the pound in a crash, as it did.

**activity:study-days-capped** A day's coding class, `activity.learn-tech` (a paid class or a day at a cyber cafe, ₦5,000 under `study`), runs at most `tuning.turn.studyDaysCap` (3) times a month, counting the now-act and a slot's commitment together (activity:acts-may-repeat-in-month); a fourth is refused and the view marks the act `capped` ("Three days of class this month already"). The days of one month give together what the free path's month gives at the band (quality:skill-bands-slow-gains): the nth day gives the free path's month times n over 3, rounded down, less what the earlier days of the month gave. So it is +1 a day while starting; 0, +1, +1 while junior-ready; 0, 0, +1 while working; nothing from 70. It is refused while a tech study path holds a slot (activity:paid-tech-courses): the days are for a life whose slots are full, not a second course on top. Real: AltSchool's short nano-diploma is ₦45,000 and Lagos classroom courses ₦140,000 to ₦500,000 each, so ₦5,000 is a day, never a course (Sources).

**activity:free-path-keeps-a-streak** The free path, `activity.learn-free` ("Free videos and sites"), is a month-act holding one slot at home: the month's data, `tuning.activity.freePathData` (₦6,500, under `study`), and a gain a month by band of +3 while starting, +2 while junior-ready, +1 while working and nothing from 70 (quality:skill-bands-slow-gains): beyond that, real skill comes from work. Discipline is a streak, with no draw: the slice counts the months in a row the path was played (`streak`), and each fifth month in a row finishes a certificate, tech +2 (never past 69) and portfolio +1, told in one line ("You finished a certificate. It goes in your portfolio."). A month the path is not played sets the streak to 0. The path reaches junior-ready from nothing in 10 months, with a certificate on the way. Real: free courses cost the data, and MTN's 15GB a month is ₦6,500 after the Feb 2025 rise; freeCodeCamp's certificates take about 300 hours each, about five months at two hours a day; 3.13% of edX learners completed in 2017 to 2018, 46% on the paid verified track (Sources). The streak is the discipline the real dropout stands for, with no randomness.

**activity:paid-tech-courses** The paid paths, one tech study at a time: while one is held, the free path, the day's class and every other path are refused, and the view says why ("You are on a course"). Each is a month-act holding one slot for its months unless said, its gain a month by band (quality:skill-bands-slow-gains), ending with its closing gain and a line that the course is finished. A course ends with the flight as `activity.masters-at-home` does (activity:waiting-months-build-profile): `activity/course-ended`, its fees closed, the months played kept.
- `activity.online-course` ("A short paid course"): ₦45,000 once at the start, under `study`; 3 months; +6 while starting, +3 while junior-ready and working up to 59, nothing from 60; ends with portfolio +1.
- `activity.tech-diploma` ("A year's diploma"): ₦40,000 a month as `obligation.tech-diploma` for 12 months; 12 months; +4, +3 and +2 for the first three bands, nothing from 70; a month its instalment is missed gives no gain and does not count toward the 12; ends with portfolio +2 and network +5.
- `activity.bootcamp` ("A full-time bootcamp"): an entry test of tech 10 and English 55, judged at the start; 6 months holding the main slot, so starting it ends the job held, as a new job does (activity:new-job-replaces-held), and no job is held meanwhile; nothing paid now (activity:bootcamp-paid-after-a-job); +6 while starting (a working value), +5 while junior-ready up to 54, nothing from 55; ends with portfolio +3 and network +10, and for `tuning.activity.bootcampHireMonths` (6) months a hunt for `activity.job-junior-developer` adds `tuning.activity.bootcampHireBp` (2,000) to its chance (activity:job-hunt-lands-offer). Once a life.
- `activity.free-internship` ("An 8-week online internship"): free; needs tech 30; 2 months; +2 a month up to 69; ends with portfolio +1 and network +5.
From nothing to junior-ready while holding a job: the free path 10 months; the short course and then the free path 7, for ₦45,000 more; the diploma 8 (₦320,000 by then); the bootcamp 5, with no job. Real: AltSchool's nano-diploma ₦45,000 (Sep 2025) and its one-year diploma $290, or $500 paid after a job; Decagon's full-time bootcamp about ₦3,000,000 repaid after a job; an 8-week free online internship with a paid tier for hiring access (Sources).

**activity:bootcamp-paid-after-a-job** The bootcamp is paid after a job. Finishing it records what is owed on the slice (`bootcampOwed`, `tuning.activity.bootcampFee`, ₦3,000,000). The first developer job started after it (`activity.job-junior-developer`, `-mid-developer` or `-remote-developer`) opens `obligation.bootcamp` for that sum: owed, crediting nothing (obligation:loan-opening-credits-principal, as a car's shortfall is), 36 monthly charges with no interest, under `loan-repay`, a loan that stays open wherever the life goes (return:return-reopens-home-charges). A life that never holds a developer job owes nothing. Real: Decagon's ₦3,000,000, repaid over up to three years once the graduate is in a job, with boarding and a stipend in the course (2020, the last figure found); the game gives no stipend, so the six months are lived on savings.

## Effects and requirements this kind handles

The part before the slash names the kind that carries an effect out or judges a requirement (`engine.md` section 5). The types are in `packages/sim/src/engine/types.ts`.

| Type | | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `activity/exam` | effect | Counts the exam's answers given with a score above zero; at the pass mark the certificate is requested and the gain applied (activity:waiting-months-build-profile) |
| `activity/hunt` | effect | One month of looking for the job the slot's param names, on stream `job`; a hire opens the job offer |
| `activity/start-job` | effect | Starts the job named, or the job a term names, as the main or side job |
| `activity/end-job` | effect | Ends the main job, with `why` for the line; on a one-employer status it starts the clock (status:lost-job-starts-clock). With `side: true` it ends only the side job, as the flight does (activity:side-job-holds-a-slot, place:entries-legal-by-where); without it only the main job. |
| `activity/gig` | effect | Pays a gig between `min` and `max`, plus `perTech` for each point of tech, on stream `gig` |
| `job/any` | requirement | A main job is held |
| `job/none` | requirement | No main job is held |
| `job/is` | requirement | The main job is one of `activities` |
| `activity/tenure` | requirement | Months across the listed jobs, added together, are at least `months`; an empty list means every job |
| `activity/local-tenure` | requirement | Months in any job in the current country are at least `months` (ladders:local-tenure-counted) |
