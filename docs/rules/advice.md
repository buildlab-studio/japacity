# Finding out where you stand

> Rules for the four ways to look into a route and the agents who can dupe you. Rests on `route.md` (route:hearing-a-route, route:friend-tells-by-month and route:looking-into-makes-known: hearing and looking into), `offer.md` (offer:opening-fixes-terms-and-fraud, offer:fraud-effects-replace-ordinary, offer:fraud-hidden-signs-shown: terms, fraud effects, signs), `luck.md` (luck:luck-never-reads-money), `document.md`, `interview.md`, `person.md` and `world.md`. It duplicates nothing: the official page and the friend are the two ways the concept names; the adviser and the agents are this page's.
> Kinds: `route` (route:looking-into-makes-known), `offer` (the agent family), `activity` (the official page, the adviser, the friend), `document` (the genuine mark). No kind of its own.

A player who has heard of a route can find out more in four ways. Each trades money, time and risk. The free word from a friend stays the safety net, so nobody is ever stuck without a route.

## Rules: the four ways

**advice:four-ways-to-learn-route** Looking into a heard route is one of four acts, each a thing in a place the game already keeps (world:things-are-tappable-models): the official page, someone who went, a licensed adviser, an agent. Any of the four moves the route from heard to known (route:looking-into-makes-known). `activity.look-into-route` is the official page; the other three are the entries this page adds. Each look-into's line is keyed by its cause, so asking around at the motor park that moves a route to known is told as the drivers' word (`line.route.looked-into-asked`), never as a reading of the official page.

**advice:official-page-shows-checklist** The official page is free and answers now (activity:now-acts-and-month-acts), and the player never reads "slot" (text:bands-not-figures). It shows the route's true checklist (the requirements as the route entry holds them) and says nothing about the player's own chances. It is read at the hub's desk or on the phone's Japa screen.

**advice:someone-who-went-answers-free** Someone who went is free and costs no slot; they answer now, by `activity/now`, in the place (activity:now-acts-and-month-acts). It needs the relationship: a bond at `courting` or later with a person who is abroad, or `quality.network` at `tuning.advice.networkToAsk` or more, or a recurring person met in a place. They tell the checklist as it stood in the month they left. Their version is not guaranteed true: where the route's rules changed since (a route's `changedMonth`, moved by the era's variables), one item they name is wrong. Reading the official page corrects any wrong item the player holds from a friend or an agent (advice:official-page-shows-checklist).

**advice:licensed-adviser-is-truthful** The licensed adviser costs `tuning.advice.adviserFee` from the naira by transfer, the bank first (decision 0014), reason `fee-advice`, answers at once as a now-act once a month and fills no slot (activity:now-acts-and-month-acts), and is always truthful. They read the whole file and say, for every route heard or known: whether it fits now, what is missing, and the months away. Months away is the longest wait among the missing items (a document's ready months, a school's answer month, the window's next opening), given as "at least N months"; it is never a promise. Only the official page and the adviser give a checklist that is guaranteed true.

**advice:agent-kind-drawn-on-offer** An agent is an offer of the `agent` family: cheap and fast. Its kind is drawn when the offer opens on the `offer` stream as offer:opening-fixes-terms-and-fraud draws a fraud, at `tuning.advice.agentKindsBp` (fixer 3000, chaser 2000, overpromiser 3000, scammer 2000; the chaser is advice:chaser-runs-around-cheaper's). The fee never identifies the kind: each kind's fee is drawn from its range in `tuning.advice.fees`, and the ranges overlap, so a scammer can cost as much as a fixer or more. A fixer brings the next appointment or paper forward by `tuning.advice.fixerMonths`. An overpromiser promises a month it cannot keep, so nothing moves and the player learns it when the promised month passes. A scammer gives nothing and may offer fake papers for `tuning.advice.fakePapersFee` more. An overpromiser's or a scammer's version of the checklist understates what is needed; a fixer's is true.

**advice:scams-show-their-tells** A scam is never a dice roll the player could not see coming, and being careful is something the player does. Every agent entry carries the full list of tells in content (asks for cash only; no office, meets at the stop; guarantees the visa; wants to hold the passport; a price far below the official fees; pay today or the slot goes; no licence number; "no passport needed, we will arrange everything", carried by scammers only and found by checking the name: the Gulf file asks for the passport, chapter:chapters-follow-lean-route). The kind fixes which tells are there: a scammer carries at least `tuning.advice.scamTells`, an overpromiser at least `tuning.advice.overpromiseTells`, a fixer at most one. One tell shows at once on the sheet. The others are found by looking: three free acts on the sheet that cost no slot and always find what is there: ask for the office, ask for the licence number, check the name with someone the player knows (a bond, or `quality.network` at `tuning.advice.networkToAsk`). Each look uncovers every tell it would find, in words, before any fee. offer:fraud-hidden-signs-shown's chance draw does not apply to the agent family. An ask that may be a scam, any offer whose entry has a fraud share (the road's pitch, the message that says you have won, a sponsor that may not be one), shows every answer as a plain button and none as the main one, so the player reads before choosing; the answers that cost nothing come first, the way to say no before the way to pay. The agent's own sheet keeps its form: its three free looks come before any fee.

**advice:agents-never-read-money** Whether an agent appears, and which kind it is, never reads money in any form (luck:luck-never-reads-money stands). The offer's `when` reads routes, documents, the place and the chapter only. `checkContent` fails an agent entry that reads money.

**advice:agent-loss-is-the-fee** The loss is the fee, from the naira, by its size (decision 0014: cash first at or under `tuning.wallet.cashUpTo`, the bank first above it), and the fee can never take the last `tuning.advice.floorNaira` of the naira in cash and bank together: an agent's fee the player cannot cover leaves the offer declined by default. Fake papers, if the player uses them (chooses them for a route's requirement), set `genuine: false` on that document record; routes whose gates ask (`document/genuine` in `route-gates.md`) refuse the application and the genuineness interview reads the mark. The mark is never cleared. A fake paper that was never used in an application is replaced by requesting the genuine one, which replaces the record whole; once used, the record keeps the mark for the life. Nothing here ends a life, opens a debt, or takes a status.

**advice:bad-news-told-straight** Bad news is told straight: the report line names the agent, says the fee is gone, and says what the player saw ("The agent was a scam. ₦8,000 gone. He had no office and wanted cash."). No euphemism, no joke.

**advice:advisers-are-invented-people** Every adviser and agent is a `person` entry with an invented name from the name pool, drawn as recurring people are, never a real name or a real firm. Where they stand: the adviser at the hub's long desk; the official page at the hub's desk and in the phone; every agent at Ojuelegba's stop, where fixers, overpromisers and scammers all find their customers, and a fixer may also keep a desk in the agent's office. A scammer never has a desk: asking for his office (advice:scams-show-their-tells) gets a story, which is the tell. No new place.

**advice:free-ways-pointed-first** A new player is pointed at the free ways first. No agent offer opens before `chapter.find-your-feet` is done, and the chapter's "look into it" nudge names the official page and someone who went, never the adviser or an agent. The adviser's fee is above a starting life's cash by design.

**advice:chaser-runs-around-cheaper** The chaser. Beside the insider (the fixer of advice:agent-kind-drawn-on-offer, who moves a paper's date himself: faster, dearer), a second honest kind: the chaser follows up the school or the office and handles the running around, slower and cheaper. An agent offer drawn as a chaser (`tuning.advice.agentKindsBp.chaser`, taken from the fixer's share: fixer 3000, chaser 2000, overpromiser 3000, scammer 2000) brings the paper forward by one month for a fee from `tuning.advice.chaserFee` (₦8,000 to ₦25,000; the fee never tells the kind, so the ranges overlap), where the fixer brings it forward by two for his fee; its signs are the fixer's. Both are found through a person (a friend's chat line, asking around at the park), one per paper where it makes sense: the transcript, the police certificate, the TB test. Neither reads money (luck:luck-never-reads-money). Shown as a plain fact of how things get done, never as a crime and never with a lecture.
