# The world first

> Rests on `../design/07-the-world-first.md` (sections 2, 3 and 6), `city.md`, `text.md` and `engine.md`. city:places-change-no-state, city:place-shows-only-the-view and city:overheard-talk-from-view hold: the world sends no command of its own and shows only the view.
> Kind: none of its own. These are rules for the client's screens, the places' files and the content that ties the story to places. The simulation holds nothing for them.

The place fills the screen. Everything the player does, they do by tapping something that is there. The support pages live in a phone. The map is the city. The story leads, and there are no forms.

## Rules: the screen

**world:world-fills-the-screen** The world fills the screen. Over it sit only a top band, two thumb buttons at the bottom corners (Map at the left, Phone at the right), round and white with a picture each and in the centred column on a laptop, and a rise of japacity passing up for a moment (world:japacity-bag-by-door). Nothing sits under the band by default (world:nudge-says-one-thing). Nothing else is on screen until the player taps something. A sheet, the phone, the map and the results each open over the world and close back to it; the world stays visible behind a sheet. A moment (a chapter's card, a share card) may take most of the screen while the world stays visible and moving behind it, dimmed; one tap dismisses it, and sharing is offered, never required. Before the first month of a life is played, the current chapter opens over the dimmed world as chapter:first-chapter-mission-card says: its mission card, handed by its person, with Let's go.

**world:top-band-is-a-pill** The top band is a rounded pill, white by day and navy at night (decision 0025): the character's name first, with the age and the place's name small under it (a tap on the name or the age opens the data page, prologue:front-door-builds-a-person), the month's chip beside the name, and the money as a green counter that counts when it changes. The age is the character's age in years and months; twelve months make a birthday. The band carries no pages, no count and no Next month (decision 0011). A small chip beside the name says the world's part of the day in words (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night) from the clock view's `world` (clock:game-day-on-real-time), moving on the device each minute, never a clock time with digits; the month's name in it is screen-reader text only (month 0 is the calendar month the life began in, from the authority), and the month's spent time does not show. A tap on the chip opens the phone's Calendar app. Nothing on the band ends the month (world:sleep-ends-the-month). The status line beside the age says the body in words only when it complains ("Hungry" under thirty belly, "Tired" under thirty energy, "Hungry, tired" for both; quality:body-belly-and-energy), never a figure.

No running month number shows anywhere a player sees: not on the phone, the results, an ask's deadline, a slip or a chat heading. A deadline or a wait reads as months away ("in 2 months", "next month"); a past month reads as months ago. The band's chip under it says the soonest of what is coming; a tap on it opens the phone's Calendar app on its coming list, soonest first, each as months away: an ask's deadline, a paper on its way, a visa decision, the next birthday, and a route's age limit once it is within three years (only dates the player can act on or waits on, never the whole calendar). Each row opens where it is dealt with (a paper or a wait in Japa, a due in Bills), and an ask names the place it is answered at. The phone's wallpaper shows the real date and time, since the world runs on the real calendar. The results sheet leads as world:month-end-results-sheet says, with the age small beside it, and on the twelfth turn it says the character turned a year older.

The band is one slim row; a handle under it opens a second row with the nudge and what is coming soonest, and closed, that row peeks for a few seconds whenever its words change. The handle shows only while something is coming; the nudge line needs no handle. The band never shows more than those. A tap on the place's name does nothing. Words fit their boxes and labels never cover each other: the band says a long place by its own short name (`<place>.short`), the ride's title wraps to a second line, the page's chips wrap, and the things' labels over a place are lifted clear of one another. The place line for a home names its kind in few words, short enough to stand whole beside the age, never cut with an ellipsis, each at most 24 letters: "Your room, Ojuelegba" for the room in a compound, "Your hostel room, Yaba", "The family house", "The room on the water" for a life that lives in Makoko, and abroad the room by its country's short name (world:painted-maps-abroad): "The shared room, Peckham" in London, by its hub's short name, "The basement, Calgary", "The bed space, Dubai" and "The shared flat, Castel". Nothing anywhere says "slots", "3 of 3 things planned" or "takes no slot".

Anything the game opens by itself comes only as the answer to the player's own tap, never unprompted and never over a walk, a moment or a sheet, and a teach is one small card the player dismisses; the same measure holds for every screen that opens by itself.

**world:nudge-says-one-thing** The nudge says one thing, the next thing to notice, chosen in this order from the view: an open set piece or interview question; an open offer; the chapter's next undone step, in its own nudge words; a report line waiting; else the month's end. It is one sentence of at most eight words, from content, and it is never two lines. Nothing sits under the band by default: an ask waiting, a due abroad and the night's bed keep their line there; the report and the month's end wait on the phone. The journey's step shows in the world only in the moment: for about two seconds after a brief is handed (the chip under the band with the brief's picture and title, then it slides into the Phone thumb, whose Japa icon takes a dot until the app opens) and while the player is at the step's place or beside its person (the chip with its three dots, the marker of world:things-are-tappable-models on the thing or person; a done thing ticks its dot with a pop and the giver's toast for three seconds); leaving the place removes it. The chip says which brief, the marker says what to do. While the first brief's card or a chapter's close stands, the band's second row waits: no chip and no nudge under the band say what the card already says; the chip comes once Let's go has carried the brief. The briefs' words (the title with a wink, the giver and where they stand, the rewards, the two-word place under each thing, the toasts) live in content beside each chapter (`chapter.<id>.brief.*`, with `chapter.brief.*` shared), read by the client; the interface keeps only its own words.

The nudge hides while a sheet, the phone, the map or a thing's fan is open, and comes back by itself when it closes, whether by a tap anywhere outside it, its sheet's close or Back (Escape), never needing a tap on the floor; a tap on the player's own figure opens nothing and the nudge stays. A tap on the nudge opens the way to what it names: the asks when a question or an offer waits; for the chapter's step, the acts of the thing that does it when that thing stands in this place, the map with its stop picked when it is elsewhere in the city, and the Calendar app when the step is the month's end (the step's places are the interface's table in `nudge.ts`); the thing the nudge points at in the place pulses gently at its mark (world:things-are-tappable-models). The step's marker over the thing is itself a tap target: its pill picks the thing as the chip's tap does (`followStep`), so a person standing in front of the thing never takes the tap meant for it. The first chapter's "Look into it" names and opens the page of the life's own way out once it is heard of (a trade's Gulf, the first meetup's fit for a degree, guide:first-meetup-hears-fitting-route), so the chapters follow that way (chapter:chapters-follow-lean-route). A report line waiting opens the phone's News. Go on the opening words and Go on the chapter's sheet take the step's way, as a tap on the nudge does.

A step already started names the wait instead of the thing to do again: a paper on its way says when it comes, a school applied to says when it answers, a hunt or a test planned says to end the month, a paper or an application planned this month says so (`.planned`), and funds short of the line say how much more to show (the story's `.started`, `.planned` and `.short` words for the step). A step the player cannot afford names the gap and a way to the money (a better job, a gig, the family) instead of the thing again; its tap opens the jobs board, where the money is; once a hunt for better pay is planned it says so, and once the job's pay covers the gap within a month it says to work and save. Every step whose act costs naira has its own gap and payday words, so a step the naira held cannot pay is never nudged as the act but as the shortfall, the board its way. While a paper is on its way, a better-paid job on the board comes before "work and save", and so does the life's own waiting-month act (activity:waiting-months-build-profile). Once News has been read for the month's report, "the month's news is in" goes. A hunt planned, abroad or while a job is held, says the application is in. A hunt missed last month with other jobs on the board names another way. A job a way out counts the months of (the nurse's, route-gates:care-route-needs-verification) is never nudged away from, and a job offer says what taking another ends. With no chapter step left, the nearest open goal's first act is the nudge (goal:view-shows-goal-items). When a tuition instalment was already missed and the next is not covered, the dues come before any ask and say the visa is at stake. The plane blocked by held funds says what blocks it and goes to the bank's counter.

Abroad, whenever the month's dues in the country's money pass that money held, the gap comes before the step, every month: the report nudge names the gap and opens the Bank at the change. The dues abroad count the month's pay coming in before the bills. Abroad, a step with no place in the world is a line only, and a step's way opens as at home: the map with the stop picked.

When the money held where the character is (the naira at home, the country's money abroad, cash and bank together) cannot pay the cheapest ride this month (at home the cheapest way's flat fare under the month's fares; abroad the cheapest of the country's ways' flat fares, nothing in a month the pass covers a way or at a stop the employer's shuttle serves) and the step's stop is too far to walk (abroad by the country's own `lonMetresPerE3`), the nudge names a free step instead, in this order: walking home whenever home is not where the character stands, however far; the racks for a life in Makoko with no job; a job on a board within walking distance; its tap opens that way. At home with none of these, the step itself is named, since the long walk out of home reaches it (place:rides-between-city-places); "End the month" is the free step only for a life with no home to walk to. The ride sheet opens on the first way whose fare the money held pays, so with no fare a stop on foot opens on Walk.

A step whose act is a call or a message (the transcript, the degree assessment, the council's verification, the licence's renewal) says "on your phone" in its nudge words, and a tap opens the phone's Papers app on that act's card wherever the player stands; the passport's step names the passport office in Ikoyi and goes there as any visit. A step shut by a need names that need's own act and opens it: the coding course at the hub's desk for tech, the portfolio's work there for the pieces, the hub's meetup for the people who will write (`<step>.needs.<quality>`); "Apply for the endorsement" opens the endorsement's own card at the desk, and the meetup's step opens the meetup's own card, one tap. A need whose act is already in the month's plan or carries on in it ("Ongoing") is passed over for the next need, and with every need's act running the step says the plan is set (`.needs.planned`), its tap the Calendar.

The care file: the nurse's certificate counts on its checklist (route-gates:care-route-needs-verification), so it is a waiting-month act. In the months on the ward it comes first, and while a paper is on its way it comes before the paper's wait, named only while the naira held pays it, else its gap and payday words (`ui.nudge.meanwhile.nurse-certification.short`, `.saving`); its tap opens the certification's card on the clinic's notice, one tap. The TB test is never pointed at before the file's other steps are met: at the care application and the care decision day it comes after the passport, the English test, the police paper, the sponsor at the bar and the window, and at the talent decision day after the passport it needs. The care file's sponsor step reads the window: while health care is shut to applicants abroad no sponsor takes a nurse, so the nudge says so ("Care sponsors are closed: keep the file ready") and its tap goes to the file's own next paper that can be got now, the passport, then the English test (never the decision day's TB test or police paper: both run out, so they are taken last, and their visa centre opens on the map with the decision day, world:map-is-painted-lagos), else to the certification's card while she can pay it, else to the Calendar; once the window opens the sponsor step reads as any other. The months until it opens are not said: the client is sent the world of the current tick only (clock:client-gets-current-world-only), and online a tick is not a month.

The talent file: its nudges follow the order its papers must be met and paid for. The passport comes first, at its office, in any step of the file while it is neither held nor on its way; while it is on its way the file's own work goes on (the portfolio, the endorsement's needs) and a step waits on it only when nothing else of the file can be done. The endorsement is named only once the passport is held and the naira held pays, at the bank now with its charge, the endorsement, the TB test and the application together, so both can follow within the endorsement's three months (route-gates:talent-visa-needs-endorsement); short of that, the gap ("Find ₦7,653,382 more before the endorsement", the board its way). With the endorsement held, the TB test, then the application, short of its fee the gap. A step whose act the news has shut (the route's window closed, route:closed-window-blocks-applying) says so plainly, never "needs more": "Every route is closed for now" while every route with a window is closed (the pandemic), else that way's own words ("The talent route is closed for now"); its tap opens Japa, which says the same and that the news says when it opens, so no desk is pointed at for an act that cannot be taken.

**world:thumb-buttons-carry-dots** The Phone button carries a dot when an app has something new (world:phone-rises-over-scene). The Map button never carries a dot. Both hide while riding and while the results show. A wide home adds a round button over Phone, "See another side", which turns the house a quarter (world:room-seen-down-its-length); it steps back while a sheet, the phone, the map, a fan, the sleep or the home's card is up.

### Why both screens

The game is built for a phone and a desktop alike. In September 2026 phones were 75% of web traffic in Nigeria and desktops 24%; in the United Kingdom, where many of the people this game is about live, desktops were 42% ([StatCounter, Nigeria](https://gs.statcounter.com/platform-market-share/desktop-mobile-tablet/nigeria), [United Kingdom](https://gs.statcounter.com/platform-market-share/desktop-mobile-tablet/united-kingdom)). The browser tests run at both sizes.

## Rules: things, acts and sheets

**world:things-are-tappable-models** A thing is a model copy in a place that the player can tap, declared in the place's file with its label and the entries it stages. A tap on a thing, or on its mark, opens its fan (world:fan-of-sticker-buttons), never a chip. An act with no cost and no story just happens (sit), and an act with a pose is seen on the thing at once: the character sits on the chair, lies on the bed. Rest is no act: the bed and the couch offer Lie down and the chair Sit, done at once with the pose and no slot, since an empty slot already rests (turn:free-slots-per-month). An act that costs money or moves the story opens its sheet, whose one button says the act itself, never "add to month". A job on a place's board or stall is an act whenever the hunt lists it, a job held or not (the board stays once hired and its card says why a hunt is shut), and its sheet is the hunt's card with that job named; held funds still open are paid back at the bank's counter through the command act `obligation/pay`; a choice inside an activity that no thing has made (which job, which school) opens the picker, never a card that takes the first; the kiosk's "Buy a ticket" makes its choice, one ticket, so its slip opens, and more tickets are picked on the slip, sold at once (activity:now-acts-and-month-acts). The street kiosk is the one thing whose sheet changes with the world: online it sells the Friday draw (lottery:draw-runs-online-only). The papers box has one act, Look in the box, which opens the phone's Papers app (world:phone-holds-what-phones-hold) and tells no line in the corner.

A thing shows no label by default: each interactive thing carries a quiet mark at its anchor, a small still dot (6 px, the theme's signal colour at about 60%), and the thing the nudge points at pulses gently. A thing's dot stays on its thing, whatever names show; only a name shown over it moves, lifted to stand clear of the other names and of every dot, at most two rows over its own dot, and a name with no room within two rows waits unshown while its dot stays, so no name floats into the sky over a place seen whole. A person keeps a name over the dot only while holding an ask or as the nudge's target; otherwise the dot, like a thing. The first time a place is entered in a life, every name shows for two seconds and fades, once per place per life, kept on the device beside the save. The step's thing or person carries the marker: a white pill with two words and an arrow, a soft bounce and a yellow ring at the feet, never on the joint, the barber, the suya man or the club. A month-act started at a thing carries on (world:month-acts-carry-on): the planned act's Ongoing and its Stop, and the spent words, sit on the fan's buttons, and the thing's name says "Ongoing" whenever the name shows. A tap on the player's own figure opens nothing, no card (the data page is on the band's name, world:top-band-is-a-pill). A person's or a shopfront's card closes on a tap anywhere outside it or Back (Escape), and the nudge comes back by itself; it sits at the foot of the screen as the sheets do, never under the band.

People speak where they stand and nothing narrates over the world. A person's words float in a bubble over that person in the scene, off-white with their name small above the words (Mama's "You have eaten? Sit small.", the agent's "Not today.", the visa officer's "Nothing for you today.", Mama's "God bless you. Light is back." when the light bill is paid at home): the bubble follows the camera, is held inside the screen and under the band, hides while the person is behind the camera, shows one speaker at a time for about four seconds (world:street-talk-budget), and goes on a tap. What an act does to the world is told in the bottom corner (world:street-talk-budget) in one short line in the third person about the world ("Mama presses a bag of oranges on you.", "In the box: the passport.", "The parcel is yours."), never the player's own act narrated, and a told line stays about 7 seconds, so a line of two sentences can be read. An act the scene shows (sit, rest, cook, fetch water) gets no line at all; a planned act is its thing's state, never a line. No card stands across the middle of the world to tell what happened or what someone said. A toast stands under the band for about three seconds and goes: money in from the player's own act (its picture, the act and the figure on a green pill, a coin flying to the band's counter; `MoneyToast`) and the interview's close (`SaidToast`). The brief's giver speaks through the brief's chip (world:nudge-says-one-thing), and the market's delivery is a note at the top of the Market app (world:phone-rises-over-scene). Money going out is the price the player tapped and has no toast; a stop reached or a refusal ("Your free time this month is all planned") is a line told in the corner.

The room's things carry the acts that answer at once (activity:acts-may-repeat-in-month, decision 0011): the cooking corner's meals, a fan button each (world:fan-of-sticker-buttons), the drum's water and bath, the bed's nap, a TV on its stand, Mama's gist and her shop, the table's study, course and portfolio; every repeat act is staged at the family house and at the home lived in (the interface's `byHandAt`). Cooking and the water answer at once (obligation:cooking-and-fetching-water). Each answers where it stands: steam, drops off the drum, the screen's flicker, the lantern turning up, the figure lying down, a few seconds each, growing in and out on scale only; a repeat act's line is told every time, Mama's over her head. A thing's act may keep hours, `parts` naming the parts of the world's day it is open in (clock:game-day-on-real-time, decision 0012, streets:lagos-street-spending); outside them the act is not offered, and a thing left with no act keeps one shut button on its fan saying when it opens ("Opens in the evening") whose tap tells why in the corner, in the thing's words, so the viewing centre and the joint open in the evening and at night and the street has a shape. The sun and the sky light of every place follow the world's clock (clock:game-day-on-real-time): a low warm sun in the morning, full light in the afternoon, an orange sun low on the other side in the evening, a navy sky at night with the bulb and the lantern as warm points (`stage.light.lamps`), eased over about 600 ms; `?clock=morning|afternoon|evening|night` pins the part in development only.

**world:one-action-one-sheet** One action, one sheet. A row in a list says its name and one figure on a line that never wraps; what else there is to know folds behind a tap on the row (the needs of a job out of reach, the details of a bill), so no sheet is a wall of words and the words come only when asked. An ask that belongs at a place (the family's send-off at the house, chapter:family-marks-the-grant) is answered there: away from it the asks sheet says someone is waiting at that place and its one button goes there, the map with the stop picked. A sheet takes the form of what it shows: a card for an ask or a choice, or a paper slip with a torn edge for a paper thing (a bill, a ticket, a receipt). An ask shows the asker's name pill and mark, a title in their voice, one line, and the replies as rows with the cost on them. Every sheet has one main button, and a way to say not now. An ask that may be a scam has no main button; its answers are plain, the free ones first (advice:scams-show-their-tells). The sheet rises from the bottom; the top of the scene stays in view. The words come from content. The month itself is no sheet: it lives in the phone's Calendar app (world:top-band-is-a-pill, world:month-acts-carry-on).

**world:back-closes-topmost** The browser's Back (a phone's back button or gesture) and the Escape key close the topmost thing the player opened, one at a time: a sheet, an app on the phone and then the phone, the map, a ride's sheet before the ride starts, and a step of the start screen, the age screen or the door. Each opening adds one entry to the browser's history and each close by the game's own control takes it off again, so Back never steps through things already closed. With nothing open in the world, Back leaves the game as it leaves any site. A ride under way, the cut into a place and the send-off's beats are not closed by Back; Back there waits until the beat ends.

**world:sheets-one-frame** Every sheet has one frame: a handle that closes it when dragged down past a third of its height or flicked down, and one close button at its top right with a hit area of at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels; tapping the handle does nothing else. On a laptop every sheet rises in the centre.

**world:lazy-part-fails-softly** A part of the client loaded on first use (the map, the bag, the send-off, the maps abroad and any other) that fails to load leaves the world up and shows the connection card with Retry, which loads that part again; the broken-screen card is kept for real faults. Once the world is up, those parts are fetched ahead while the connection is idle, and when the connection comes back a request that failed for it is tried again once.

**world:reach-and-read** Every control a player taps has a hit area of at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels, made with padding where the drawn shape is smaller. Text, and any line that carries meaning (a refusal, a free or taken name, a cannot-pay), meets a contrast of 4.5 to 1 against its ground by day and by night; a control's edge or icon meets 3 to 1. Text is at least 12 CSS pixels. Keyboard focus shows a ring at least 2 px wide in the game's own colours. Under an open sheet or the start screen the scene behind is inert: no Tab stop and no tap. Under Reduce Motion a bar that shows time passing steps once a second instead of standing still or jumping to full, and nothing turns on its own.

**world:costly-acts-confirmed** An act that throws away something the player paid for or cannot get back asks once more and names what is lost: turning back from a ride (its fare), removing a passkey (and that phones signed in with it are signed out), and leaving a guest life. The safe choice is the main button; the costly one wears the warning colour, never the main yellow, and sits apart from it. Starting a new life asks for a press and hold (account:start-a-new-life).

A priced act's button says the act and its price ("Indomie and egg · ₦1,500"); under it stand the balance before, struck through, and the balance after beside it, nothing moving until the tap. No act, ride or the kiosk says "₦X left"; "₦X short" is the one exception: when the cost cannot be paid from the naira in cash and bank less what the month's plan already pays for, the main button is shut and says how much is short. The balance after a fee in another money is the naira the band reads after it, the cash counted, though only the bank buys the foreign money. The phone's own asks (change money, send money, a bill) keep their words. The main button's second line says "Now" for an act that happens at once (a ride, the deposit, the plane), or that it counts when the month ends. A reason from the world that stops the act (held funds open before a flight) shuts the button, which says only "Not yet", and the reason stands once on the card. The sheet says the time the act takes in words (activity:now-acts-spend-half-days) and what it does in words ("fills you", "rests you", "lifts you", "+2 English"). Chips earn their place: a price shows only where there is one; Free only on an act a player would expect to pay for, never on sleep, a bath or water, and never beside a price; the time only when it is more than a morning; at most two effect chips.

The English band a sheet or a result names is the plan's route's own, read from that route's content (its file's English item: health care and the skilled worker 5.5, which the degree's English statement also meets, and Canada 6.0), so the file, the centre's "Sit the test" sheet and the result ("That meets the route's 5.5.") say one figure; with no plan, or a plan that asks no band (the master's, whose English is the degree, route:degree-meets-masters-english), the sheet and the result say the band alone and name no route's figure. The asks sheet's count is the asks still waiting, "1 of 3" over three, and with one left there is no count. Over a set piece (the exam, the visa interview) the asks sheet's header holds no count: the set piece counts its own questions ("Question 1 of 3"), so one counter stands, and the header says where the player stands.

**world:month-acts-carry-on** The month plan has no page and no sheet. A month-act the player starts from its thing (a course, study, the portfolio, practice) carries on every month until the player stops it, from the Stop on its thing's fan or from the Calendar app, or until it ends itself: the view no longer allows it or it can no longer be paid (a course finished, a way shut), or it was the search for a job now held. The client keeps these commitments across the month's end, every pick still allowed and paid for that is not a now-act, and sends them with each month's `turn/end`; the simulation keeps no plan of its own. An exam is one sitting: an act with an exam on its entry (the nurse's certification) leaves the plan the month it runs, pass or fail, so it never carries on; a failed exam is sat again, and paid again, only by a new tap on its card. Every other act, `once` or not, carries on (`once` is at most once a month, activity:waiting-months-build-profile: the master's at home runs twelve months of one slot). The free time a life has is the month's half-days (turn:month-lived-in-half-days, decision 0011), spent by hand. The Calendar app shows the job, each commitment that carries on with its Stop, the chores done this month and what is coming; it has no free-time rows and no Next month.

**world:month-end-results-sheet** The month's end opens one results sheet over the scene, whichever way the month was ended (world:sleep-ends-the-month). It is short, up to six lines in the room: payday (the salary line) when there is one, rent when due, the moments in the story's order, then Kept. An ask that came up as the day was lived (decision 0012) is not told again at the close, so a quiet month may close in three. There is no "Done this month" for an act that answered at once, no living line at home, and no list of every naira in and out (News and the Bank keep those). The results lead with the best thing that happened (payday or the best moment) as a card with its picture: when the month's lines hold the visa's answer (the line that leads alone, `leadsAlone`), the moments stand before payday and the rent, the answer first as the hero; otherwise payday leads. Money in comes before money out, rent as a chip, three moments shown and the rest behind See the whole month; a loss in quiet ink, never the loudest thing. The month's events come before its chores (food cooked, water fetched). The results tell a stamp reached right after the visa granted line (wallet:savings-stamps). The draw's numbers show with any matched one lit, then the savings bar toward the move, then one button that returns the player to where they are. The morning's first result is a white card on the lock screen with the others under Also this morning.

Kept is counted in every money that moved. A change of money counts for nothing either way (decision 0024): the kept figure leaves out the change's two sides, and the bank's charge stays money out. Each change of money in the month has its own line under Kept, in the order made, both sides as they were, saying what went at the rate, the charge not in it ("Changed ₦1,022,058 into £561": naira into pounds or dollars, or back; with the FX man what was handed over), quiet and plain, never like a bank alert (`text.md`); it takes the place of the plain "changed" tag, so the change is said once (text:show-then-tell). The bank's charge is counted once, as its own money line, money out, in the results' words ("The bank's charge for the change: ₦15,331."), so Kept plus each change is the band's move (for example Kept +₦69,336, a change of ₦1,022,058, the band down ₦952,722). A charge too small to show as a figure has no line, and Kept still counts it. The wallet's month net (wallet:months-kept-counted) counts the charge once, and so does the client's telling of a change (`shared/words/money.ts`). A purchase whose foreign side waits in the bank's queue is told by its own line the month it is paid in and has no changed-into line, since only one side is on that month's lines. All of this is read from the report lines; the simulation and the view hold nothing more for it.

A paper asked for whose result is out the same month (a test's band) is told once, as out, never "on the way" beside it. A month a lotto ticket was bought says the draw's outcome in words, never only the ticket's tag: the kiosk's draw among the month's events, after them and before the chores, never cut by a full month (luck:kiosk-sells-lotto-tickets), and the Friday draw's "Your ticket did not win." under its numbers the month its result comes (lottery:draw-result-on-front-page). An offer's answer whose effects move a quality says what came of it in one line.

The month's report lines stay in the phone's News app afterwards. News orders money that went unpaid first, as the results do. News tells the month in the results' own words, every line the results tell and none it contradicts; a paper a route judges says only when the answer comes, never that it should be ready. News never lists the years before's lines (a grade, a quality up or down, their money); the years are told once, on the page.

## Rules: the camera and the place

**world:camera-always-raised** The camera is always raised, never at eye level and never straight down. One finger turns the view within the place's limit. The pinch zooms, and on release the zoom snaps to one of two states: the whole place, or close on the character; for a wide home the close state is a room, not the character. When close, a chip offers "See the whole place". It stands only while the world is free: it steps back under a card, a sheet, a share card, the chapter's close, the first brief's card, a fan, the first-night card and the sleep, and while a line of the player's own stands in the corner, so it never shares the foot with that line. Every place file declares what the whole view looks at, how wide it must be, its pitch far and near, and its turn limit, so the whole place fits whatever the screen's shape. A place file whose turn limit is 360 turns the full circle and rests on quarters (the wide homes); there a vertical drag is a second one-finger gesture, tilting toward the view from above, still raised and never straight down. Entering a place always shows the whole of it.

**world:nothing-roams** Nothing roams. A tap on a thing walks the character to it in a straight line and the act follows: the walk takes about one second whatever the distance, a second tap ends it at once, and nothing between a tap and its result takes longer than about a second and a half. A fan button's act follows once the character has walked to the thing, whatever its kind. A tap on the ground walks there only inside the place's walkable polygon, with no route round obstacles. Route-finding and strolling sit behind `tuning.city.roam`, which is false (city:tap-to-walk).

**world:place-is-one-screen** A place is one screen. An outdoor place is a frontage of about 24 metres; an indoor place is a room of about 16 by 9 metres seen down its length (world:room-seen-down-its-length), so it fills a portrait screen, with its two far walls and no roof. That size does not bind a home: a wide home is an exterior place of about 13 by 13 metres (the family house, with its yard and carport) or an interior of about 9 by 8 (the compound room's house, the hostel), seen whole as world:room-seen-down-its-length says. A wide home's near walls stand knee-high (0.6 m) and its yard fences 2.1 m, each outer side a full and a low wall of which one shows (world:room-seen-down-its-length). People: at most 6 outdoors and 4 indoors in low mode, 10 and 6 in high; the send-off party keeps its ten and its dancers. Moving sheets: at most one per place, none in low mode. The place check enforces these and the sizes. The Inverter Room (`place.yaba-hub`) is dressed as its own room and not the hub's: rows of small laptop desks with their stools on a grey floor, the inverter with its two batteries on the side wall, a standing fan, the chair, the notices and the door, so the hub and the Inverter Room are two pictures.

**world:faces-never-share-height** No two horizontal faces in a place stand within one centimetre of the same height; anything laid over the ground sits above it or draws with a depth offset. The shadow camera covers only the place, with a bias and a normal bias set, and reaches about half the scene's own floor plus a margin (never less than 8 m either side, never past the city's 24), since a wide home's floor is wider than a place's width. The place check fails a file that breaks the first sentence.

**world:entering-is-a-cut** Entering a building is a cut: the screen goes dark for under half a second and the room opens as its own scene. "Step outside" cuts back. The same cut carries every scene change except the ride.

**world:opens-where-last-stopped** The game opens where the player last stopped, or at home. Where the player last stopped includes a room: a reload made inside opens inside, not outside; only a move to another place steps back outside. Home is per country: the family house in Nigeria, the room abroad once the character lands. The family house is a place on the map with a position; it has no inside: it is one wide house seen whole from above, and a reload at home opens on the house whole. The people of the story (the partner, a friend, the family) have homes that are places the player can visit. The family house stays on the map for every life in Nigeria, home or not, a little from the home spot, so the people met there (world:nobody-picked-from-list) can be reached from a room in a compound or in Makoko.

## Rules: look, sound and motion

**world:colours-are-role-tokens** Every interface colour is a role token; a component names a role, never a colour. The look is one system with two paints (decision 0025, `../design/09-the-games-look.md`): the day paint (a pale sky sheet, white cards, navy ink and edges, the danfo yellow action with a navy underside, soft tints by kind of act) on the root, and the night paint (navy sheets, cream ink and edges, the action unchanged) under `data-paint` night, driven by the world's clock (clock:game-day-on-real-time; `shared/world/paint.ts`, `NIGHT_PAINT_LIVE`); the development switch `?paint=night` also shows it. The two paints are the only themes, and Settings offers no choice of look. The 3D world keeps its own colours. Yellow is the action and the signal and never fills a band. Offers and the hunt's jobs are picture cards with the pick ringed in yellow. The adire circle pattern is drawn on the data page, the wordmark, the door's and the prologue's headers and the moment card, and on nothing else. The Danfo display face is on the seal and the share cards alone; every other mounted surface sets Rubik.

Things drawn round are few: a dot, a person's avatar, and the look's round parts (the thumbs, the fan's buttons, the stickers' pins, the marker's ring). The look's own pills (`.pill`, `.sticker-chip`, the face mark) are round by design and are not counted against them, and the front door's round header buttons, its colour swatches and the page's round stamp are the exception. A thing's mark is one of the few round things, a 6 px dot in the signal colour at about 60% with a hairline; what it opens keeps its role colours.

Some pictures keep their own colours, as the 3D world does. The two lights over the start screen (account:two-lights-and-wink-lines) are a picture of everyone, not the interface: they alone may draw soft gradients, two of them, the dusk across the top and the glow of each bulb, and the palette's test counts them apart. The third light, a player's own light beside it and the dedication page's light (account:dedication-page-and-its-doors, account:name-a-light-for-yours) glow with the same fill, read from the two lights' file; no other gradient is drawn for them. The dedication page's picture (account:dedication-page-and-its-doors) uses literal colours like the two lights and the paper map: the sky `#172647`, the water `#111d38`, the boxes `#24345a`, `#3b4c77`, `#c8c3b4` and `#8e97ab`; it adds no gradient. The drawn map's water (`--water`) is the lagoon tone `#5f9fc0` in both paints, so the lagoon reads as water wherever the map draws it, the start screen included, never as the bands' navy. Google's own mark on its sign-in button (`apps/client/public/brand/google-g.svg`, drawn as Google ships it, with its gradient) is the one outside mark the palette rule does not touch, since Google's branding guideline requires it unchanged.

**world:interface-sounds-made-live** Interface sounds are small cues made on the fly, no files: a tap, a coin, a confirm, a whoosh, the cues of world:motions-under-half-second, and a `pop` for a thing lifted in buy mode. Mute in Settings silences them; muted fetches nothing.

**world:motions-under-half-second** Every motion of the interface takes under half a second: sheets rise, chips pop, the phone rises tilted, the money counts. The one motion allowed over half a second is the plane's lift of world:plane-cut-at-send-off, about three seconds. Reduced-motion settings cut them to a fade. Wherever it costs nothing, a visible state change is smooth, never abrupt: a sheet, a card, the phone or a folded row opening, closing, appearing or expanding moves by a plain CSS transition on transform and opacity only (the compositor's), 150 to 250 ms, never on a layout property and never by a render loop; the shared sheets and cards carry it (`index.css` `.motion-*`, `Sheet`, `WorldSheet`, `AskSheet`, `FullScreen`, the moment card, every `<details>`), and under reduced motion each snaps. The phone grows and shrinks as one transform from the box it left to the box it is laid out in (measured once, FLIP), its content hidden in flight and faded in once the frame is in place, and it is put away by dropping back the way it rose; nothing under it is laid out again. The look's motions, the press squash (120 ms), the wobble in (260 ms), the pop (260 ms) and the shake (280 ms, three times each way), are transform and opacity only and cut to a fade under reduced motion; the cues `ding`, `thud` and `no` are in the set, each under 300 ms (decision 0025).

Reduced motion reaches the camera too. When the phone asks for reduced motion (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, read by `motionReduced` in `shared/ui/motion.ts` and followed while the game is open), the camera's own moves change at once instead of easing: a wide home's quarter turn, its tilt, the ease in on a room and back out to the whole house (`easeRoomView`, the rate `ROOM_EASE`), every place's zoom and the street's rise to the plan view; a finger turning or tilting still moves the view live under it, and the snap on release is at once. Where the page cannot answer the query the camera eases.

**world:no-classic-view-kept** Every address opens the world, `?classic` among them, and the shell has no month page. Rule tests run against the world and the phone. The client keeps no view it does not mount: the month's facts are on the phone's apps (world:phone-holds-what-phones-hold), and nothing counts the months held (clock:no-month-countdown-shown).

**world:sleep-ends-the-month** The bed is a thing. Its fan buttons are Nap (`activity.nap`, a sheet) or Lie down, and Sleep. Lie down is done at once with the lying pose and no slot, since an empty slot already rests (turn:free-slots-per-month); while no nap can be had, Lie down stands in Nap's place (the contract's `otherwise`). Sleep carries the sleep glyph and no price. There is no automatic turn (decision 0011): night falls on the world's clock and, when the body is tired (energy under thirty, the band's "Tired", quality:body-belly-and-energy), the nudge says "Night. Go to bed" unless a question or a person asking waits; a night the body can still take keeps the nudge as it was. The bed's Sleep is how the month turns, at any hour. The simulation has no command and no rule of its own for it. Sleep is a timed act, about thirty-six seconds (`SLEEP_MS`; `?sleep=<seconds>` in development): the figure lies down, the room takes a soft dark wash, the moon chip under the band says "Asleep · tap to wake" with a thin bar running down, a large Wake up sits above the Phone thumb, and a small mark floats over the bed. While the sleep runs the others in the place are not seen under the wash (the crowd, the dancers and those standing at a thing of their own); the sleeper alone stays, and waking, or the sleep running out, brings them back. When the sleep runs out the month turns (`turn/end` once, with the commitments as they stand; the time left is rested, as an empty slot rests) and the results sheet opens in the room (world:month-end-results-sheet). Waking early, from the chip or the button, turns no month and is a nap (`activity.nap` when allowed), with "Up again. The night is still yours." in the corner in the evening and at night, and "Up again." by day. The first time a life sleeps, one small card, the first-night card, says "Each night you sleep is a month of your life." before the first sleep, with Sleep and Not yet, once per life. An act whose body is spent says "You are spent. Sleep." where its button was (activity:spent-body-refuses-acts). A sleep never moves the world's clock.

**world:street-talk-budget** Street talk follows the talk budget (decision 0025, `../design/09-the-games-look.md` section 5). Pictures over people carry the ambient life: emotes over at most two residents at a time, turning every few seconds. An emote over a resident is a tap target, and its tap is a tap on that person, so their line comes up as a tap on their figure would bring it. Words over a person come only when that person is near the player's figure (within about 7 m, `NEAR_M`, as every voice): at most one bubble, at most eight words, about four seconds, then a quiet of thirty seconds between overheard lines (`QUIET_MS`). A long line shows its words too, clipped to two lines in the bubble, and a tap opens it whole in a sheet with the name. At most three callouts stand over the scene (the fan, the chip, the marker first; the bubble waits). Nothing speaks in the first three seconds in a new place, nor over a sheet, the phone or the map. There is no corner chatter tied to nobody: the bottom corner holds only the player's own act's answer, one line at a time, a told line staying about 7 seconds (world:things-are-tappable-models).

Lines and the speech bubble are off-white with dark text by day and by night, lighter than the band, with a soft shadow and a hairline. While a sheet, the phone, the map, a card or the results cover the world the corner and the bubble hide. Over the phone a told line ("You quit", "Money changed") stands in a lane of its own just above the handset, below the band, never inside the glass on an app's icon or button; grown, the handset leaves that lane's room above it. The bubble divides the words from the corner: a person's own words go over their head; what happens goes in the corner. The corner keeps out of the thumbs' columns, so the turn button never stands on its line.

A line overheard that one person plainly says names them. Content gives the speaker beside the line, `<place>.overheard.<n>.by` in `place.text.ts`, a person's name of at most two words ("Mama", "Your brother", "Conductor", "Trader"), never an engine word; `overheard.ts` reads it and the bubble says that name in place of Someone nearby or Someone in the house, and where that person stands in the place as a thing (Mama at the family house) the words float over them when near, only while that thing is within about 7 m of the player's figure, else the line is held. A line with no `.by` keeps the house's or the street's voice over the resident nearest; at home the overheard voice is Someone in the house, not a stranger's. The family house's three are named (the gate line a brother's, the uncle line Mama's, the pot line a sister's), the hostel's corridor where one person speaks, and the street's conductors calling a stop and sellers calling their goods. A place's own lines name what only that place has, so none reads as another kind of home or place: the room in a compound hears the compound's sweeping rota, next door, its one tap for ten rooms and the landlord; the family house hears the father, Mama and the uncle, in lines short enough to float whole; the room abroad its shared kitchen.

A word bubble is placed from the whole scene, not from its speaker alone, and its tail is never longer than a bubble's height (80 px) and always ends over the speaker: first a spot over or beside the speaker's head clear of every head, every name shown and the marker; among those, one over a thing's dot or the road is better than one further off (a dot can be tapped a moment later, a face is read); only where no spot within the tail's reach is clear does it stand over the crowd, its tail at the longest and the bubble as low as that allows, covering least. It keeps the spot it had while that stays clear, so it does not hop as the crowd idles. The tail is the bubble's own, in its fill with its edge round it and its underside, by day and by night. Every line in a bubble wears one pair of curly quotes, the bubble's and never the line's, so the overheard lines, the news and a person's words read one way. While a bubble stands the emotes fade and wait, so no picture sits at its tail; a thing's fan opened hushes the bubble; the street's talk waits while a place's names are taught on its first entry and while the first brief's card stands.

## Rules: the month's count, the quiet months and the room

**world:no-months-left-count** Nothing on screen counts the months left or the months held (decision 0011): the month turns only at the bed's Sleep (world:sleep-ends-the-month), and the bank of turns behind it never holds a month back (turn:month-turns-on-empty-bank). The player never reads "turn" or "banked". A card's button says the act ("Buy a ticket", "Study"), never "Add to month" or "Add to this month", and a small line under it says it uses this month's free time when it does. Nothing lectures before play (world:no-walkthrough-before-play).

**world:no-auto-run-months** Nothing runs months in a row for the player (decision 0011). A quiet month is lived in minutes by hand, with the room's free acts, and turns when the player sleeps (world:sleep-ends-the-month), whatever time is left. Abroad the same. The simulation has no batch command.

**world:room-seen-down-its-length** The room, at home and abroad, is seen down its length: the camera turned so the long side runs up a portrait screen, fitted to the room's width, nothing cut off, the room about twice as large as seen from the front. From straight above a standing figure is a dot in a room this long, so such a room's whole view looks in from no higher than 50 degrees, at the file's distance and width, every corner of the floor still on screen. It holds the basics a life uses, each a thing with its acts on its fan (world:things-are-tappable-models, world:fan-of-sticker-buttons): the cooking corner ("Cook", obligation:cooking-and-fetching-water), the water drum ("Fetch water", obligation:cooking-and-fetching-water), the mattress (Nap or Lie down, and Sleep, as world:sleep-ends-the-month), the lantern or the desk (study), the bag by the door (pack, once the route is granted). Needs are not bars: the band names the body in words only when it complains (world:top-band-is-a-pill), and the room's acts answer it at once.

A home is one wide house of walled rooms (decision 0018), each named in its place file (`rooms`: the floor it covers and the view eased in on it), seen whole straight down its length on a longer lens (`Shot.fov`, 30 degrees), the near walls cut low. A tap on a room, a pinch or the wheel eases the camera in on that room in under 400 ms; a thing tapped from the whole house eases in first, so its fan opens close; a tap outside the room, on another room, past the walls, a pinch out or the whole-place chip eases back out; and the view does not reset when the same place is shown anew after an act. The things of a home carry its acts wherever they stand: the family's pledges and the send-off at the centre table (`family`), the way out on the slippers by each door (`out`, which opens the map), the family's bed as `mattress` and the hostel's as `bunk`, since the picture table allows one model per id. The things a life owns stand at the home's `stage.owned` spots (world:room-things-and-buy-mode), and the car stands at the home's car spot (`stage.car`) only while the life has one, whether the market's or one on finance.

A wide home turns the full circle a quarter at a time, four fixed views, by the round button over Phone or a one-finger sideways drag that snaps to the nearest quarter on release; the quarter is kept per home beside the save, so the home opens as it was left. Each outer side of the shell is built twice, full with its windows, doors and wall things and knee-high (0.6 m) with none; the pair that faces away from the camera stands full and the near pair low, and on the way round a wall only ever rises behind the rooms, never in front. Rooms ease in at every quarter, the view fitted across the turned floor between 2.4 and 7 metres; past 7 the room's middle shows and its ends run off the sides. A vertical drag tilts between the normal view and a 65 degree view from above and snaps to one; the tilt is not kept.

**world:month-passes-as-lived** The month passes as the player lives it. The days of the month are the simulation's: the view's `halfDays` (turn:month-lived-in-half-days, decision 0011) says how much of the month's free time is spent, and the interface keeps no count of its own. The simulation's outcomes come at the month's end and nothing runs on real time. Each month-act planned tells of itself as the month passes, in a line of its own ("Module 1 done. Two to go.", "Your first shift ran late. You came home at eleven."), one or two a month from content, its day read from the share of the free time spent, delivered as a phone notification once the character is home (the house or the room), never on the street; the phone's dot shows them waiting and they stand on its wallpaper at home ("This month so far"); away from home the wallpaper says only how many wait ("Something to read at home."). The Calendar app says when the plan stretches the character (work, a class and a side job together: "Work, then class, then the tutoring. You will be tired."); the cost itself is the simulation's as it is. The results keep their place as the month's telling. A paper that comes or moves at the month's end (issued, run out, made void, a copy on its way or brought forward, from the report's paper lines) is told on the wallpaper above the month's lines, at home or away, as a message from the office, in the paper's own words where it has them ("Your transcript is ready to collect.", "The council has verified your licence."), else the kind's ("Your {paper} is ready."). A tap on it opens Papers; it and the app's dot stay until Papers is opened. Nothing opens by itself: the phone opens on its home screen.

**world:japacity-bag-by-door** Japacity is shown two ways at once: in the band's tap-open figure, and in the room as the bag by the door, which swells with the figure (small at nothing, full at a hundred, eased toward a new size over about half a second), carries on top what is held (the passport, a folder of papers, the flyer once a route is known) and wears a small luggage tag with the bare figure, never a percent sign, hidden while no route is heard of. Every rise of the figure flies up from the room as "+N" toward the band and fades, one at a time, never on a fall; while a sheet, the results, the map or a card covers the world the figure waits and the rise and the growth come together when the cover goes. The rise is the one thing that may pass over the world unbidden (world:world-fills-the-screen). It is one model scaled, a drawn tag and a CSS animation.

**world:ride-cuts-between-angles** The ride cuts between its angles by itself: outside at the stop during the pre-roll, then from above, then for the danfo the window (from the aisle out of one sliding window: the yellow pillars, the rail across it, the street sliding past) and the seat (from a back seat: the row in front at the edges, the roof, the dashboard, the road through the windscreen), each for a share of the ride's seconds on its own clock; on a keke, out of its open side from the bench; on an okada the rider ahead and the open street; the cuts are the cut of world:entering-is-a-cut (a short dark, never a slide across the screen), and no setting changes the angles. The card's where-you-are lines follow the ride's clock; once the danfo's ride cuts from the window to the seat, the card's road line moves on to the next, so the two angles never carry the same caption. Skip ends it all at once and Turn back gets the character down at the next stop. A slow frame never jumps the ride ahead (the step is clamped). A frame up to a second long counts whole, so a slow but steady device keeps the ride's pace and still shows every angle (no frame is longer than the shortest angle); only a frame past a second (`STALL_MS`) is a stall and moves the clock one step. The ride still ends once the wall clock has run its length plus three and a half seconds (`RIDE_SLACK_MS`), so a slow phone never holds the player in the ride.

**world:no-walkthrough-before-play** Nothing lectures before play. On a device that has not played, once the first brief is carried, the Map and Phone thumbs carry their word at their foot until each is tapped once. The device keeps that it has played under its key (`japacity.walkthrough`, `shared/storage/storage.ts`), once both thumbs were tapped, and at once for a life that opens with a month behind it.

**world:fan-of-sticker-buttons** The fan (decision 0025, `../design/09-the-games-look.md` section 3). A tap on a thing opens no chip: every thing fans its acts out of the thing itself, round sticker buttons along a curve that sweeps sideways and down from the thing into the larger free side of the screen (a radius of 170 px on a phone and 200 on a laptop); up to six, a seventh peeking, a drag along the curve; the buttons follow the thing every frame; a spring-in 40 ms apart; a refusal shakes the button and plays the low double note. The simulation's now-acts are priced buttons, each with the act's picture as a tile inside a sticker, a name pill outside the curve and the price pill alone (no balance pair, no legend); a sheet act whose entries are all now-acts splits into one button each; while a button is pressed the band's counter shows what would be left and settles back when released. Any other act (a pose, the bed's Sleep, a sheet that opens a card, a command, out, enter, ride, an app, a word with someone) is a button with the act's own label and its tile or a glyph drawn for its kind (`shared/tiles/Glyph.tsx`: sit, lie, sleep, pay, papers, pack, out, in, talk, look, ride, home, stop, shut), no price and no Free. The thing's name stands in a pill over it with the person's mark where the game plays them; a thing with no act shows its name alone. A planned act is a shut Ongoing button with a Stop beside it (world:month-acts-carry-on); a spent act's button is shut with You are spent. Sleep. No room beside the thing, or more than eight buttons, opens the activity sheet (all now-acts) or a small sheet of the same buttons (`FanSheet`). A tap follows the act's own way: a now-act is sent on arrival; a pose poses; Sleep opens the first-night card or sleeps; a card opens after the walk; a command sends; out opens the map. The fan is the same in every build; no address turns it off.

**world:room-things-and-buy-mode** The things in the room and buy mode. Each home's place file has `stage.owned`, a set spot for each thing it may hold, which may `hides` a model it replaces (the foam mattress for the old bed) and carry the `acts` the thing brings (the TV's watch, the laptop's portfolio and tech, the gas cooker's cooking); `showOwned` adds each owned model where it stands, and the car stands at `stage.car` through `parkCar` for the market's car or one on finance. Buy mode is on only in the home lived in when it is a home of your own (the compound room, the hostel, Makoko, the room abroad) and something owned stands there, never at the family house, a street, an office or someone else's room. A hold of 420 ms on a thing you own lifts it (a ring, a shadow, the `pop` cue, the hint Drag to move, tap to turn); a drag sets it down where it fits (its footprint's corners on the walkable floor, no overlap with any other footprint, 0.2 m from the door's spot, people's spots and the spots things are walked to), else it shakes back to the last spot that fitted with the `no` cue; a tap while lifted turns it a quarter; a tap anywhere else sets it down. Where things stand is the device's, kept beside the save by life and home (`japacity.arranged`), cleared when a new life starts, never the simulation's; moving home puts things at the new home's set spots. The lifted copy moves by its transform alone; the room is rebuilt once, when the thing is set down. A test holds each home's budgets with everything it may hold owned and the car parked.

**world:lantern-canoe-on-map** The lantern canoe. On the open map of Lagos (world:map-is-painted-lagos, the paper map) one canoe is moored on the open water of the lagoon north-east of the Third Mainland Bridge, where nothing is staged and no ride goes, with a lantern at its prow: by day one more canoe of the water's decor with a warm dot, at night the one light on that water, its glow drawn above the night paint so it never goes out. Its spot is `[6515, 3425]` (`LANTERN_CANOE` in `paper/geometry.ts`), clear of the open map's close button at every opening; the canoe keeps a constant size on screen at every zoom, scaled like the place stickers; its glow is the shell's `glowFill` (world:colours-are-role-tokens). It is not a stop: no board, no name, not in the stops list nor the map probe's stops, never on the strip or a card, never the target of a ride, and no route crosses it; the scatter keeps 22 units off it. The open map's first look from a card that would show only part of the canoe, or put it under the head row, moves by the least that brings it whole into view below the head row (`openingView` in `paper/view.ts`); a look that leaves it wholly off the screen stays as it is, so the canoe is never half off the screen as the map opens from any stop. A tap on it (a hit area of at least 44 px) opens the dedication page (account:dedication-page-and-its-doors) and folds nothing; a screen reader hears "A canoe with a lantern". Once the account's own light is lit (account:name-a-light-for-yours) the canoe carries a second lantern at its stern. Abroad the map has no lagoon and no canoe (the five taps of city:credits-screen-attributions reach the page there). The lantern's glow breathes by opacity alone and holds still under reduced motion (world:motions-under-half-second).

**world:plane-cut-at-send-off** The plane cut (chapter:send-off-scene-in-beats beat 4; the teaser's shot drawn in the game). After "Board the plane" the painted map of Lagos (world:map-is-painted-lagos) is shown at night, folded out, the lagoon under; a small plane sticker lifts off from the airport's position along a dashed line toward the top of the screen in the route's direction, about three seconds by `element.animate` on transform alone (the one exception to world:motions-under-half-second's half second), and the city the plane lands in arrives on a sticker card ("London", "Calgary", "Dubai", "Naples"), keyed by the hub place's `city` in the scene's words, so Castel Volturno's plane comes down at Naples; then the cut ends into beat 5. Under reduced motion the map, the plane standing at the line's end and the sticker are shown still for the same time. It draws nothing the map does not already have but the plane sticker and the dashed line; no tiles of its own, no three.js scene; it is one layer over the paper map and costs the map nothing when it is not shown. The cut is handed to the scene by the shell (`planeCut`), so the scene and the map import nothing of each other.

**world:painted-maps-abroad** The painted maps abroad (`../design/10-london.md` section 3, `../design/11-calgary-dubai-and-castel-volturno.md` section 6; decision 0017). Abroad the map is a painted map as Lagos's is (world:map-is-painted-lagos, world:one-country-at-a-time, decision 0026), each country on the same descriptor (`paper/city.ts`), its own chunk loaded only when the map shows that country: its own fit from content's positions, its own land, roads and district names, the strip, the cards and the open map, every built place of the country as its sticker, and the home sticker at the country's home spot (`../design/11-calgary-dubai-and-castel-volturno.md` section 3). The UK's is south-east London: the river across the top, the railway through Peckham, the districts Peckham, Camberwell, Nunhead, the Old Kent Road, Elephant and Castle, London Bridge and the City across the water, and Heathrow on an inset in a corner of the paper with the plane, since it is too far west for the paper. Calgary's is the grid with the CTrain line to downtown, the airport north and the river south (cards: the north-east, the airport, downtown). Dubai's is the creek, the metro line, the airport south-east, the camp east and the site south across the creek (cards: Deira, the airport, the camp, the site). Castel Volturno's is the sea along the west, the Domitiana the length of the paper, the river and the fields, with Naples airport on an inset (cards: the town, the Domitiana, the fields). The country menu, the lantern canoe's absence, and the ways back are as at home. The stops list stays for a screen reader alone. The room abroad is named per country in content (`place.the-room.name.<country>`: the basement room, the bed space at the camp, the room in a shared flat), the full name on the place's own card; on the band and on the map's sticker it wears a short name of the client's ("The basement, Calgary", "The bed space, Dubai", "The shared flat, Castel", and London's by its hub), since content's full name runs past the band's width and off the paper.

**world:ride-abroad-and-workplace** The ride abroad and the workplace with a door (`../design/10-london.md` sections 3 and 4, `../design/11-calgary-dubai-and-castel-volturno.md` sections 4 and 6). Picking a stop abroad opens the ride sheet with the country's ways from the view's `place.ways` (in the UK the bus at its flat fare, the train by distance, on foot within walking distance), priced by `rideFare` as at home, and the fare pill names the country's pass in a month the pass is bought ("£0, bus pass", place:country-pass-covers-ways). The ride plays as the danfo's does (world:ride-cuts-between-angles) inside the country's own vehicle: London's red bus from a top-deck seat and its window, and the train from its carriage; Calgary's bus and the CTrain car; Dubai's metro car, the labour bus and the taxi; Castel Volturno's bus and the white van. The cuts and the clock are the ride's. The labour bus and the van are the shuttle's (place:employer-shuttle-rides-free), their pill saying the ride asks nothing. Dubai's city bus rides in Calgary's bus model and Calgary's taxi in Dubai's.

A workplace is a place with things (world:things-are-tappable-models): the door, the desk or the clock-in where the job is got and held (the hunt's card with that job named; the thing's label says "On shift" once held), the rota board for `activity.overtime` (staged at every workplace, city:places-stage-their-acts), the manager's door or the glass office for the next rung, and a colleague at the tea point, the kitchen or the shelter who says a line (a bubble, world:things-are-tappable-models). The job's days pass as the lived month plays (world:month-passes-as-lived). The things, their labels and their spots are the place file's. A workplace reached by the shuttle says On shift as any other; the Gulf's site is On shift from the first morning, since the job comes with the agent's contract. The interiors a country shares with London (the big shop, the care home, the office, the warehouse, the church) are London's scenes under the country's own entry, their signs and lines the country's: a shared interior's stage file is the country's own copy in a folder named after its place id (`public/assets/places/<place id>/place.json`), the models inside London's at the same spots, read by the client for a place abroad whose id-named folder holds a published file, else the `assets` folder. The tills, the staff notice and a manager's door with no rung in that country are look-only; the Gulf site's guard post and cleaner's trolley keep a look act for the job the contract did not give.
