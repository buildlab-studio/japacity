# Screenshot moments

> Rests on `share.md`, `text.md`, world:world-fills-the-screen and world:month-end-results-sheet, route:grant-moves-to-granted, route:refusal-refunds-and-resets, route:refusal-reasons-recorded and route:departure-pays-flight, advice:agent-kind-drawn-on-offer and advice:bad-news-told-straight, activity:one-chance-bus-robbery, luck:big-wins-are-staged, `lottery.md`, `chapter.md`, road:road-earns-nothing.
> Kind: none new. The simulation holds nothing for these rules: the client picks the moment from the lines it receives and draws the card from the view; content holds every word (`packages/content/src/moment/moment.text.ts`).

What people post from games like this is not a designed card: it is a plain screenshot of a dramatic outcome with one line and a big number, often a loss. So every big outcome in the game is one clean card over the scene, worth screenshotting, and every screenshot carries the game's name. The share cards (`share.md`) are their own thing; a moment card is a different thing.

## Rules

**moment:outcomes-that-are-moments** These outcomes are moments, each made by its report line: a visa granted (`line.route.granted`), a visa refused (`line.route.refused`), the endorsing body's refusal (`line.route.endorsement-refused`, route-gates:endorsement-decided-by-draw), the agent who was a scam (`line.offer.agent-scam`), the deposit a road pitch took for nothing (`line.route.road-fraud`), the one chance on the bus home (`line.activity.robbed`), a big win on the draw (`line.lottery.letter` or `line.activity.lotto-won`, staged as big by luck:big-wins-are-staged: a jackpot or a mid prize), landing abroad (the flight's `line.route.departed`), a chapter closing (`line.chapter.closed`), and a stamp reached on the passbook (`line.passbook.stamped`, `line.passbook.ready`; wallet:savings-stamps). The list is one list in the client (`apps/client/src/shared/moment/moment.ts`, `MOMENTS`; the card is `apps/client/src/features/moment/MomentCard.tsx`), most telling in this order: refused, granted, robbed, the agent, the deposit, the win, the landing, the chapter, held. A batch of lines (a month's report, a command's answer, the authority's own lines such as the draw's letter) gives at most one card, the first in that order, so the decision day closing beside the grant, or a chapter closing beside the flight, never stacks a second card; of several chapters closed at once, the last is told; the road's own figure comes before a fixed stamp, and the highest stamp of a batch makes the card. Nothing on the road chain is a moment (road:road-earns-nothing), and a life that has ended has its own card. The endorsing body's refusal is a refusal and makes the refused card itself, since the screen tells the endorsement's story once by the body's line and folds the route's refusal noted beside it away. The deposit a road pitch took for nothing (road:taking-pitch-pays-deposit's fraud, which ends the chain before it begins, the life still at home) is a loss in the agent's style; its figure is the deposit, read from the month's lines where the pitch was taken, and a deposit the month's lines do not hold makes no card. A stamp reached on the passbook is the moment `held`.

**moment:moment-card-contents** A moment card holds: a kicker naming the outcome ("Visa refused"), a title (the route, the country, the chapter, the agent's name, the bus home, the draw), one line in the game's voice, the figure that makes it land with its caption under it, at most one small fact beside it, and in its corner the game's small mark, the seal's small drawing picked by size through `markFile` (the small drawing up to 48 px, the medium up to 192, the full above: `apps/client/public/brand/japacity-mark-small-<ground>.svg`, `japacity-mark-medium-<ground>.svg`, `japacity-mark-<ground>.svg`), with the address japacity.app. The figure is a whole number, said whole: money in whole naira, pounds or dollars with its sign of currency, a share as a whole percent, or a count. Money may stand on a moment card, as part of the story, unlike a share card. The figures: granted, the months played; refused, when the letter refused on the funds, the share of the funds that could not be traced on the file it judged (route:unexplained-share-of-funds, route:refusal-reasons-recorded: the route's figure in the view while it was being decided, since once refused the figure is worked against the funds a new file would need), else the months played, and "Refusal 2 on this route" beside it from the second; the agent, the fee gone; the deposit, the deposit gone, with the pitch's own name as the title ("Your cousin's friend") and the kicker "The deposit is gone"; the endorsement refused, the months played under the kicker "Endorsement refused"; the one chance, the cash taken (the phone's cost when no cash was on you), with the new phone beside it; the win, the sum won, with the tickets and their cost beside a kiosk win; the landing, the months from Lagos; a chapter, japacity at home while a route has a figure, else the months played; held, the stamp's naira, under the kicker Held for the road or Money ready at the target, with the title Mama heard or the way's name. A win is drawn light (the sheet under the band's head), a loss dark (the band's colour throughout), in either look.

**moment:card-reads-when-cropped** A moment card reads in a cropped screenshot: it stands centred over the dimmed scene, no wider than 380 px, at least 16 px from each side of the screen and clear of the safe areas, so nothing it says is at an edge; the line is at least 24 px and bold, the figure at least 44 px and sized by its length so the longest sum stays on one line, the kicker at least 12 px, the address at least 16 px; it fits a 360 by 640 screen without scrolling.

**moment:card-never-looks-official** A moment card never looks like a bank alert, a transfer receipt, a payment app or a letter from a government (share:card-never-looks-official's list, `text.md` "Money on screen"): no alert layout, no reference number, no bank's name, no account, no balance, no crest, no barcode, no stamp, no tick beside a figure; the figure carries no plus or minus sign and stands in the card's own ink, never green or red; no word on a card says alert, credit, debit, transaction, receipt, reference, account, balance, transfer, bank or stamp.

**moment:words-in-game-voice** The words are content's, in the voice of `text.md`: second person, Nigerian English, short, no exclamation mark, no em-dash or en-dash. A loss is said straight, with no joke (text:bad-news-never-joked; the agent as advice:bad-news-told-straight says, naming him and the fee gone); its punch is the plain fact and the figure. A win may carry a wink. Money is said in the game's own words for it ("the cash on you", "paid to him, and gone"), and the line never repeats the figure the card already shows.

**moment:card-shows-when-outcome-lands** A moment card shows when its outcome lands, over the scene as it is then, and changes nothing else about the flow. At a month's end it stands over the results sheet once that sheet is up (as a dialog inside the sheet's own, so the sheet stays as it was under it); after an act, or when the draw's letter comes, it stands over the world once the staged lines told in the corner have passed. A big line the card tells is not told again in the corner. One tap anywhere on it or around it, Close, or Back dismisses it, and the sheet under it is there as before; the next card, if a run of months brought more than one, follows. The share card that is due waits until no moment card is left (`apps/client/src/shared/ui/stage.ts`). Moments stand over the world and its results sheet (world:month-end-results-sheet). While a card stands, the corner, the bubble and the rise wait, as under a share card (world:street-talk-budget, world:japacity-bag-by-door). A chapter's close is told twice, by the results line and the moment card, and never a third time. When a moment card is queued for a chapter closing and the view's share card is that chapter's (share:cards-due-at-set-moments), the share card is put aside as closed and kept in the gallery without opening; where no moment card stands, the chapter's share card opens. While a moment card stands over a world sheet (the results), the sheet's head steps back, its small line, its title and its close, so one close is in view; the sheet under it is otherwise as it was, and its head comes back as the card goes.

**moment:moment-is-not-share-card** The rules of `share.md` all hold, but for the chapter's share card a moment card has told (moment:card-shows-when-outcome-lands). A moment card is not a share card: it is drawn on screen only, never to an image; nothing on it is offered, shared or sent; it carries no share button; nothing about it is counted beyond what the screen under it counts, and it is never stored on the server.

**moment:new-moments-join-same-way** A new moment joins the same way: an entry in `MOMENTS` and its words under `moment.<kind>` in content, drawn by the same card, so it holds the line, the figure, the mark and the address of moment:moment-card-contents and obeys moment:card-reads-when-cropped, moment:card-never-looks-official, moment:words-in-game-voice, moment:card-shows-when-outcome-lands and moment:moment-is-not-share-card. A new system designs its key moments as moments from the start.

## The lines

Every line is in `moment.text.ts`. Wins carry a wink; losses are straight.

| Moment | Line chosen by |
|---|---|
| Granted | the route (`moment.granted.line.<route>`), else the plain line |
| Refused | the letter's first reason (`moment.refused.line.<reason>`) |
| The agent | one line, his name in the title |
| The deposit | the pitch (`moment.deposit.line.<offer>`), else the plain line; the pitch's name in the title |
| One chance | one line |
| The win | one line |
| Landed | the country (`moment.landed.line.<country>`), else the plain line |
| Chapter done | the chapter's own closing line (`chapter.<id>.close`, `chapter.md`) |
| Held | the stamp: the road's own figure (`moment.held.line.ready`, the way's name in the title), else the plain line (`moment.held.line`, Mama heard) |
