# The economy

**Context and scope.** Why a life at home earns, climbs and learns as it does, and how its money reaches a way out: the trades' ladders, side jobs and yearly naira pay review; learning tech in bands; the family's gift for Canada; and the student's money abroad against the real fees the routes charge. The ways out cost what they really cost ([`route.md`](../rules/route.md), [`route-gates.md`](../rules/route-gates.md), [`status.md`](../rules/status.md)); this page is about the income side and the money flows that meet those costs. It is for game design, not immigration or financial advice.

The rules and their figures are on [`activity.md`](../rules/activity.md) (the ladders, the review and the tech paths), [`quality.md`](../rules/quality.md) (quality:skill-bands-slow-gains), [`route-gates.md`](../rules/route-gates.md) (the family's gift and Canada's funds), [`route.md`](../rules/route.md) (the pledge and the education loan), [`abroad-money.md`](../rules/abroad-money.md) (the tuition instalments), [`status.md`](../rules/status.md) (the course, the Graduate visa and renewals) and [`scenario.md`](../rules/scenario.md) (scenario:economy-meets-its-targets, the targets and the balance test). The decisions are [0031](../decisions/0031-each-trade-climbs-its-own-ladder.md), [0032](../decisions/0032-learning-in-bands.md), [0033](../decisions/0033-the-students-money-flow.md) and [0035](../decisions/0035-the-balance-test-reads-calm-and-crash-worlds.md).

**Goals.**

- Every trade reaches a way out that suits it, by its own work, in a playable time (section 2).
- Loyalty to a trade pays: a rung above, a side job that belongs to the trade, and naira pay that keeps some pace with the dollar.
- Tech stays the richest road, as it is in life, but it costs time or money like any other. Switching to tech is one choice among several, never the answer for everyone.
- A student who really holds the tuition completes the course. One who shows only borrowed money meets warnings, with a way back at each, and can still fail.
- Real figures are the floor: every fee and pay figure has its real value and source on its rule page, or is marked "working value", and any factor needs a reason.

**Non-goals.**

- A cost factor below real on the routes' fees or funds: the gaps are closed on the income side (section 10).
- Borrowed money for Canada's funds: IRCC refuses it.
- Money that tops itself up: every catch-up road is chosen and has a price.
- A UK teacher's route. From 9 Sep 2026 teachers trained in Nigeria cannot use the "Apply for qualified teacher status in England" service, so the game builds no such route. The teacher's levers are the trade's own ladder, Canada's education draw and the master's.
- A cap on months or a timer: the targets are tuned to a time, never enforced by a limit.

## 1. Summary

Each trade climbs its own ladder, with a side job of its own and a yearly naira pay review. Tech is learned in bands that slow as they rise. The family's gift for Canada counts when it has a deed. The student pays the tuition in instalments, and every missed payment brings a warning before any withdrawal.

## 2. Targets per trade

The targets are months to the first flight for a careful player who stays in the trade, without luck, in a calm world (decision 0035). The bands, and the test that holds them, are scenario:economy-meets-its-targets. Each trade's cheap and dear way follows the pattern of real emigration from that trade:

| Trade | The pattern in life | So the cheapest way is |
|---|---|---|
| Nurse | Nigeria is among the top three sources of nurses joining the UK register; Canada runs healthcare rounds at CRS 475 in 2026 | UK care as a nurse with a trust's post |
| Teacher | Canada's education category lists secondary and elementary teachers | Canada's points or the master's |
| Customer service (the graduate's usual first job) | Remote support for foreign firms at $400 to $600 a month; outsourcing team leads | The Gulf, or Canada once on the dollar rung |
| Shop worker, trader | The Gulf contract; UAE visas for Nigerians resumed on 15 Jul 2024 | The Gulf contract; no formal route without a degree is invented |
| Fish | As the shop, with Makoko's savings collectors | The Gulf contract |
| Banker | Finance in Express Entry; a UK master's in finance | Canada's points |
| Accountant | Chartered accountants on Canada's and the UK's lists | The skilled worker as a chartered accountant |
| Developer | Remote work in dollars, the UK talent route, Canada | Canada or the talent route once remote |
| Graduate | 30,204 study grants to Nigerian main applicants in 2025 | Canada's points on the customer service ladder |

Notes:

- **The teacher is the hardest trade, on purpose.** Lagos pays teachers the least of the graduate trades: ₦25,000 to ₦250,000, with ₦100,000 a common figure. The teacher's faster ladder and home lessons, with the family's gift, bring Canada within the band; the director's education draw is the lever that can bring it sooner.
- **The accountant's dearer way is Canada's points**, because Canada's proof of funds outruns an accountant's naira pay.
- **The master's is the dearest road for every naira trade.** About $37,000 in all, which is the real cost. It is reached through the family (the pledge, which pays the school), a dollar rung, land, or the education loan. Below the senior bank officer, it is no one's first road.
- **Switching to tech** by the free path lands inside every trade's dearer band and is slower than every trade's cheap one (section 5).
- **A crash.** Every formal route priced in foreign money pulls away from naira pay, even with the review. That is honest for a crash: the dollar rungs (remote support and the remote developer) are what survive it, as in 2023 and 2024. The Gulf and the UK nurse's route stay reachable (threshold 10 of the balance test).

## 3. Design principles

| | The principle | Source | How the game applies it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every source of money flows to a sink the player wants, and the sinks run a little larger | Daniel Cook: value chains end at a motivation, and a chain with no reachable end makes early actions feel meaningless; sinks a little larger than sources | Each trade's rungs point at a way out its own surplus can reach. The passbook already shows the target; the ladders complete the chain |
| 2 | Tune to a time to goal, not to a hard limit | Ian Schreiber, Game Balance Concepts, level 7: tune the gains, the loops and the phase lengths so the game ends in the intended time; hard limits are the lazy answer | Section 2 sets months to the first flight by trade, and the balance test holds them. No cap on months, no timers |
| 3 | Costs climb faster than income, so each next step takes longer | Anthony Pecorella, on the maths of idle games: costs grow faster than production until a milestone resets the pace | Learning bands: each band gives less a month. A rung is the milestone that resets the pace |
| 4 | Many roads; the strongest arrives late and needs an investment | Stardew Valley's best earners need the greenhouse or rare seeds; The Sims 4's promotions ask for several skills at once | Tech can stay the richest road, but its remote job takes about as long as a trade's own dearer route, and each trade's own first way out stays faster than switching |
| 5 | Every career is a ladder with readable unlocks | The Sims 4's career levels list the skills each needs; Football Manager's contracts carry yearly rises that compound | Every trade has rungs, each unlocked by months and one quality or paper, shown in the Jobs app; a yearly review rewards loyalty |
| 6 | Catch-up the player controls | Schreiber: help for a player who is behind feels artificial when the player has no control over it; Civilization VI's boosts cut a technology's cost when the player does the linked thing | A poorer start gets more roads, each chosen and priced: the pledge or the gift (standing, the family's stake), the savings circle, the free path (time and discipline), the payment plan (a fee). Nothing tops up money by itself |
| 7 | Failure is a sequence of warnings with a way back, never a cliff | The Sims 4 cuts the power after unpaid bills rather than sending the repo man at once | Tuition: warnings, a payment plan, then withdrawal with time to put it right (obligation:way-out-at-every-stage) |
| 8 | No single dominant strategy | Schreiber and Romero, Game Balance (2021), on transitivity and cost curves; balancing progression with simulated players | The balance test caps the share of flights from a developer rung among lives that did not set out to switch, and the share of any one route (thresholds 4 and 6) |
| 9 | Model before you ship, and keep the model in the loop | Economy spreadsheets that simulate time, money and progression | A first model sets the tuning; simulated play is the proof (scenario:economy-meets-its-targets) |
| 10 | Real figures are the floor; any factor needs a reason | The game's own rule pages | Factor 1.0 wherever a source exists. Factors are used only to keep the dearer schools dearer, for the December peak week, and where a page says why |

## 4. Ladders and the yearly review

**Ladders** (activity:each-trade-climbs-its-ladder; the rungs, their pay and what each asks are in [`activity.md`](../rules/activity.md)). Every trade at home has a few rungs and one side job tied to it. The way up is months across the trade's jobs plus one quality or paper of the trade, so a rung reads as a real promotion: a nursing grade, the teaching registration, the chartered exams, or capital for the fish trade. The pay is the real advertised pay, after tax, at a factor of about 1.0.

Some choices need their reason said:

- **Night support is tied to the customer service trade.** Open to any life with fair English and a little tech, its dollar pay would beat a bank officer's and give everyone a second road to the dollar.
- **The fish trade's capital comes from a savings circle**, as it does in life: 85% of Makoko's women fishers fund their trade through a savings collector (activity:capital-opens-own-work).
- **The junior developer's job asks for a piece of work to show** as well as tech, because employers judge the portfolio.
- **The banker's side job is a POS stand in a cousin's name**, since banks bar their staff from outside business.

**The yearly review** (activity:naira-pay-reviewed-yearly). Naira pay is reviewed against the official dollar once a year: a formal employer gives more than an informal one, own work keeps its own growth, and dollar pay is not reviewed because the naira it buys already moves with the world. The real basis: Zenith raised all staff pay by 20% to 30% in Jan 2025, and four banks' wage bill rose about 13% a head in 2025, against inflation of 15% to 25%. Small employers rarely review pay, and low-end private schools pay below the minimum wage. So a calm year adds a little real pay, and a crash still pushes naira pay down against the pound, as 2024 did. The review reads the dollar because the dollar moves the routes' price.

## 5. Learning tech

The problem: a cheap day of class that could be repeated as often as the body allows would make tech the escalator for everyone, with the junior developer's job a month and a small sum away for any life. Real learning costs time or money.

- **Bands** (quality:skill-bands-slow-gains): each band gives less a month, as real skill slows as it rises.
- **The day's class is capped** (activity:study-days-capped), so a month of days gives no more than a month of the free path.
- **The free path** (activity:free-path-keeps-a-streak) costs only the month's data. Discipline is a streak with no random draw, standing for the real dropout's discipline. It reaches junior-ready inside the real 9 to 15 months, and gives nothing at the top bands, where real skill comes from work.
- **The paid paths** (activity:paid-tech-courses, activity:bootcamp-paid-after-a-job) buy speed with money: a short course, a year's diploma, a bootcamp paid after a job, and a free internship for the junior-ready. One tech study at a time.
- **The apprenticeship** at Computer Village stops early (streets:apprentice-learns-repair). It is a hardware trade, not a free road to a developer's skill.

**Worked example: a teacher who switches by the free path**, from the model:

| Step | Around month |
|---|---|
| Junior-ready | 9 or 10 |
| Hired as a junior developer | 12 |
| Working band, from the job and the path | 17 |
| The mid-level rung | 36 |
| The remote job found | 40 to 45 |
| Canada's funds held | about 5 months later |
| First flight | 45 to 50 |

That is inside the trade's dearer band, and the life abroad is richer: the trade-off a switch should carry.

## 6. The family's gift for Canada

The held-funds loan and the pledge belong to the master's, so without a family road Canada is funded from own savings alone. The family's gift is the real road (route-gates:family-gift-counts-with-deed):

- The family meeting gives a gift sized by the family's means, once a Canada plan has its credential assessment.
- A notary's gift deed states that it is not a loan, which is what makes it count as the applicant's own money.
- The family's stake rises, so a life gets either the gift or the master's pledge, never both.
- The funds are checked at the pool, at the invitation and again at the flight (route-gates:canada-funds-checked-at-departure), so spending the gift shuts the door until it is replaced.

In the model the gift brings the nurse to 58 months rather than 64, the teacher to 60 rather than 66, and the banker to 42 rather than 46.

## 7. Students' money abroad

**The problem.** A large first tuition instalment in the first month, against a small part-time surplus, with the held funds returned to the lender at the flight and a pledge that cannot be spent, broke 81% of simulated students. In life most Nigerian students complete and go on: 20,604 Graduate visa extensions went to Nigerians in the year to Jun 2026, against about 19,000 to 30,000 study grants a year before. The ones who fail are the ones without the money they showed.

**The design**, each part from a real practice:

1. **Instalments** (abroad-money:tuition-paid-in-instalments), as Sunderland and Greenwich run them.
2. **The pledge pays the school** (route:family-pledge-pays-tuition): the parent's account the visa counted is the account the fees come from, as Appendix Finance has it.
3. **A missed payment starts a sequence** (abroad-money:missed-tuition-runs-in-stages): reminders, a payment plan and a final warning before withdrawal, then the real 60 days to put it right (status:course-passed-or-withdrawn), as the universities' published policies run.
4. **Work as the Home Office rules allow** (status:status-sets-work-rule): part time in term, full time in the vacations, any job once the course has ended.
5. **The Graduate visa costs what it really costs** (status:graduate-visa-applied-for), paid from the full-time months after the course.
6. **Warnings at the source, and a regulated loan** ([`offer.md`](../rules/offer.md), route:education-loan-pays-the-school): the lender's show-money card says it goes back at the flight and the fees stay due. A regulated education loan pays the school directly and is repaid from the Graduate status.

**In the model** (Northgate at a second upper, £11,000 of tuition left, £1,003 a month of living costs):

| Student | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Careful, holding the full £21,539 shown | Completes, pays for the Graduate visa in month 13, and has £10,203 left |
| Pledged, with £8,000 of their own | Completes with £554 |
| Holding £5,000 | Misses once, takes the plan, and completes |
| Holding £9,000 | Withdrawn in month 7 |
| Arrived on borrowed money, with £300 | Withdrawn in about month 4 or 5, then has the two months after |

So the advice to the player is plain and true: hold the tuition that is left, and work pays for living.

## 8. Real fees set at factor 1.0

Each of these uses the real figure, given with its source and the date it was read on its rule page:

- Canada's citizenship fee ([`status.md`](../rules/status.md)).
- The care and nurse renewal at the Health and Care fee with no surcharge, and the skilled renewal at the inside-the-UK fee plus the surcharge (status:renewing-a-status).
- A hospital trust's sponsorship of nurses while the care window is shut, since the 2025 closure applied to care workers (route-gates:nurses-sponsored-while-care-shut).
- The NMC's tests ([`ladders.md`](../rules/ladders.md)).
- December from each city's real December median (place:december-factor-from-the-real-median). A single factor taken from London's for every country would put Canada at about 1.6 times real.
- The rent of a room in a compound ([`obligation.md`](../rules/obligation.md)).

## 9. How sure the figures are

- **Confirmed from primary sources:** the visa, surcharge, citizenship and NMC fees; IRCC's proof of funds and its refusal of borrowed money; the education category and its rounds; Appendix Finance on a parent's account and regulated loans; the universities' instalments and missed-payment steps; the Home Office's work rules and the 60 days.
- **From advertisements and press, single sources:** most home pay figures, the 2025 raises, the course and data prices.
- **Working values:** the months between rungs, the side jobs with no figure, Makoko's earnings, the TRCN and ICAN fees, the gift deed's fee, the payment plan's fee, the trust's find chance, the English figure that stands for IELTS 7.0, the scale of the education cut-off.
- **Reported, not confirmed on canada.ca:** that a gift with a deed counts as the applicant's own funds.

## 10. Alternatives considered

- **A cost factor below real on the fees and the funds.** Rejected: halving Canada's funds or the master's would still leave the nurse at about 87 months for Canada and the junior developer at about 110 for the master's. The gaps between home pay and the routes' money are 3 to 50 times, and a factor cannot close that. It would also teach false figures. The fix is on the income side, at real pay.
- **A loan for Canada's funds.** Opening the lender's show-money to the points route would be the cheapest fix in content. Rejected: IRCC says the money cannot be borrowed from another person, and a game that teaches borrowed funds teaches a refusal. The family's gift with its deed is the real road.
- **Withdrawal at the first missed payment.** Teesside's withdrawals of 2024 show it can happen. Rejected: it breaks four in five simulated students, careful ones with a pledge among them, where in life most complete. The multi-step policies the universities publish (which Teesside was found not to have followed) are the real sequence.
- **Making "being noticed" a yearly raise.** The cheapest way in content to grow pay. Rejected: it would reward a random draw, not loyalty, and would ignore the dollar, which moves the routes' price. The review reads it.
- **A cap on tech alone, or halving tech gains from the working band.** Rejected: either stops the escalator but leaves the player no roads to choose between. The bands and the paths (free, paid or full time) give the choice a price in months or in naira.
