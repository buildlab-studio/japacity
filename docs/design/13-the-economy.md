# The economy

**Context and scope.** This page covers what a life at home can earn, how fast it climbs, how it learns, and how its money reaches a way out:

- the trades' ladders and side jobs, and the yearly naira pay review;
- learning tech in bands;
- the family's gift for Canada;
- the student's money abroad, against the real fees the routes charge.

The ways out cost what they really cost (`../rules/route.md`, `../rules/route-gates.md`, `../rules/status.md`). This page is about the income side and the money flows that meet those costs. This page is for game design, not immigration or financial advice.

**Goals.**

- Every trade reaches a way out that suits it, by its own work, in a playable time: a careful player who stays in the trade flies within the trade's band in section 2.
- Loyalty to a trade pays: a rung above, a side job that belongs to the trade, and naira pay that keeps some pace with the dollar.
- Tech stays the richest road, as it is in life, but it costs time or money like any other. Switching to tech is one choice among several, never the answer for everyone.
- A student who really holds the tuition completes the course. One who shows only borrowed money meets warnings, with a way back at each, and can still fail.
- Real figures are the floor: every fee and pay figure has its real value and source, or is marked "working value", and any factor needs a reason.

**Non-goals.**

- A cost factor below real on the routes' fees or funds: the gaps are closed on the income side (section 10).
- Borrowed money for Canada's funds: IRCC refuses it.
- Money that tops itself up: every catch-up road is chosen and has a price.
- A UK teacher's route. From 9 Sep 2026 teachers trained in Nigeria cannot use the "Apply for qualified teacher status in England" service, so the game builds no such route. The teacher's levers are the trade's own ladder, Canada's education draw and the master's.
- A cap on months or a timer: the targets are tuned to a time, never enforced by a limit.

## 1. Summary

Each trade climbs its own ladder, with a side job of its own and a yearly naira pay review. Tech is learned in bands that slow as they rise. The family's gift for Canada counts when it has a deed. The student pays half the tuition at enrolment and the rest monthly, and every missed payment brings a warning before any withdrawal.

## 2. Targets per trade

The months to the first flight for a careful player who stays in the trade, without luck, in a calm world (`world-3`, where the UAE and Canada windows stay open; decision 0035). The money is the months of surplus needed to reach the route's money, starting money included. The papers add their own waits: the passport, the tests, a school's answer, the verification, the pool. scenario:economy-meets-its-targets holds the bands.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nurse (`scenario.nurse`, the nurse) | Nigeria is among the top three sources of nurses joining the UK register; Canada runs healthcare rounds at CRS 475 in 2026 | UK care as a nurse with a trust's post; the Gulf's nursing offers as the fallback | 18 to 36 (the verification asks for 24 months of nursing, then takes 6 to 12) | Canada's points with the care cut-off | 42 to 60 |
| Teacher (`scenario.teacher`, the writer) | Canada's education category lists secondary and elementary teachers | Canada's points (the education draw while it stands) or the master's with the pledge | 48 to 60 | None: the UK teacher's route is shut (Non-goals) | |
| Customer service (the graduate's usual first job) | Remote support for foreign firms at $400 to $600 a month; outsourcing team leads | The Gulf, or Canada once on the dollar rung | 24 to 36 | The skilled worker (the sponsor is the gate) or the master's | 36 to 66 |
| Shop worker, trader | The Gulf contract; UAE visas for Nigerians resumed on 15 Jul 2024 | The Gulf contract | 18 to 30 | Own stall is the rung; no formal route without a degree is invented | |

Notes on the table:

- **The teacher is the hardest trade, on purpose.** Lagos pays teachers the least of the graduate trades: ₦25,000 to ₦250,000, with ₦100,000 a common figure. The teacher's faster ladder (the senior rung at 18 months, head of department at 30) and home lessons at up to ₦120,000 bring the Canada road to about 60 months with the family's gift. The director's education draw is the lever that can bring it sooner.
- **The master's is the dearest road for every naira trade.** About ₦49.3m in all at the starting rates, about $37,000, which is the real cost. It is reached through the family (the pledge, which pays the school), a dollar rung, land, or the education loan. Below the senior bank officer, it is no one's first road.
- **Switching to tech.** A non-developer who switches by the free path flies at about month 45 to 50 (section 5): inside every trade's dearer band, and slower than every trade's cheap one.
- **A crash.** The crash world (`world-99`, the pound about twice as dear by month 36). The Gulf and the UK nurse's route stay reachable in 7 to 22 months of money. Every formal route priced in foreign money pulls away from naira pay, even with the review. That is honest for a crash: the dollar rungs (remote support and the remote developer) are what survive it, as in 2023 and 2024.

## 3. Design principles

| | The principle | Source | How the game applies it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every source of money flows to a sink the player wants, and the sinks run a little larger | Daniel Cook: value chains end at a motivation, and a chain with no reachable end makes early actions feel meaningless; sinks a little larger than sources | Each trade's rungs point at a way out its own surplus can reach. The passbook already shows the target; the ladders complete the chain |
| 2 | Tune to a time to goal, not to a hard limit | Ian Schreiber, Game Balance Concepts, level 7: tune the gains, the loops and the phase lengths so the game ends in the intended time; hard limits are the lazy answer | Section 2 sets months to the first flight by trade, and the balance test holds them. No cap on months, no timers |
| 3 | Costs climb faster than income, so each next step takes longer | Anthony Pecorella, on the maths of idle games: costs grow faster than production until a milestone resets the pace | Learning bands: each band gives less a month. A rung is the milestone that resets the pace |
| 4 | Many roads; the strongest arrives late and needs an investment | Stardew Valley's best earners need the greenhouse or rare seeds; The Sims 4's promotions ask for several skills at once | Tech can stay the richest road, but its remote job takes about as long as a trade's own dearer route, and each trade's own first way out stays faster than switching |
| 5 | Every career is a ladder with readable unlocks | The Sims 4's career levels list the skills each needs; Football Manager's contracts carry yearly rises that compound | Every trade has two or three rungs, each unlocked by months and one quality or paper, shown in the Jobs app; a yearly review rewards loyalty |
| 6 | Catch-up the player controls | Schreiber: help for a player who is behind feels artificial when the player has no control over it; Civilization VI's boosts cut a technology's cost when the player does the linked thing | A poorer start gets more roads, each chosen and priced: the pledge or the gift (standing, the family's stake), the savings circle, the free path (time and discipline), the payment plan (a fee). Nothing tops up money by itself |
| 7 | Failure is a sequence of warnings with a way back, never a cliff | The Sims 4 cuts the power after unpaid bills rather than sending the repo man at once | Tuition: a reminder, access cut with a payment plan, a final warning, withdrawal, then two months to put it right (obligation:way-out-at-every-stage) |
| 8 | No single dominant strategy | Schreiber and Romero, Game Balance (2021), on transitivity and cost curves; balancing progression with simulated players | The balance test: among lives that did not start as developers and did not set out to switch, at most 35% of flights leave from a developer rung; no route takes over half the formal flights |
| 9 | Model before you ship, and keep the model in the loop | Economy spreadsheets that simulate time, money and progression | A first model sets the tuning; simulated play is the proof (scenario:economy-meets-its-targets) |
| 10 | Real figures are the floor; any factor needs a reason | The game's own rule pages | Factor 1.0 wherever a source exists. Factors are used only to keep the dearer schools dearer, for the December peak week, and where a page says why |

## 4. Ladders and the yearly review

**Ladders.** Every trade at home has two or three rungs and one side job tied to it (activity:each-trade-climbs-its-ladder). The way up is months across the trade's jobs plus one quality or paper of the trade: a nursing grade, the teaching registration (`document.trcn`), the chartered exams (`document.ican`, three sittings over 18 months), or capital from the savings circle for the fish trade. The pay is the real advertised pay, after tax, at a factor of about 1.0 (activity.md, Numbers):

| Trade | Rungs (requirements) | Side job |
|---|---|---|
| Nurse | Staff nurse ₦180,000; senior nursing officer ₦280,000 (30 months, care 70); ward manager ₦420,000 (60 months, care 80, standing 50) | Home-care visits at weekends |
| Teacher | Private school ₦90,000; senior teacher at a better school ₦220,000 (18 months, English 60, the registration); head of department ₦400,000 (30 months, a master's at home or standing 60) | Home lessons, with the exam coaching tier for a teacher of two years |
| Customer service | Agent ₦110,000; team lead ₦220,000 (18 months, English 60); remote support for a foreign firm $500 (24 months across support work, English 70, tech 20) | Night support. It is tied to the trade because at $350 for any life at English 50 and tech 20 it would pay more than a bank officer earns and give everyone a second road to the dollar |
| Shop | Phone sales ₦70,000; shop supervisor ₦120,000; your own stall ₦180,000 | Weekend phone repairs |
| Fish | The racks ₦70,000; your own kiln ₦120,000 (₦100,000 for the kiln and the first stock); fish trader ₦200,000 (₦300,000 of capital) | Canoe deliveries |
| Banker | Bank officer ₦320,000; senior ₦560,000; relationship manager ₦900,000 (36 months as senior, network 50) | A POS stand in a cousin's name, ₦300,000 for the machine and the float |
| Accountant | Accounts officer ₦200,000; chartered accountant ₦400,000 (24 months, the chartered exams); finance manager ₦700,000 | Bookkeeping for small firms |
| Developer | The junior job asks for a piece of work to show (portfolio 1) as well as tech 30, because employers judge the portfolio | |

The fish trade's capital is paid as it is in life, where 85% of Makoko's women fishers fund their trade through a savings collector (activity:capital-opens-own-work).

**The yearly review** (activity:naira-pay-reviewed-yearly). At each 12 months in a naira job, pay is reviewed against the official dollar:

- A formal employer gives 10%, plus half of the dollar's rise beyond 10% over the year, at most 30% in all.
- An informal employer (the private school, the shop counter, the racks) gives 5%.
- Own work keeps its own growth.
- Dollar pay is not reviewed: the naira it buys moves with the world.

The real basis: Zenith raised all staff pay by 20% to 30% in Jan 2025, and four banks' wage bill rose about 13% a head in 2025, against inflation of 15% to 25%. Small employers rarely review pay, and low-end private schools pay below the minimum wage. So a calm year adds a little real pay, and a crash still pushes naira pay down against the pound, as 2024 did. The review reads the dollar because the dollar moves the routes' price.

## 5. Learning tech

The problem: a ₦5,000 day of class that gives +2 and can be repeated as long as the body allows would make tech the escalator for everyone. Tech 30 and the junior developer's ₦320,000 would be one month and about ₦75,000 away for any life. Real learning costs time or money.

- **Bands** (quality:skill-bands-slow-gains): starting, junior-ready (30), working (50), strong (70), senior (85). Every tech study at home names its gain a month for each band, and the gains shrink as the bands rise.
- **The day's class is capped** (activity:study-days-capped): three a month, which together give what a month of the free path gives.
- **The free path** (activity:free-path-keeps-a-streak): free sites and videos for the cost of the month's data, ₦6,500. It gives +3, +2, +1 a month by band, and nothing from 70, where real skill comes from work. Discipline is a streak with no random draw: every fifth month in a row finishes a certificate. Junior-ready takes 10 months, inside the real 9 to 15.
- **The paid paths** (activity:paid-tech-courses): a short course (₦45,000), a one-year diploma (₦40,000 a month), a full-time bootcamp paid after a job (₦3,000,000 over 36 months once a developer job is held, activity:bootcamp-paid-after-a-job), and a free internship for the junior-ready. One tech study at a time.
- **The apprenticeship** at Computer Village stops at tech 30 and gives +2 (streets:apprentice-learns-repair). It is a hardware trade, not a free road to tech 60.

**Worked example: a teacher who switches by the free path.**

| Step | Around month |
|---|---|
| Junior-ready | 9 or 10 |
| Hired as a junior developer | 12 |
| Tech 50, from the job and the path | 17 |
| Tech 60 | 21 |
| Strong (75) | 33 |
| The mid-level rung, after its 24 months | 36 |
| The remote job found | 40 to 45 |
| Canada's funds held | about 5 months later |
| First flight | 45 to 50 |

That is inside the trade's dearer band, and the life abroad is richer: the trade-off a switch should carry.

## 6. The family's gift for Canada

The held-funds loan and the pledge belong to the master's, so without a family road Canada is funded from own savings alone. The family's gift is the real road (route-gates:family-gift-counts-with-deed):

- The family meeting, called for a Canada plan once the credential assessment is held, gives family means times ₦100,000 (₦3,000,000 at means 30).
- A notary's gift deed states that it is not a loan.
- The family's stake rises, so a life gets either the gift or the master's pledge, never both.
- The gift is the applicant's to spend. The funds are checked at the pool, at the invitation and again at the flight (route-gates:canada-funds-checked-at-departure), so spending the gift shuts the door until it is replaced.

In the model the gift brings the nurse to 58 months rather than 64, the teacher to 60 rather than 66, and the banker to 42 rather than 46.

## 7. Students' money abroad

**The problem.** A first tuition instalment of about £3,667 to £4,334 in month 1, against a part-time surplus of £400, with the held funds returned to the lender at the flight and a pledge that cannot be spent, breaks 81% of simulated students. In life most Nigerian students complete and go on: 20,604 Graduate visa extensions went to Nigerians in the year to Jun 2026, against about 19,000 to 30,000 study grants a year before. The ones who fail are the ones without the money they showed.

**The design:**

1. **Half at enrolment, then four monthly instalments** (abroad-money:tuition-paid-in-instalments), as Sunderland and Greenwich run it.
2. **The pledge pays the school** (route:family-pledge-pays-tuition): the parent's account the visa counted is the account the fees come from.
3. **A missed payment starts a sequence** (abroad-money:missed-tuition-runs-in-stages): a reminder; access cut and a payment plan; a final warning; withdrawal only after two plan payments missed in a row; then two months (the real 60 days) to pay and be reinstated, take a new course, or switch status (status:course-passed-or-withdrawn).
4. **Work as the rules allow** (status:status-sets-work-rule): 20 hours a week in term, full time in the Christmas and Easter vacations, 20 hours over the summer for the dissertation, and any job once the course has ended.
5. **The Graduate visa costs what it really costs** (status:graduate-visa-applied-for): £937 plus the surcharge, paid from the full-time months after the course.
6. **Warnings at the source, and a regulated loan** (offer.md, route:education-loan-pays-the-school): the lender's show-money card says it goes back at the flight and the fees stay due. A regulated education loan pays the school directly and is repaid from the Graduate status.

**In the model** (Northgate at a second upper, £11,000 of tuition left, £1,003 a month of living costs):

| Student | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Careful, holding the full £21,539 shown | Completes, pays for the Graduate visa in month 13, and has £10,203 left |
| Pledged, with £8,000 of their own | Completes with £554 |
| Holding £5,000 | Misses once, takes the plan, and completes |
| Holding £9,000 | Withdrawn in month 7 |
| Arrived on borrowed money, with £300 | Withdrawn in about month 4 or 5, then has the two months after |

So the advice to the player is plain and true: hold the tuition that is left, and work pays for living.

## 8. Real fees set at factor 1.0

Each of these uses the real figure (the date each was read is in its rule page's Sources):

- Canada's citizenship fee, C$653 (status.md).
- The care and nurse renewal at the Health and Care fee, £324, with no surcharge, while a care, senior care or nurse job is held. The skilled renewal at the inside-the-UK fee for up to three years, £943, plus the surcharge: £3,013 in all (status:renewing-a-status).
- Nurses can be sponsored by a hospital trust while the care window is shut, at a 4% chance a month, since the 2025 closure applied to care workers (route-gates:nurses-sponsored-while-care-shut).
- The NMC's tests at £1,226 (ladders.md).
- December from each city's real December median times 1.15 (place:december-factor-from-the-real-median): the UK keeps 1.7; Canada 1.2, the UAE 1.4, Italy 1.2. A single factor of 1.7 for all would put Canada at 1.6 times real.
- The rent of a room in a compound, ₦350,000 a year, on the obligation page, as content and the prologue have it.

## 9. How sure the figures are

- **Confirmed from primary sources:** the visa, surcharge, citizenship and NMC fees; IRCC's proof of funds and its refusal of borrowed money; the education category and its rounds; Appendix Finance on a parent's account and regulated loans; the universities' instalments and missed-payment steps; the Home Office's work rules and the 60 days.
- **From advertisements and press, single sources:** most home pay figures, the 2025 raises, the course and data prices.
- **Working values:** the months between rungs, the side jobs with no figure, Makoko's earnings, the TRCN and ICAN fees, the gift deed's fee, the payment plan's fee, the trust's find chance, the English figure that stands for IELTS 7.0, the scale of the education cut-off.
- **Reported, not confirmed on canada.ca:** that a gift with a deed counts as the applicant's own funds.

## 10. Alternatives considered

- **A cost factor below real on the fees and the funds.** Rejected: halving Canada's funds or the master's would still leave the nurse at about 87 months for Canada and the junior developer at about 110 for the master's. The gaps between home pay and the routes' money are 3 to 50 times, and a factor cannot close that. It would also teach false figures. The fix is on the income side, at real pay.
- **A loan for Canada's funds.** Opening the lender's show-money to the points route would be the cheapest fix in content. Rejected: IRCC says the money cannot be borrowed from another person, and a game that teaches borrowed funds teaches a refusal. The family's gift with its deed is the real road.
- **Withdrawal at the first missed payment.** Teesside's withdrawals of 2024 show it can happen. Rejected: it breaks four in five simulated students, careful ones with a pledge among them, where in life most complete. The multi-step policies the universities publish (which Teesside was found not to have followed) are the real sequence.
- **Making "being noticed" a yearly raise.** The cheapest way in content to grow pay. Rejected: it would reward a random draw, not loyalty, and would ignore the dollar, which moves the routes' price. The review reads it.
- **A cap on tech alone, or halving tech gains from 50.** Rejected: either stops the escalator but leaves the player no roads to choose between. The bands and the paths (free, paid or full time) give the choice a price in months or in naira.
