# 0035. The balance test reads calm and crash worlds

Status: Accepted

## Context

The balance test (scenario:economy-meets-its-targets, decision 0031) first played every life and style in the golden world and in world-b. 8 of its 10 measures failed. Read against the game's aims, part of the failure was the test reading the wrong world or the wrong players.

**The trades' bands read a world with a ban in it.**

- The bands are months to the first flight "without luck".
- The golden world draws a UAE ban in its months 13 to 30, as the real ban on Nigerian visas ran for most of two years.
- The Gulf's trades, the shop and the fish trade, could not fly inside their bands while the ban stood.
- Every band was read in a world with that luck in it.

**world-b was not a crash.**

- It was meant as a crash with the pound 2.36 times dearer in 36 months. As built it rises 1.07 times.
- No seed of the first 500 reaches 2.36. The steepest, world-v, reaches 2.08, with its UAE window shut from month 18.
- The tenth measure asks whether the cheap ways hold up in a crash, and in world-v the Gulf's trades read a ban instead.

**Canada's points could not be the accountant's cheap way.**

- Canada's settlement funds, C$15,263 or about ₦16m, outrun an accountant's naira pay, as in life.

**The banker's band had no basis.** A banker's pay saves those funds in about two years, so a band that starts at month 36 had no basis.

**The developer-rung measure counted the pivot styles.** The two pivot styles exist to pivot. They alone made about a third of all flights, so the measure could not be met whatever the rest did.

**A care worker's renewal is small by law.** The Health and Care visa's reduced fee and its exemption from the surcharge put the renewal at about a fifth of a month's keep, under the sinks' 0.5.

## Decision

**A calm world for the bands.**

- The first, second, third and fifth measures read a calm world.
- A calm world is the first `world-<n>` whose windows for the UAE and Canada's points never close before month 72, and whose UK care window, shut at the start in every world, never closes again once opened.
- It is `world-3`, named in the test.
- The golden world keeps the developer rung, the mix, the students, tech's pace and the money, so the luck of a real ban still shows there.

**The crash world is a crash with the Gulf open.**

- The tenth measure reads the steepest pound by month 36, among `world-v` and `world-0` to `world-499`, whose UAE window never shuts through month 36, with the pound at least 1.8 times dearer by then.
- It is `world-99`, 1.99 times dearer.
- The review's own figure is still read in the golden world beside it.

**The accountant's cheap way is the skilled worker as a chartered accountant,** 36 to 54 months.

- Canada's points are the dearer way, 48 to 72.
- The trade-loyal accountant works the skilled worker first, then Canada.

**The banker's band is 24 to 48 months** to Canada's points.

**The developer rung reads the players who did not set out to pivot.** The fourth measure counts every style but the two pivots.

**The sinks' bounds are 0.2 to 6 months** of what the month keeps, for settlement, renewal and citizenship. The broke run stands as it was.

**One test file for each world.** Each cites scenario:economy-meets-its-targets and holds its own measures, so they run on separate workers.

## Consequences

- scenario.md's measure table names each measure's world, and its bands table reads "in a calm world". Design 13 section 2's banker and accountant rows and the test's cheap bands hold the same figures.
- A trade that cannot reach its band in a calm world is the economy missing its target. A band missed only in the golden world is the luck of the draw, and shows in the mix and the money instead.
- A world's draws move when the stories or the eras move. So a later change that moves the calm or the crash world's windows or pound names the new seed in the test and in scenario.md.
- If no seed meets the crash world's rule, the tenth measure stops there until the eras give one.
