# 0032. Learning in bands

Status: Accepted

## Context

A day's coding class at ₦5,000 that gives tech +2 and repeats as long as the body allows (activity:acts-may-repeat-in-month, decision 0012) puts tech 30, enough for the junior developer's ₦320,000 with no degree asked, about ₦75,000 and one month away for any life, with the remote job 7 to 20 months on: in simulated play the developer's jobs become everyone's road out. An apprenticeship at Computer Village that runs to tech 60 is a second free road there. Real learning costs time or money: free courses take months of discipline at the cost of data, paid ones cost tens of thousands to millions of naira, and employers judge a junior by work shown. `../design/13-the-economy.md` section 5 gives the design.

## Decision

- **Bands** (quality:skill-bands-slow-gains): tech is read in five bands, and every tech study at home names its gain a month for each band in its entry's `bandGains`; the halving at 70 still applies.
- **The day's class capped** (activity:study-days-capped): at most three a month, together giving the free path's month; refused while a tech study path is held. This changes decision 0012's repeat answer for this one act.
- **The free path** (activity:free-path-keeps-a-streak): a month-act for the month's data, with a streak of months, not a draw, that finishes a certificate every fifth month.
- **The paid paths** (activity:paid-tech-courses, activity:bootcamp-paid-after-a-job): a short course, a year's diploma as an obligation, a full-time bootcamp that takes the main slot and is paid after a developer job, and a free internship; one tech study at a time.
- **The junior developer asks a piece of work to show** (portfolio 1), and the apprenticeship stops at tech 30 with +2 a month (streets:apprentice-learns-repair).

## Consequences

- Every life that studied tech learns more slowly and pays more or waits longer, so every golden and recorded life that uses the day's class moves from the month it first does; the teacher, who starts at tech 32 with no portfolio, no longer meets the junior job at once.
- New state: the free path's streak and the bootcamp's debt on the activity slice; new content: the paths, their band gains and the bootcamp's obligation.
- The developer's road stays the richest, and a pivot to it lands inside a trade's dearer band (scenario:economy-meets-its-targets, the fifth measure).
- The study acts abroad keep their flat figures.

## Alternatives

- **Cap the day's class at three a month and nothing more.** It stops the escalator but leaves no choice between free, paid and full-time learning.
- **Halve tech gains from 50.** It slows the top but leaves tech 30 a month away.
- **A dropout drawn by chance on the free path.** The real dropout is the discipline; a streak the player keeps is the same lesson with nothing left to luck.
