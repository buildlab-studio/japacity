# A main journey, a long game, turn budgets, and debt

> Research, 7 Oct 2026, from official wikis, trophy statistics, designer writing, and regulator pages.
> Completion figures come from people who paid for console games. Nothing found proves that any structure here raises 30-day retention in a free browser sim. Unverified items are listed at the end.

## 1. A guided main sequence, then open play

| Game | Main sequence | Play time to credits | Who gets there | What comes after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A console racing game | 39 menu books, mostly "collect these cars" | 23 to 28 hours | First book 96%. Finale 40%. All licences 28%. All gold 11.5% | Extra menus added after launch, licences, missions, circuit challenges, weekly online races. Online is locked until book 9 |
| An open-world crime game | Story missions | Not confirmed | Story finished by 15 to 29% depending on platform | The online mode, which is where long-run play and spending sit |
| An island life-sim | Raise the island to three stars, then a concert rolls credits | Gated by real days | No statistics | The main building tool unlocks after credits |
| A farming game | Restore the community centre. A review at the start of year 3 | About 50 hours | Community centre 23%. Full completion 2.3% | Perfection goals, family |
| A mobile life-sim | None. Each life is the unit | | | 35 ribbons awarded at death, weekly challenges, continuing as your child |

- **Even in paid games, 20 to 55% of players see the credits.** A free browser game will be far lower.
- **The praised model for a start**: a small, self-contained version of the whole game that teaches every core mechanic before the map opens.
- **Takeaway**: the "credits" moment has to land within the first few sessions, and the systems that power the long game should be introduced on the way there.

## 2. Pacing by a budget of actions

| Game, since | Budget | Still going |
|---|---|---|
| A browser story game, 2009 | One action every 10 minutes, cap 20 | Yes, 15+ years |
| A browser comedy role-playing game, 2003 | 40 turns a day plus extras. Banked turns cut to 200 each night | Yes |
| A browser crime game, 2004 | Energy refills 5 every 15 minutes, cap 100 | Yes. About 80,000 daily players in 2025 |

- **In all three, your character moves only when you spend your budget, while festivals and events run on the real calendar for everyone.**
- A day's turns fit in a lunch hour. Players call it habit-forming but bounded.
- **Energy systems are disliked when they block play.** Practitioner advice: a free player should be able to play an occasional hour-long session, and energy should refill on level-up. One game removed energy after its core players rejected it.
- **No long-lived shared world with characters that age was found.** One that planned it closed before launch.

## 3. Debt and repossession in games

| Game | How it works |
|---|---|
| A household life-sim | Weekly bills. Unpaid: power off, then a collector takes items daily until you pay |
| The island life-sim | Home loans with no interest and no deadline, by deliberate choice |
| A border-inspector game | Rent every night is forced. Food, heat, and medicine are choices. Skipping makes the family ill |
| A street-vendor sim | A street vendor's rent, bills, and time. Admired and found hard to bear |
| A boxing management game | Stats decay daily and feed each other. Rated a chore. An easy mode was added |
| A trucking sim | Loans at 12 to 23%, repaid daily. In overdraft: three days, then trucks are taken and garages close |

**No mainstream game with a credit score was found.**

**The spiral problem**: falling behind makes recovery impossible, and players quit. What designers do about it:

- **Grace periods** before any penalty.
- **Soft exits**: hand the thing back and walk away with less.
- **No kicking while down**: when a player is struggling, random events tilt toward helpful ones.
- **A one-time rescue.**
- **A fresh start** that keeps something.

## 4. Real rules to build on

### Car finance in the UK (the common dealer plan)

- Deposit of 10% or more, three to five years of monthly payments, then a large optional final payment to keep the car. Mileage usually capped at 10,000 a year.
- Typical rates about 10 to 15% for good to average credit (2026, from a lender, not an official figure).
- Two missed payments bring an arrears notice, then a default notice with at least 14 days.
- **Under a third paid: the lender can take the car without a court order. Over a third: it needs one.**
- **At half paid you can hand the car back and owe nothing more.**
- Any shortfall after repossession stays as debt, and a court judgment sits on the credit file for six years.

### Car loans in the US (mid 2026)

- Rates by credit tier for a used car: about 6% at the top, 9% prime, 14% near prime, 19% subprime, 22% at the bottom.
- In most states the car can be taken straight after one missed payment, without warning. If the sale does not cover the debt, you owe the rest.

### Arriving with no credit history

- Credit history does not cross borders. Newcomers to the UK, US, and Canada start with an empty file.
- It is built by small on-time payments: a starter card, bills in your name.
- It decides renting, phone contracts, and car finance. One UK broker says an empty file sinks more foreign applicants' mortgages than income does.

### UK visa conditions that touch money

- **No public funds**: no housing support or general benefits on most visas.
- **A sponsored worker who loses the job** usually has 60 days to find a new sponsor, switch visa, or leave.
- Landlords must check the right to rent.
- Health surcharge: £1,035 a year, paid up front for the whole visa.
- Mortgages for visa holders typically need a 10 to 25% deposit and time left on the visa.

### Cars and work

- Home-care jobs commonly ask for a full UK licence and your own car. In a 2026 survey of home-care providers, 57% named staff being unable to drive as their top hiring barrier.
- A foreign licence is valid for 12 months after becoming resident. After that: a provisional licence, a theory test, and a practical test. Learning costs about £2,200.

### At home in Nigeria

- Cars are bought with cash. About three quarters of vehicle imports are used cars. Bank car loans cost 18 to 30%.
- About 14% of adults have formal credit (2024).
- Loan apps have messaged borrowers' contacts to shame them. The consumer regulator brought in rules with heavy fines in July 2025.
- Rotating savings groups run on trust: members pay in, one member collects each round.

## 5. What this means for design

1. **Pace the character's life by a budget of turns and run the world on real time.** It is the one model with 15 to 20 years of precedent in shared browser games, and it makes absence harmless.
2. **Be generous early.** Refill the budget at each step of the main journey so a keen new player can play for an hour.
3. **A short main journey that ends in a clear moment**, introducing each long-game system on the way.
4. **Debt abroad is institutional and has rules with thresholds.** A third paid and half paid are natural decision points. Debt at home is social.
5. **A credit file that starts empty** is an untried and true-to-life progression system.
6. **The car is a work tool.** For a home carer, losing it means losing shifts, which is how the chain reaches the visa.
7. **Build the spiral dampers in from the start.**
8. **Treat ageing as an experiment.** Nothing long-lived does it in a shared world.
