## 1. Borrowing at home in Nigeria

- **Loan apps shame borrowers through their contacts.** The apps ask for access to the phone's contacts before they lend. After a missed payment, recovery agents message those contacts and call the borrower a debtor or a fraudster. The game's quick loan says in its small print that it takes your contacts. Source: [ICIR, 2021](https://www.icirnigeria.org/nigerian-lending-apps-that-shames-threatens-customers-for-late-payment-of-loans/).
- **Digital lenders are now regulated.** The consumer regulator's digital lending regulations came into force on 21 July 2025. They target harassment and abusive recovery. Fines reach ₦100 million or 1% of turnover. Source: [FCCPC](https://www.fccpc.gov.ng/media/press-releases/digital-lending-fccpc-tackles-abuses-issues-landmark-regulations/).
- **The savings circle (ajo) runs on trust.** Members pay a fixed sum each week or month, and one member takes the whole pot each round. What a member gets out equals what they paid in; the gain is getting it early. The risk is a collector who disappears. The game's ajo (₦20,000 a month for 10 months) is a working value. Source: [The Conversationalist, 2022](https://conversationalist.org/how-a-traditional-microsavings-system-enabled-nigerian-women-to-save-their-businesses-during-the-pandemic).

## 2. Falling behind without a spiral

Falling behind in a simulation can make recovery impossible, and players quit. Designers soften it: the next blow scales with what the player still has, a loss takes something but not everything, and a rare rescue can arrive. In this game the car's grace, its notice and its hand-back at half paid play that part, and they are also the real rules (section 4). Source: [Game Developer, 2024](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/the-art-of-the-spiral-failure-cascades-in-simulation-games).

## 3. The car as a work tool

- **Home care needs drivers.** In a survey of home care providers published in January 2026, 57% named staff being unable to drive as a significant barrier to recruiting and keeping carers. Source: [Healthcare Management](https://www.healthcare-management.uk/transport-factors-barrier-recruiting-retaining-carers).
- **A foreign licence lasts 12 months.** A new resident of Great Britain with a full licence from a country with no exchange agreement, such as Nigeria, may drive on it for 12 months from becoming resident. After that: a GB provisional licence, the theory test and the practical test. Source: [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/driving-nongb-licence/y/a-resident-of-great-britain/full-car-and-motorcycle/any-other-country).
- **Test fees.** Theory test £23. Practical car test £62 on a weekday, £75 on evenings, weekends and bank holidays. Source: [GOV.UK: driving test costs](https://www.gov.uk/driving-test-cost).
- **Learning costs about £2,200** in all for an average learner who passes first time (May 2026). An estimate built from lesson prices (about £39 an hour) and the suggested 45 hours of lessons, not an official figure. Source: [Pass Rates UK](https://passrates.uk/guide/cost-of-learning-to-drive-2026-breakdown).

## 4. Credit and car finance abroad

### An empty credit file

- **Credit history does not cross borders.** A newcomer to the UK has no UK credit report, or an empty one, and starts from scratch. Canadian credit bureaus track only credit used in Canada, so a history from abroad does not carry over. Sources: [Equifax UK](https://www.equifax.co.uk/resources/loans-and-credit/credit-score-when-you-move-to-the-uk), [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/financial-consumer-agency/services/credit-reports-score/credit-report-score-basics.html).
- **It is built by small payments made on time**: household bills in your own name paid by direct debit, and a card used lightly and cleared each month. Source: Equifax UK, above.

### Car finance in the UK

- **The common dealer plan (PCP)**: a deposit, monthly payments over a fixed term, a mileage limit with a charge for each mile over, and an optional final payment at the end to keep the car. Source: [MoneyHelper](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/everyday-money/buying-and-running-a-car/financing-buying-car-personal-contract-purchase-pcp).
- **A deposit of about 10% and a term of three or four years** are common market terms, not official figures. The game uses 10% and 36 months.
- **Two missed payments bring an arrears notice.** A default notice then gives at least 14 days to put things right. Source: [National Debtline](https://nationaldebtline.org/get-information/guides/car-repossession-what-happens-and-what-you-can-do-about-it/).
- **The third and the half.** Once a third of the total amount payable has been paid, the lender needs a court order to take the car without your consent; under a third, it can take it from a public road without one. Ending the agreement yourself, you owe up to half the total plus arrears; with half or more paid, no arrears and no damage, you may owe nothing more. Source: National Debtline, above.
- **What is left owing stays a debt.** After the agreement ends, the rest is a debt the lender can pursue in the county court. What is passed to a credit reference agency normally stays on the file for six years. Source: National Debtline, above.

### Rates by credit

- **UK used car finance, 2026**: about 9.9% to 14.9% APR for good to excellent credit at mainstream dealers, 15% to 25% for average credit from specialist lenders, and up to about 30% for poor or thin files. One dealer's summary of market rates, not an official average; none was found. Source: [Carsa, 2026](https://www.carsa.co.uk/blog/is-10-9-apr-good-car-finance-2026).
- **US used car loans, Q2 2026** (Experian): 6.29% for super prime credit, 8.81% prime, 13.93% near prime, 19.10% subprime, 21.62% deep subprime. The game's credit ladder follows these tiers. Source: [Bankrate](https://bankrate.com/loans/auto-loans/average-car-loan-interest-rates-by-credit-score).

### UK visa conditions that touch money

- **No recourse to public funds.** Most visas carry a condition that bars most benefits, tax credits and housing help (Universal Credit, Housing Benefit, homelessness assistance and others). Source: [GOV.UK: public funds](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/public-funds--2/public-funds).
- **Health surcharge**: £1,035 a year for most visas, £776 a year for students and their dependants, for the length of the visa. Source: [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/healthcare-immigration-application/how-much-pay).
- **A sponsored worker who stops working** normally has permission cut to 60 days, to find a new sponsor, switch or leave. See `07-status-cliffs-and-restarts.md` section 1.
