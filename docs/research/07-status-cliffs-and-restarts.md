# Losing status abroad

Sources checked October 2026.

Backs `../rules/status.md` and `../rules/route-gates.md`, and the cash jobs in `../rules/ladders.md`. For game design. Not immigration advice.

## 1. Losing the job on a UK sponsored visa

- **Permission is normally cut to 60 days** when someone on a sponsored (points-based) route stops working or studying, so they can apply for something else or leave. The game's sponsored worker gets a clock of 2 months.
- **It is never extended by the cut.** Cutting to 60 days happens only when more than 60 days remain; a visa ending sooner keeps its own date.
- **It can be immediate** when the person was to blame: gross misconduct, being complicit in the sponsor losing its licence, or a sponsor that stopped trading more than 60 days before with no new sponsor found.

Source: [GOV.UK: cancellation and curtailment of permission, caseworker guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/considering-immigration-status-and-deciding-enforcement-action/cancellation-and-curtailment-of-permission-accessible).

## 2. Overstaying and leaving the UK

Most later applications to come back are refused for a fixed period after a breach of immigration law. The period depends on how the person left.

| How the person left | Refused for |
|---|---|
| Overstayed 30 days or less, left by choice at own expense | No ban |
| Overstayed longer, left by choice at own expense | 12 months |
| Left by choice at public expense, within 6 months of being told they were liable to removal | 2 years |
| Left by choice at public expense, later than that | 5 years |
| Removed at public expense | 10 years |
| Used deception in an application | 10 years, from the decision |

The game's removal ban is 120 months (10 years) and its deception ban 120 months.

Source: [Home Office: mandatory refusal periods (re-entry bans), Part Suitability of the Immigration Rules](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6971ff98a1311bdcfa0ed899/Mandatory_refusal_period.pdf).

## 3. Removal from Canada

| Order | Coming back |
|---|---|
| Departure order | Leave within 30 days and confirm the departure, and no permission is needed. Not enforced within 30 days, it becomes a deportation order |
| Exclusion order | Barred for 1 year, or 5 years for misrepresentation |
| Deportation order | Barred for good, unless an Authorization to Return to Canada is granted |

The game models a removal from Canada as a 240-month (20-year) ban, since the real bar has no end date.

Source: [Canada.ca: who needs an Authorization to Return to Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/admissibility-enforcement/authorization-return-canada/who-needs-authorization.html).

Misrepresentation, including hiding an earlier refusal or removal, makes a person inadmissible for five years. Source: [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act, s. 40](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/i-2.5/section-40.html).

## 4. Work without papers in the UK

- **Couriers on a rented account.** Undocumented delivery riders work on someone else's app account and pay its holder a weekly fee, typically £55 to £100 (2023). Working weeks of 80 to 115 hours were common. The game's account rent is £60 a week. Source: [The Conversation, May 2023](https://www.theconversation.com/im-always-delivering-food-while-hungry-how-undocumented-migrants-find-work-as-substitute-couriers-in-the-uk-201695).
- **Pay that does not come.** No current UK figure was found for how often cash workers go unpaid. The game's chance that a month's cash pay is withheld is a working value.
