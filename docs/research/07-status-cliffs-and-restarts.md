# Losing status abroad, and whether a long game needs restarts

> Research, 7 Oct 2026, from government pages, law-firm summaries, press, and game wikis.
> For game realism. Not advice. US rules were changing through 2026 and several are in court. Unverified items are listed at the end.

## Part 1. Status cliffs

### The US work visa

- **A lottery.** 85,000 places a year. Selection odds were about 29% and 35% in the last two published years.
- **Weighted by pay since Feb 2026.** A registration is entered once at the lowest wage level and up to four times at the highest.
- **The employer pays** the filing and legal fees and cannot take them from wages.
- **A $100,000 fee on new petitions from abroad** was proclaimed in Sep 2025, struck down by a court in June 2026, and proclaimed again in Sep 2026. Whether it is being collected is disputed.
- **Three years, extendable to six.** Tied to one employer.

### Losing the job

| Country | The clock | What you can do inside it |
|---|---|---|
| US | 60 days, or until the stay expires if sooner | A new employer files and you can start at once. Switch to dependant, student, or visitor (no work). Self-petition for a green card. Leave, with the employer owing the fare home |
| UK | 60 days from the Home Office notice, with no right to work | A new sponsor, another visa, or leave |
| Canada | The permit stays valid to its end date, but working for anyone else is illegal. After losing status, **90 days** to apply to restore it, with no work | A new permit, restoration, or leave |

The 90-day window is Canada's. The US and UK both use 60.

- In the 2026 tech layoffs, workers built their own job-matching lists. Advisers recommended taking an offer in the first month because paperwork takes three to four weeks. Switching to visitor status to buy time drew extra scrutiny.

### Staying past the clock (US)

- Overstaying voids the visa.
- **More than 180 days, then leaving: barred for 3 years. A year or more: 10 years.** The bar is triggered by leaving.
- **Unauthorised work blocks a later green card from inside the country, except through marriage to a citizen.**
- Voluntary departure avoids the removal bar but not the 3 and 10 year bars.
- 2026: record arrest numbers. The government pays people who leave on their own $2,600 and a flight.

### Work without papers, as reported

- Restaurants account for a quarter of wages found stolen in one state. Owners threatened workers with deportation.
- Home carers paid as little as $4.17 an hour on 24-hour shifts, in cash. One had a passport seized.
- Delivery: couriers rent someone else's account for a weekly fee and work 80 to 115 hours a week (UK reporting).
- Employers face fines per worker, rising for repeat offences.

### Ways to stay (US)

- **Employer green card.** Nigeria is not in the long queue that Indian and Chinese applicants face.
- **Self-petitions** for extraordinary ability or national interest. Approval rates fell sharply by 2025.
- **Marriage.** A marriage under two years old gives a conditional two-year card, and the couple must file together to make it permanent.
- **Someone who got status through marriage cannot sponsor a new spouse for five years**, unless the first marriage is proved genuine.
- **A sponsor signs a financial undertaking** with an income floor of about $24,650 for a household of two. **It lasts until the immigrant becomes a citizen or has worked about ten years. Divorce does not end it.**
- **Who can sponsor whom**: citizens can sponsor spouse, children, parents, and siblings. Permanent residents cannot sponsor parents or siblings. The sibling queue is about 15 years.
- **2026 for Nigerians**: visas issued abroad are largely suspended. Applications from inside the US are possible again after a court ruling.

### Leaving by choice or being removed

| Country | Left by choice | Removed |
|---|---|---|
| US | The 3 or 10 year bar still applies if the overstay was long enough | 5, 10, or 20 years |
| UK | Overstayed 30 days or less and left: no ban. Otherwise 12 months | 10 years |
| Canada | Left within 30 days of a departure order: free to return | Needs permission to return, indefinitely |

Canada asks applicants to declare refusals and removals from any country. Hiding one brings a five-year ban.

## Part 2. Does a long game need restarts?

| Approach | Precedent | What it adds | What it risks |
|---|---|---|---|
| No ageing, no restart | A browser crime game: 21 years, about 80,000 daily players | Nothing is ever lost. Veteran identity | Running out of things to do. A life sim where decades pass and nobody ages |
| Ageing that only changes eligibility | A household life-sim lets players set or switch off ageing | Real time pressure. Long family queues mean something | Punishes slow players unless age is tied to turns spent |
| Retirement and an heir | A medieval dynasty strategy game, a family roguelike | A natural long arc | Forced loss. Wealth piling up across generations. The one shared world that planned it never launched |
| An optional second character who is a relative | In one online role-playing game families share a name and an energy cap. Another made banks, collections, and reputations account-wide in 2024 | Mirrors the theme: settled people sponsor relatives | Extra characters used to feed the first. Feeling compulsory |

**Other findings:**

- **Opt-in resets draw players.** A temporary eight-week mode in one long-running game set its record for players online. Forced wipes suit games built around sessions that end.
- **Shared budgets limit abuse.** One game lets only six characters per account earn its main currency, and feeder accounts still caused inflation.
- **No retention figures were found** for any restart or legacy system.

**What follows**: ageing that only changes eligibility, with an optional relative, unlocked by real sponsorship rules and sharing one turn budget.

## What this means for design

1. **Every sponsored status has a clock when the job goes**: 60 days in the US and UK, 90 in Canada to restore.
2. **Overstaying is a slope.** Short and leaving by choice costs little. Long, or being removed, closes that country for years.
3. **Cash work pays badly and can be withheld**, and in the US it blocks most later routes.
4. **Sponsoring a partner is a long obligation that outlives the marriage.** Someone sponsored cannot sponsor again for five years.
5. **Nothing needs to reset.** Age should count turns and affect only the routes that really use it.
6. **A relative as a second character** is the restart for players who want one.
